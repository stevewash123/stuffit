**Preconditions:** Both accounts exist (Scenario 25) and are clean (empty New Items and My Items). The reader is on the real model (`Ai:Provider=anthropic`), so exact readings can vary: where a step says "expected" and the reader misses it, mark **Partial** and quote what it did read, not Fail. The sample receipts are in `Testing/SampleData/receipts/pdf` (the answer key is `receipts/pdf-as-jpg/answer-key-by-item.csv`). Upload each receipt through New Items.
**Time:** 50 min
**Built per:** S2.4.7 (a protection plan on a receipt is its own record, not an item or part of its name) and S2.4.8 (a warranty the receipt only states becomes a low-confidence "stated on receipt" coverage). Built 2026-11-02 on the `feat-intake` branch. The reader prompt is `document-v22`; cached readings from before are not reused. NOT built, so not planned: reading a plan from a contract upload, several items per receipt each with its own plan, plan companies as shared data or under a parent company (Geek Squad under Best Buy), a plan record on `/plans` (accepting makes an extended-plan **coverage** on the item, not a plan record), and an edit box for each plan field (the plan is kept or unkept with a tick on Add Details).
**Why this plan:** a person who paid for a plan, or whose receipt states a warranty, loses that coverage if the app drops it; and the app must never say something is covered only because a phrase was printed.
