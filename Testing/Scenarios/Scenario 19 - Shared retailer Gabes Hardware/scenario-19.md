# Scenario 19: A retailer the shared data does not have is reported by three people and verified

A regional store, **Gabe's Hardware** (Portland, ME), is not in the shared data. Three different people buy from it. Once three have, an operator looks it up, finds its published return policy, and verifies the store with that policy. Then the question is what happens to the three people's own records, and to the next person.

Needs **three people (three accounts)** plus an **operator**, and a **fourth person** at the end. `python run_shared.py 19` plays all of them on new accounts and prints `PASS` or `FAIL` for each check below.

## Steps and what should happen (S6.1.4)

### Part 1: three people report the same new store

1. **Person 1** brings in files 01 and 02 (New Items, `/add`), and adds the item to My Items.
   - New Items shows the Makita impact driver, order GH-20417, 2026-10-20, $136.10. The store "Gabe's Hardware" is **not recognised** (it is shown as store text).
   - The purchase's return window: **none suggested** (no store policy). Makita warranty and the Chase card benefits are suggested as usual.
   - *Operator view:* one claim, **store "Gabe's Hardware", 1 account, not ready**. Nobody else sees anything.
2. **Person 2** does the same on their own account: the claim shows **2 accounts**.
3. **Person 3** does the same: **3 accounts, ready**. (The same person bringing the order in twice would still count once.)

### Part 2: the operator verifies it

4. The operator runs **evaluate** (`POST /api/admin/claims/evaluate`): the store claim comes back.
5. The operator looks the store up (its site says: *returns within 30 days with a receipt*) and **approves** with the policy (`POST /api/admin/claims/approve`):
   `{ "id": "<claim id>", "returnPolicy": { "days": 30, "fromDelivery": false, "conditions": "With receipt.", "sourceUrl": "<the store's returns page>" } }`
   - The source page is required. Without it, or without a window or a description, nothing is approved.
   - A new shared store "Gabe's Hardware" exists, with a 30-day return policy and the operator's page as official-source evidence. The claim is closed.

### Part 3: what changes for the three people

6. Each of the three people, **without doing anything**:
   - Their purchase now shows **Gabe's Hardware as a shared store** (not "yours"). Their own copy is gone.
   - The item's page suggests the **Gabe's Hardware return policy: 30 days, ends 2026-11-19, verified** (checked against the official source), ticked by default. It was not there before.
   - Anything they had already added (the Makita warranty, the Chase benefits) is unchanged.
7. If a person had **typed their own return policy** for the store (45 days, say), it **stays** and still wins for them. Its line carries a note: *Gabe's Hardware's verified return policy is 30 days; yours says 45 days. Check which applies to you.* A person whose own policy agrees (30 days) sees no note. (Not part of `run_shared.py`; covered by the integration tests.)

### Part 4: a longer name

8. **Person 4** brings in files 03 and 04 ("Gabe's Hardware & Home Center"). The store matcher matches it to the shared store on its first words, so the purchase has **Gabe's Hardware** and the 30-day window (ends **2026-11-21**) straight away.
9. *Operator view:* a new claim, **alias: "Gabe's Hardware & Home Center" for "Gabe's Hardware", 1 account, ready** (an alias needs one account: an operator looks at each).
10. The operator **approves** it (`{ "id": "<claim id>" }`). The longer spelling is now an **alias** of the shared store: from now on that name matches exactly, not by a guess from its first words. The claim is closed.
    - A person who disagrees, or an operator who **rejects** it (`/api/admin/claims/reject` with a reason), leaves the store matching as it was; a rejected claim stays closed.

## Expected coverage for the three people after verification (dates from the purchase date, 2026-10-20)

- Gabe's Hardware return policy: **30 days, ends 2026-11-19, verified**.
- Makita manufacturer warranty: 12 months, ends **2027-10-20**.
- Chase Freedom Unlimited purchase protection: 120 days, ends **2027-02-17**; extended warranty +12 months, so coverage ends **2028-10-20**.
