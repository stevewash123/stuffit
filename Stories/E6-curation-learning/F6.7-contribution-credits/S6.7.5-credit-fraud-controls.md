# S6.7.5 Fraud controls

**Feature:** F6.7 Contribution credits  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** Not planned  
**Depends on:** S6.7.2  

As a **operator**, I want to stop credit farming.

## Acceptance criteria
- Rate limits and caps, content-hash de-duplication, review queue for first-time contributors
- Reversal and account flags for abuse; every grant traceable to a reviewed contribution

## Notes
The main risk of this feature is gaming. Cost to us is a free month per redemption, so caps and review matter more than clever rules.
