# S6.7.2 Earning rules

**Feature:** F6.7 Contribution credits  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** Not planned  
**Depends on:** S6.7.1  

As a **operator**, I want to configure how credits are earned.

## Acceptance criteria
- Credits are granted only when a contribution is Confirmed (S6.6.3)
- Default 1 credit per confirmed contribution; early-contributor bonus (for example 10) for the first N confirmed contributions where we had no policy for that brand and category
- Values are configuration, with optional promotion windows (double credits during a sale)
- Limits: verified email, minimum account age, daily and monthly caps, a duplicate or already-known document earns nothing
- The user is told why each credit was earned
