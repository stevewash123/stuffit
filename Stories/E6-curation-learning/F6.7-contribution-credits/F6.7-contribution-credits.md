# F6.7 Contribution credits

**Epic:** E6 Curation & Learning  
**Phase:** future (idea from Steve, validate before building)

Reward users whose uploads improve the shared data: earn credits, redeem them for free months. Credits are awarded for Confirmed contributions, not for uploads, so the reward cannot be farmed with junk. Ideas: 10 confirmed warranties earn a free month (possibly during a sale), and the first 3 people to contribute something we have nothing for earn 10 credits instead of 1.
