# S6.7.1 Credit ledger

**Feature:** F6.7 Contribution credits  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** Not planned  
**Depends on:** S6.6.3

## Acceptance criteria
- Append-only ledger: user, amount, reason, related contribution, created at; balance is the sum
- Entries can be reversed (a later-found fake reverses its credits) but never edited
- No cash value; stated in the terms
