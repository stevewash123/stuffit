# S6.8.4 Detect a lower price from member receipts

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** v2 (same store); future (other stores)  
**Milestone:** M9  
**Depends on:** S6.2.1 (Log coverage observations), S2.7.2 (Evidence behind a fact), S7.2.2  

As a **user**, I want to know when other members paid less for what I bought, so I can ask for the difference.

## Acceptance criteria
- Match on the product (model or SKU, not the free-text name) and the same store; Stage 2 widens the store match
- A lower price counts only when the observation is **after** the user's purchase date, inside the user's return window, and from a **high-confidence** source (structured email receipt, or a verified OCR extraction); low-confidence OCR prices are ignored
- Normalize before comparing: pre-tax price, quantity, currency, and obvious exclusions (bundle, open-box, refurbished, member-only, coupon-applied) are flagged and excluded or shown as a caveat
- Flag a lower price seen during a known sale period (sale-event calendar is reference data, S6.4.3) as **sale-event pricing**; it is carried to S6.8.5 (exclusions) and S6.8.6 (stronger disclaimer), and may need a higher member count before it triggers an alert
- Needs a minimum number of distinct members at the lower price (S6.8.7); a single receipt never triggers an alert
- Region matters: compare like with like (same country; same state where prices vary)
- The result is stored as an observation of "lower price seen", with the evidence references kept internally for review (S6.4.4)
- Honest wording: it means "paid by other members", never "available now"
