# S6.8.1 Opt in, and the settings that govern alerts

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9  
**Depends on:** S4.4.2 (Notification preference grid), S7.2.2 (Crowd-data terms and consent at signup)  

As a **user**, I want to choose whether I get price-drop alerts and how sensitive they are, so I only hear about drops worth acting on.

## Acceptance criteria
- Off until the user turns it on; the account has a default (on or off) and each purchase can override it
- Requires the crowd-data consent of S7.2.2: a user who has not agreed cannot receive alerts, because receiving them depends on contributing their own prices
- Settings, each with a sensible default and a per-purchase override:
  - Days to Decide (S6.8.2)
  - Minimum savings (S6.8.3)
  - Include other stores (Stage 2; hidden until that stage exists)
- Alerts are a message type in the S4.4.1 catalog and honor the channel grid, quiet hours and unsubscribe
- Opting out stops alerts at once; it does not remove the user's receipts from the shared observations (that is the S7.2.2 consent, separate)
