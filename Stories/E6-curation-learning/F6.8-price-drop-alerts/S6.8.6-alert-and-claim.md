# S6.8.6 The alert, the claim steps, and the outcome

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9  
**Depends on:** S6.8.2 to S6.8.5, S5.4.4 (Prepared message), S5.4.7 (Track a return), S5.4.11 (Mark refunded), S4.5.4 (Actionable notifications)  

As a **user**, I want the alert to take me straight to asking for the money, and to record what happened.

## Acceptance criteria
- Alert text: item, what I paid, the lower price members paid at this store, the saving, the days left and the deadline. No member identities (S6.8.7)
- Short-form copy (lock screen, SMS, push) leads with the money and the time left, and says **potential** savings, never a promised one. Example: "Potential savings: $65 on your Sony earbuds. Members paid less. 5 days left to ask." Never "Save $65" or "Get $60 back"
- Item details are hidden on a locked screen unless the user allows them (a notification preference, S4.4.2)
- The alert opens one **action hub** screen (deep link, S4.5.4) holding: the original receipt (S2.7.2), the store's current price to check, the prepared message or phone script (S5.4.4), and the preflight list of what to bring (S5.4.3)
- **Verify the current price** is the first step on the hub: link to the store's own product page and ask the user to confirm the price before contacting the store. Member receipts are the trigger, not the proof; they are never shown to the user (S6.8.7) and a store will not accept them. The user brings their own proof (the store's page, or a screenshot of it)
- **Sale disclaimer** on the hub and in the full alert (not squeezed into short-form copy): "Sale prices are often temporary and stock may be limited. Confirm the price and that the item is available before you act." Never imply the price will still be there or that a store must honor it
- Stronger wording when the observed price falls in a known sale period (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Prime Day, holiday and back-to-school events, store anniversary sales): "This price may be a short-term sale event. Stores often exclude sale-event prices from price adjustments and sell out of them." The event calendar is reference data (S6.4.3 seasonal windows)
- If the user confirms the price is no longer lower, one tap records "price gone" and ends the alert
- One action then opens the prepared message or phone script for the store (S5.4.4)
- Outcomes the user can record: adjusted (amount), refused, price gone, not worth it, or dismissed; "adjusted" feeds S5.4.11 and the dashboard's total recovered (S9.2.5)
- Refusals feed back to the seller's policy data as an observation (S6.2.1), so a store that keeps refusing stops being alerted
- Honest labelling (S5.4.10): "members paid"; "the store may or may not adjust"; never promises approval
- Dismiss means no more alerts for this purchase unless the lower price falls further
