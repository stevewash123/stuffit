# S6.8.2 Days to Decide

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9  
**Depends on:** S5.4.6 (The real last day), S6.8.1  

As a **user**, I want an alert only when I still have enough days to act, so I am never told about a deal I can no longer use.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Days to Decide** = the number of days that must remain before the deadline for an alert to be worth sending. User setting; default proposed 5 days (to confirm)
- The deadline is the real last day from S5.4.6 (including transit and drop-off time), not the store's nominal window
- Same-store adjustment (Stage 1): needs only time to contact the store. Other-store rebuy (Stage 2) uses the larger of the user's setting and the rebuy lead time in S6.8.11
- When fewer days than the setting remain, no alert is sent; the item page may still show a quiet note ("a lower price was seen; the window has nearly closed")
- The alert states the days left and the exact deadline
- Items with no known return window or deadline get no alerts, and the item page says why
