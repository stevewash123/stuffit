# S6.8.8 Alert limits: no churn nudging

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9  
**Depends on:** S6.8.6  

As a **user**, I want alerts to stay occasional and real, so I do not end up returning things constantly.

## Acceptance criteria
- At most one alert per purchase unless the lower price falls by a further threshold amount
- A cap on alerts per user per week (proposed 3, to confirm); extras roll into the digest (S4.2)
- Stage 2 alerts require the net saving to clear a higher bar than Stage 1 (a returned item costs more of the user's time and attention)
- Track the user's return history; if returns are frequent, stop Stage 2 alerts (retailers flag serial returners) and say why
- No alert on an item the user already returned, refunded or marked used up
