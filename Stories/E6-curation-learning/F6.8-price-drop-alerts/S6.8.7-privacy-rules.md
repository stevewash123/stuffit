# S6.8.7 Privacy rules for aggregate prices

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9  
**Depends on:** S7.2.2 (Crowd-data terms and consent at signup)  

As a **member**, I want my purchases used for other people's alerts only in a way that cannot identify me.

## Acceptance criteria
- Only members who agreed to S7.2.2 contribute prices
- Nothing is shown unless at least a minimum number of **distinct** members paid the lower price (proposed 3, to confirm); the count may be shown, names, timing down to the hour and locations finer than the region are not
- Contributed fields are limited to product, seller, price, region (coarse) and date (coarse, such as the week); no account, address, or card data
- A member's receipts can be withdrawn from the shared observations when they leave or ask (links S1.4)
- Review with counsel before launch (consent wording)
