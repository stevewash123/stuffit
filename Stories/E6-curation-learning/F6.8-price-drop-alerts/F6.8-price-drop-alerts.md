# F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window

**Epic:** E6 Curation & Learning  
**Phase:** same-store v2; cross-store future (validate first)

While a purchase is still returnable, tell the user when other members paid noticeably less. The signal is other members' receipts, not retailer scraping. Two stages:

- **Stage 1, same store (v2):** ask the store for a price adjustment. Cost to the user is near zero.
- **Stage 2, other store (future):** return and rebuy elsewhere. Needs a net-savings calculation and a seller check, and more crowd data than Stage 1.

Related: S6.4.6 (policy-only price-protection reminder; the fallback when there is no crowd signal), S5.4 (claim steps, scripts, deadline), S6.2 (crowd observations).

## Principles
- **Member receipts are the trigger, not the proof.** The user confirms the price on the store's own page and brings that proof. Member data is never shown (S6.8.7)
- **Wording says "potential savings."** The store may refuse or the price may be gone, so no copy promises money back (S5.4.10). Money counted as recovered is only what the user records as received
- **Sale prices are temporary.** Every alert carries a disclaimer that sale prices may end and stock may be limited, and to confirm price and availability first. Known sale periods (Black Friday and similar) get stronger wording and are treated as excluded from adjustment unless the seller's policy says otherwise
- **Channels:** v2 alerts go by email, SMS and in-app (S4.4, S4.5). Push (F4.3) is future; when it exists it is one more channel for the same alert, and the same copy rules apply
