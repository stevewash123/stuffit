# S6.8.10 Is the other seller comparable?

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** Not planned  
**Depends on:** S3.2.2 (Pick a known manufacturer policy), S5.4.9 (Research an unknown seller), S3.3.2  

As a **user**, I want to know whether buying from the cheaper seller costs me protection, before I switch.

## Acceptance criteria
- Compare, side by side: the new seller's return policy, whether it is an authorized seller for the manufacturer (so the manufacturer warranty applies), and whether my card benefit still applies
- Recognize the bad cases: gray-market or import units without a local warranty, marketplace third-party sellers, sellers with no return policy
- Show the plus side too: a fresh return window and coverage start date from the new purchase
- A seller we know nothing about is labelled unknown and the alert is not sent (S5.4.9 can research it on request); never recommend an unvetted seller
- A very large gap at a little-known seller gets extra caution wording, because that is also how scams look
