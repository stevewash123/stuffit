# S6.8.9 Net savings for return-and-rebuy

**Feature:** F6.8 Price-drop alerts during the return window  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** Not planned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.5 (Which route first), S5.4.1 (How-to card), S6.8.4  

As a **user**, I want the real saving from returning here and buying there, not just the price difference.

## Acceptance criteria
- Net saving = price paid here, minus price there, minus restocking fee, minus return shipping or drop-off cost, minus shipping and tax on the new purchase, minus anything lost (a card benefit, bundled accessories)
- Return terms come from the original seller's policy data; the new seller's price, shipping and tax are estimates and labelled as such
- Shown as one number with the parts listed, and the assumptions the user can change
- Only the net figure is compared with the minimum-savings threshold (S6.8.3)
- Shown as another route in the S5.4.5 comparison, next to repair and refund
- A price seen from members is an observation, not a live offer: the user is told to confirm the current price **and that the item is in stock** before buying
- Same sale disclaimer as S6.8.6, stronger in known sale periods: the deal may be temporary, limited stock, or gone by the time the user orders
