# S6.1.3 Sharing is the default for policies and official documents

**Feature:** F6.1 Candidate policy queue  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S7.2.2  

As the **operator of the service**, I want what people upload to help confirm shared data, and I want the licence to say so.

## Acceptance criteria
- Everything uploaded is treated as shared, always. There is no Share checkbox and no opt-out; the terms and the consent screen say so (decided 2026-10-26, and may be written into the licence)
- What is shared is only the extracted facts: store and brand names, aliases, return windows, warranty terms. Never the document or file, and never personal data: names, addresses, order numbers, prices paid, card numbers, serials, account details
- The facts are recorded as claims (S6.1.4) and are seen by no other user until an operator verifies them
- Deleting an account removes its claim rows (counts drop with them); the shared data an operator already verified stays
- The consent text lists exactly what is extracted and what is never shared

## Replaces
The earlier Share checkbox (default on), the account setting Contribute to shared data, and per-submission withdraw.
