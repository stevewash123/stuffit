# S6.1.5 Show how trustworthy shared data is

**Feature:** F6.1 Candidate policy queue  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M7  
**Depends on:** S6.1.4  

As a **user**, I want to see whether a policy is verified, reported by other users, or only mine.

## Acceptance criteria
- Labels: Verified (checked against the official source, with the date), Reported by N users (not yet verified), Added by you
- A single unverified submission is not shown to other users and never used to suggest coverage for them
- Community-reported policies are suggested with a clear warning; Verified ones are suggested normally
- The same labels appear on the store and brand pages and on suggestions during a purchase

## See also
Labels are bands of the confidence value defined in S3.2.5 (Verified, Likely, Unconfirmed); a document-backed report counts for more than a typed one.
