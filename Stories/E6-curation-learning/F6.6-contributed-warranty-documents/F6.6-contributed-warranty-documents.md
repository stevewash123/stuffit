# F6.6 Contributed warranty documents

**Epic:** E6 Curation & Learning  
**Phase:** v2 (curation)

Users upload the official warranty or return terms (PDF or screenshot), especially when we have none for that item. The document backs the user's own coverage right away and, after verification, can become the evidence for shared data. Statuses: Submitted > Confirmed > Superseded (or Rejected). A user upload is never Confirmed on its own; Confirmed means checked against the official source.
