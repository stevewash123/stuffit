# S6.6.3 Verify submitted documents

**Feature:** F6.6 Contributed warranty documents  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M7  
**Depends on:** S6.6.1; S6.1.2; S7.1.2  

As a **operator**, I want to check a submitted document against the official source and confirm it.

## Acceptance criteria
- AI reads the document (term, start rule, conditions) and looks for the official page; shows both side by side
- Outcomes: Confirmed, Rejected (with reason), or Needs another look
- Confirmed documents become the source of a versioned shared policy; our stored copy and the original link are both kept
- Duplicate files (same content hash) are linked, not stored twice for review
- Personal information (names, order numbers, account details) is checked for and cropped or rejected before anything is shared
