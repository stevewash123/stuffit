# S6.6.2 Ask for the document when we have none

**Feature:** F6.6 Contributed warranty documents  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M7  
**Depends on:** S6.6.1; S3.1.2  

As a **user**, I want to be asked to upload the warranty card when nothing is known for my item.

## Acceptance criteria
- When an item has no manufacturer policy, the suggestions area offers: Do you have the warranty card or terms? Upload it
- Never blocks saving the purchase; dismissible per item
- Camera capture on phones for a photo of the card or leaflet
