# S6.9.5 Promote a group to a model-level policy

**Feature:** F6.9 Model-level warranty terms from owners' documents  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S6.9.4; S6.1.2 (official-source check); S3.2.5 (evidence)  

As an **operator**, I want to turn a clear group into a model-level warranty, so owners of those models get the right term proposed.

## Acceptance criteria
- Offered only when the group meets the threshold (S3.2.5 band Likely or better; default at least 3 independent accounts, configuration) or the operator has verified it against the brand's own source
- Creates or updates a manufacturer policy limited to the group's node, listed model numbers or purchase window; the general policy is untouched
- The operator reviews and confirms term, start rule and components first; the confirm step is the same one used for store and brand pages
- Each contributing observation becomes an evidence record on the new policy (S3.2.5); the band and reason are computed, not typed
- Nothing is published automatically at any count
- The previous version of the policy keeps its dates (versioned); coverage already saved keeps its snapshot
- Existing owners with a matching item are offered a confirm-or-keep notice (S6.3.3)
