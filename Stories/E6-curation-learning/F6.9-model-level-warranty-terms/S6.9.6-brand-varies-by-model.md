# S6.9.6 Mark a company's terms as depending on a model or a lookup

**Feature:** F6.9 Model-level warranty terms from owners' documents  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1  

As an **operator**, I want to mark a brand or store whose terms vary by model, or can only be found by entering data on its site, so the general term is not presented as the answer and the owner knows what to bring.

## Acceptance criteria
- A flag on the company with a reason: **varies by model**, **needs a lookup**, or both
- For a lookup: what the lookup asks for (any of serial number, reference number, model number, order number, purchase email) and the address of the lookup page. We never store the values an owner types there
- Applies to brands (warranty) and to stores (returns, store-sold protection plans, order-based warranty lookups)
- The company's general policy is read as the shortest term that applies across its range, with the conditions saying so
- A coverage proposed from that general term shows "varies by model" and/or "check by lookup", names what the lookup needs, and links to it; the owner is asked to check the label, the paperwork or the lookup and enter what it says
- The owner's own entered or uploaded term replaces the proposal for their item (S6.9.7)
- The AI reading of saved pages (the "different terms" warning) can suggest the flag; an operator sets it, and the note on the company records why
- On the admin cards, flagged companies are visible so they can be found; the "varies by model" part clears once model-level policies cover the range (S6.9.5), and the lookup part stays
