# S6.9.4 Operator view of the groups

**Feature:** F6.9 Model-level warranty terms from owners' documents  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S6.9.3; S7.1.1 (operator admin)  

As an **operator**, I want to see, on a brand's admin page, what owners' documents say by model line, so I can decide what to turn into a policy. Read-only first: this is the story to build and look at real data before anything is promoted.

## Acceptance criteria
- On the brand page: groups listed by distinct accounts, each showing node, term, start rule, accounts, agreement, band and date range
- Groups that disagree are marked and listed together (S6.9.8)
- The "not in the tree" bucket shows the model numbers seen, with an action to add a node or prefix for them (existing tree editor)
- The card colour or a count on the brand list shows when groups are waiting, the same way saved docs do
- A group opens to its quotes and the linked files for review; personal details are never shown (S6.9.2)
- Visible to operators only
