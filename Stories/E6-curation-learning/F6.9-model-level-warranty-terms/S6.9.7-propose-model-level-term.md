# S6.9.7 Propose the model-level term for an owner's item

**Feature:** F6.9 Model-level warranty terms from owners' documents  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S6.9.5; S6.9.6; S3.2.8  

As a **user**, I want my item's warranty proposed from its own model line when we know it, and my own document to win over anything shared.

## Acceptance criteria
- Resolution order for a coverage proposal: the owner's own uploaded document, then a manufacturer policy for the item's node or model number (exact, then prefix, then name, as the matcher already does), then the brand's general policy
- The proposal shows its band and reason, for example "Reported by 5 owners who attached their documents" or "Read from the brand's site on Oct 3" (S3.2.5)
- A brand flagged "varies by model" with no match shows the general floor with the check-your-paperwork wording (S6.9.6)
- An owner who changes the term keeps their value as their own; that is logged as a typed observation if they choose to share it (S6.9.2)
- A saved coverage keeps the term and band it had when accepted; later changes ask affected owners (S6.3.3)
