# S6.9.8 Conflicting terms open a review

**Feature:** F6.9 Model-level warranty terms from owners' documents  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S6.9.3; S6.2.3 (disagreement and split flag); S6.1.4  

As an **operator**, I want to be told when documents for the same model line disagree, so I find out why instead of averaging them.

## Acceptance criteria
- Two different terms in one group, each with at least 2 independent accounts, open a review; nothing is averaged and the higher term is not assumed
- The review lists the possible reasons to check: a finer model split not yet in the tree, a purchase-date change, a registration bonus or extended term, a region, or a retailer-specific edition
- Outcomes: split the group (add a node or prefix, or a purchase window), correct a stale policy, or dismiss with a reason; the choice is recorded on the brand's notes
- Until resolved, proposals for that line show the lower-risk term with "may differ" and are not marked Verified
- A review reopens if later observations disagree again
