# S6.3.2 AI checks the change against the official source

**Feature:** F6.3 Automated policy-change detection & user confirmation  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M7  
**Depends on:** S6.3.1 (Scheduled job detects crowd-reported changes); S6.1.2 (AI verifies against the official source)  

As a **operator**, I want to have the AI compare crowd reports with the issuer/manufacturer page.

## Acceptance criteria
- Fetches the official benefits page or guide and extracts the current term and effective date
- Outcomes: confirmed, contradicted, or unverifiable (page missing or ambiguous)
- Confirmed: proposes a new versioned policy row with source_url and best-known effective_from
- Contradicted or unverifiable: routed to human review, wording never asserts the change as fact
- Full audit trail of the evidence used
