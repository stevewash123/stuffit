# S6.3.4 Apply a confirmed change without rewriting history

**Feature:** F6.3 Automated policy-change detection & user confirmation  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M7  
**Depends on:** S6.3.2 (AI checks the change against the official source); S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables)  

As a **system**, I want to roll a verified change forward safely.

## Acceptance criteria
- Closes the old policy version, opens the new one at the effective date
- Existing coverages keep their snapshotted terms (benefit applies as of purchase date)
- New purchases after the effective date propose the new terms
- User-visible "what changed" note on affected cards
- Change is revertible if later contradicted
