# F6.3 Automated policy-change detection & user confirmation

**Epic:** E6 Curation & Learning  
**Phase:** v2 add-on

A scheduled AI review notices when many users report the same change to a policy (e.g. 10 accounts edit their Citi card benefit the same way), checks it against the official source, and asks affected users to confirm their own policy.
