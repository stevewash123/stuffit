# F4.6 General reminders (to-dos)

**Epic:** E4 Reminders & Notifications
**Phase:** MVP candidate (the base); the document-review feed is v2

Coverage reminders (F4.1) say "this return window or warranty ends soon". Some things the user should do have nothing to do with a coverage end: register the warranty (sometimes by a date), mail in a rebate, keep the box, check a condition. A general reminder is one small, flexible record that can carry any of these, made by the user, the system, or a document review that reads what came with a purchase.

## Principles
- **One generic base.** Title, text, optional date, status. Specific kinds add properties later in a details field, and a property becomes a real column only when it earns it.
- **A found task is a suggestion.** Anything the system or AI review finds starts as a suggestion with its source quote and confidence; the user accepts it before it nags (same rule as suggested coverage).
- **The user is in control of the nag.** Done, Dismiss for now, and Not applicable are one click.
- **Reuse the existing delivery.** Due dates go out through the reminder job, the digest and the notification center, not a second system.
