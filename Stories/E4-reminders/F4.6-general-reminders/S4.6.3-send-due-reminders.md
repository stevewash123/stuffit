# S4.6.3 Send due reminders

**Feature:** F4.6 General reminders (to-dos)
**Phase:** v2
**Milestone:** unassigned
**Depends on:** S4.6.1; S4.1.2 (daily job); S4.4.1 (message type catalog); S4.5.1 (notification record)

As a **user**, I want a nudge when a reminder with a date gets close, through my usual channels.

## Acceptance criteria
- A new message type, "Things to do", in the catalog (S4.4.1) with its own row in the preference grid; default on, in-app and email.
- The daily job sends one message for each open reminder when its remindFrom date arrives, and again 1 day before dueOn. Snoozed ones wait until their date. Done and dismissed ones never send.
- Several reminders due the same day join one message (S4.2.4).
- Each message links to the item and has the one-click actions where the channel allows (S4.5.4).
- Reminders without a date never send; they only sit on the list.

## Added 2026-10-03 (owner request)
- **Setup steps nudge too, not only dated reminders.** A card used on a purchase whose benefits were never confirmed is a thing to do: its extended warranty and purchase protection are silently not being added. A message goes to the account's people a day after the card's first use, then weekly, three times at most, and stops once the benefits are confirmed. It is its own message type ("Card benefits to confirm", Policies group, links to the card) so it has its own preference row.
- The same pattern covers other "complete this registration" steps as they are found (a product or card registration with a deadline is already a to-do of kind RegisterWarranty).

## Status

Built 2026-10-03, in the hourly reminder job (`ReminderSendJob.Todos.cs`), no migration: the notification record is the memory (one per to-do and nudge kind, so a rerun sends nothing twice). Dated to-dos nudge when their start date arrives (or 3 days before the due date if none is set) and again the day before they are due; if both are already past, only the later goes out. Snoozed ones wait for their date; undated, done, dismissed, suggested and long-overdue ones never send. Item to-dos go to the people on the item; others to the person who made them, else everyone on the account. Each person is told at their own local time. Two new catalog types: `things_to_do` and `card_benefits_to_confirm`, with email templates. Seven integration tests in `ReminderNudgeTests.cs`.
Joined 2026-10-06 (in the job, no migration, does not wait for S4.2.4): nudges ready in the same run for one person become one message ("N things to do", link to /todos), a single one keeps its own wording and link. A batch records its nudges in its payload (`todo-batch`, "type:todoId;...") so a rerun repeats nothing. Integration test `Several_nudges_ready_together_join...`; reminder suites 41 pass.
Not built: the one-click actions in the message (S4.5.4); the card nudge for a card never used (by design it waits for a first purchase).
