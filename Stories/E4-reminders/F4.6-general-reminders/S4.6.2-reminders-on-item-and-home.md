# S4.6.2 Reminders on the item page and home

**Feature:** F4.6 General reminders (to-dos)
**Phase:** MVP candidate
**Milestone:** M6 (proposed)
**Depends on:** S4.6.1; S9.2.3 (item detail page)

As a **user**, I want to see and act on my open reminders where I look at my things.

## Acceptance criteria
- The item page shows a **To do** card when the item has open reminders: title, due date with days left, and **Mark as done**, **Dismiss for now** (snooze), and a menu for **Not applicable**. In edit mode the user can also add and edit one.
- Suggested reminders show in the same card as a suggestion with the quote or page it came from, **Add** and **Not applicable** (same pattern as suggested coverage).
- The home page shows a count of open reminders that are due soon, linking to a list of all of them.
- Overdue and due-soon dates use the same wording and colours as coverage.
- Empty state: nothing is shown unless there is something to do.

## Status

Built 2026-10-03 (UI plus one API field), not yet browser-checked. The item page has a **To do** card (open and suggested reminders; Mark as done, Dismiss for now = snooze a week, Not applicable; suggested ones show where they were found, with Add). In edit mode the card always shows and a reminder can be added (title and optional due date). Home has a **To do** tile counting open reminders overdue or due within 30 days, with the suggested count, linking to `/todos`, which lists every reminder with its item. Due dates use coverage's wording and colours (`dueStatus`). `GET /api/todos` now returns `itemName`. Tests: `todo-list.spec.ts`, `todo-card.spec.ts`, `todos.page.spec.ts`, home spec, `format.items.spec.ts`, `TodoTests`.
Not done: editing an existing reminder, and a nav link to `/todos` (reached from the Home tile only).
