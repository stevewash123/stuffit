# S4.5.8 Retention and cleanup

**Feature:** F4.5 Notification Center (in-app)  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S4.5.1 (Notification record and API); S7.6.2 (Background job scheduler)  

As a **operator**, I want to keep the notifications table from growing forever.

## Acceptance criteria
- Read notifications older than a set period are purged
- Unread, important and pending-action notifications are kept longer
- Retention values are configuration
