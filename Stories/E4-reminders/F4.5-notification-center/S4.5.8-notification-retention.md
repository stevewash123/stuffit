# S4.5.8 Retention and cleanup

**Feature:** F4.5 Notification Center (in-app)  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S4.5.1 (Notification record and API); S7.6.2 (Background job scheduler)  

As a **operator**, I want to keep the notifications table from growing forever.

## Acceptance criteria
- Read notifications older than a set period are purged
- Unread, important and pending-action notifications are kept longer
- Retention values are configuration


## Status

2026-11-06 (feat-weekly, API, not merged): Done. The nightly `receipt-retention` job (03:00 UTC) also purges notifications (`NotificationRetention`) and reports the count (`NotificationsPurged` in its summary; same `POST /api/maintenance/run-retention`). Config `Notifications:Retention:ReadDays` (default 90: read, or deleted by the person, counted from when read/deleted) and `Notifications:Retention:KeptDays` (default 365: unread or important, counted from creation), documented in the API README. Never purged: Pending action, email Held for a digest, a required system alert the person has not deleted (one they deleted goes on the long clock). Digest notifications (daily and weekly) are kept at least 30 days whatever the settings say (`MinDigestDays`): a digest's id is the proof a day/week was sent, the widest window it guards is 7 days, held notices live at most 14, so 30 days leaves a wide margin. No migration. Tests: `NotificationRetentionTests`.
