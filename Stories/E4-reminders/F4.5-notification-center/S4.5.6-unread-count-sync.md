# S4.5.6 Keep the unread count current

**Feature:** F4.5 Notification Center (in-app)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S4.5.1 (Notification record and API)  

As a **system**, I want to show an accurate count without a page refresh.

## Acceptance criteria
- Unread count loaded on sign-in and refreshed on window focus and on a modest timer
- Marking read or unread updates the badge immediately, optimistic with rollback on failure
- Bell and Notification Center share one count source

## Status

Done (M4): `UnreadService`: loaded on sign-in, refreshed on window focus and every 60 s; marking read/unread moves the badge at once and rolls back on failure; shared by the bell and the Notification Center. Tests: `unread.service.spec.ts`.
