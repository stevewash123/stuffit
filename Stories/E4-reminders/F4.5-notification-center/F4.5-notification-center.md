# F4.5 Notification Center (in-app)

**Epic:** E4 Reminders & Notifications  
**Phase:** MVP (central to the product)

The in-app home for everything the system tells the user: a bell in the header, a full Notification Center page, and deep links into the item or task. Most of the app prompts (needs info, confirm a policy change, return window closing) land here first. Modeled on the ConsignmentGenie bell, center, notification service and Notification entity (see docs/consignmentgenie-lessons.md).
