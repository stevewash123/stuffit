# S4.5.2 Header bell and dropdown

**Feature:** F4.5 Notification Center (in-app)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S4.5.1 (Notification record and API); S4.5.6 (Keep the unread count current); S9.1.1 (Responsive, mobile-first layout)  

As a **user**, I want to see at a glance whether something needs me.

## Acceptance criteria
- Bell in the header with an unread count badge
- Dropdown of the 5 most recent notifications with icon, title, message and time ago
- Click a notification: marks it read, closes the dropdown, opens the Notification Center with that notification focused
- Mark all read action; View all link
- Click outside or press Escape closes the dropdown; keyboard accessible
- Badge and list stay current (S4.5.6)

## Status

Done (M4): the header bell with an unread badge (99+ cap), a dropdown of the 5 newest, open-and-mark-read into the Notification Center with that card highlighted, Mark all read, View all, Escape and outside click close. Tests: `notification-bell.spec.ts`.
