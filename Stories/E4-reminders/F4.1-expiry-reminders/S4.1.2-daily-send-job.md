# S4.1.2 Daily job sends due reminders

**Feature:** F4.1 Expiry reminders  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S4.1.1 (Generate reminders from coverage dates); S4.4.3 (Dispatcher: store preferences and enforce them on every send); S7.6.4 (Transactional and inbound email)  

As a **system**, I want to send reminders exactly once.

## Acceptance criteria
- Idempotent: sent_at recorded, safe to rerun
- Failures retried and logged
- Email first via transactional provider

## Status

Done (M4, API): `ReminderSendJob` (Infrastructure/Reminders), scheduled daily at 13:00 UTC as `send-due-reminders` with 3 retries (1, 5, 15 min). Also runnable now with `--send-reminders`. Due pending reminders become notifications via `INotificationService` for every user on the account. Exactly once: the notification carries the reminder id, so a rerun after a crash only marks the reminder sent. If several notices for one coverage are due (job was down) only the closest goes out and the rest are cancelled. Ended coverage, returned or deleted item: cancelled, not announced. An email outage does not hold a reminder back: the notification exists with email status failed and the hourly retry job resends. Wording lives in `ReminderText`. Tests: `ReminderSendTests.cs`.

Open: delivery is by UTC date (no per-user timezone yet); the check-later reminder (see S4.1.1).

Update: the job now runs HOURLY and reaches each user at their own local time (profile time zone + preferred time, 9:00 default; `UserClock`). Days left count from the user date. Profile: `PUT /api/me/profile` {timezone (IANA name), reminderTime}; signup drops an unrecognized zone. The check-later follow-up is built in the same job (below, S4.1.1).

Update (S1.3.3): recipients are the people on the item (owner and followers), not every user in the account.
