# S4.1.1 Generate reminders from coverage dates

**Feature:** F4.1 Expiry reminders  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S3.1.1 (Add or edit a coverage (snapshot of terms)); S7.6.2 (Background job scheduler)  

As a **system**, I want to create reminders whenever a coverage is saved or changed.

## Acceptance criteria
- Defaults 60/30/7 days, return windows get their own shorter cadence
- Editing a coverage regenerates its pending reminders
- Per-user quiet preferences respected

## Status

Done (M4, API): `ReminderPlanner` (Core, pure rules) and `ReminderService` (Infrastructure). Expiry coverage gets 60/30/7 days, return windows and card return/price protection get 7/3/1. Reminders are created on coverage save, regenerated on edit (an end-date change re-arms even a sent notice; an unrelated edit never re-sends), cancelled when the end date is removed or the item is returned or disposed, and restarted when it is owned again. Dates already behind us are skipped. `--backfill-reminders` CLI covers coverages saved before this existed. Notification type per coverage: `ReminderPlanner.NotificationType`.

Not done here: per-user quiet preferences are the dispatcher/S4.4.6 (M8), applied at send time; the "not sure yet" check-later reminder needs its own date on the dismissal (reminders are keyed to a coverage) and is open. S4.1.2 must read reminders with IgnoreQueryFilters (no signed-in tenant in a job) and skip a coverage that has already ended.

Update: the check-later reminder is done. "Not sure yet" sets `check_on` = today + 30 days on the dismissal; the hourly job asks again once (type `coverage_check_later`), unless the item is gone or the coverage has since been added. Pre-existing dismissals are covered by `--backfill-reminders`.

Update (S1.3.3): the check-later follow-up also goes to the people on the item.
