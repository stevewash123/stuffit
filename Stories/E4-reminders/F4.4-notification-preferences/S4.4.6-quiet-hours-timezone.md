# S4.4.6 Quiet hours and timezone

**Feature:** F4.4 Notification preferences (message type x channel)  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S4.4.3 (Dispatcher: store preferences and enforce them on every send)  

As a **user**, I want to not be notified at bad times.

## Acceptance criteria
- Timezone on the user profile
- Quiet hours delay SMS and push, not email or in-app
- Urgent return-window alerts may override quiet hours only if the user allows it
