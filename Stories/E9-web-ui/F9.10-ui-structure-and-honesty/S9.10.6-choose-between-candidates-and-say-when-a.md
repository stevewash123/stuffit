# S9.10.6 Choose between candidates, and say when a match is wrong

**Feature:** F9.10 UI structure and honesty  
**Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S2.8.4; S2.8.5

As a **person**, I want to see the likely stores or brands, pick one, and tell the app when it got one wrong.

## Acceptance criteria
- A shortlist shows two or three candidates with the certainty word and reason; the top one is the main action (F2).
- "This is the wrong store/brand" and a confirmed pick call the match-feedback endpoint; per-item dismissals stay as they are.
- Brand match on add purchase uses the shortlist instead of silently filling a maker from a three-letter name.
- After a correction, the same printed text on a later receipt matches the corrected party (an end-to-end check with the API).
