# S9.10.2 The bell says when it could not load

**Feature:** F9.10 UI structure and honesty  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Depends on:** none

As a **person**, I want a failed notification load not to look like an empty inbox.

## Acceptance criteria
- If the list fails, the bell says it could not load and offers Try again, not "You are all caught up" (F10).
- Setup-notice refresh failures keep the last good list and retry on the next poll instead of being swallowed.
- Specs cover the failure and the retry.

## Status
Built (Push 65). The bell says "Could not load your notifications" with Try again; spec covers the failure and the retry. Not done: the setup-notice refresh still swallows its errors silently (it keeps the last good list, which is the behavior wanted, but there is no spec).
