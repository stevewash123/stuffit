# S9.1.7 Welcome: bring in the purchases I already have

**Feature:** F9.1 App shell & navigation  
**Phase:** MVP (the mailbox-link half needs S2.2.10; forwarding needs S2.2.1 live)  
**Depends on:** S2.2.10 (link a mailbox), S2.2.1 (private inbound address), S2.2.9 (guided forwarding setup), S2.2.5 (pending items), S9.1.2 (first-run checklist)  

As a **new user** with a mailbox full of order confirmations, I want to be walked from "I just signed up" to "my purchases are in WarrantyCat", so I never face an empty app wondering what to do.

## Decision: two doors, one recommended
1. **Link my mailbox (recommended, shown first).** Gmail and iCloud first. The user gives an app password (S2.2.10), we read only order mail, bring in the last 12 months, and keep checking. Least work for the user, no filters, no batches.
2. **Forward my order emails (for people who would rather not give out a login).** Private address, a rule for new mail, and a way to send what is already there. Gmail and Apple Mail first; any other mail app gets general advice only.

The page says plainly why the first is easier and what we can and cannot do with it (read order mail only; never send, move or delete; unlink any time), and that forwarding shares nothing but the emails the user chooses.

POP3 is not offered: it can only download everything, cannot search by sender or subject, and so would pull private mail we then have to discard. IMAP only (S2.2.10).

## The journey
1. Sign up, confirm the email address. First sign-in lands on **/welcome**, not an empty Home.
2. Welcome explains in two lines what happens and what we keep: *we read order and receipt emails, keep the ones that are purchases, and throw the rest away.*
3. The recommended card: **Connect my mailbox.**
4. Or **forward**: the private address with a copy button; the user picks Gmail, Apple Mail or Other and sees only those steps, in two jobs:
   - **From now on:** one filter or rule that forwards store mail. Not "forward everything"; the page says why.
   - **What I already have:** see "Bringing in the backlog" below.
5. **Live feedback.** The page watches for mail arriving: "Waiting for your first email…", then "It works, 3 new purchases", with a button to review them (New items).
6. Optional, skippable: **Add the cards you pay with**, and PayPal if used (S3.3, PayPal funding card).
7. Other doors, small: upload a receipt, PDF or photo; add one by hand.
8. Done when the first purchases are in; the Home checklist takes over.

## Bringing in the backlog by forwarding
- **Gmail, easiest first:** make the forwarding filter, and tick "Also apply filter to matching conversations". If Gmail forwards the old mail too, each order arrives as its own email. *To verify with a real Gmail account: it is believed that Gmail applies that tick to labels and similar actions but not to forwarding.*
- **Otherwise, forward as attachment:** search for order mail, select up to **20**, ⋮ More → Forward as attachment, repeat. Apple Mail: select up to 20, Message → Forward As Attachment.
- **At most 20 orders are read from one message.** Extra attached emails are not read and the user is told how many were left out so they can send them again. (Built: each attached email becomes its own New Item; see S2.2.1.)

## Acceptance criteria
- A first sign-in with no purchases goes to /welcome once; "Skip for now" is always there and remembered. The page is reachable later from Home's checklist and Account ("Bring in purchases").
- The mailbox-link card comes first and is labelled recommended. It is live (S2.2.10), with forwarding shown right below it.
- The private address shows with a copy button. If no inbound domain is set up, the page says forwarding is not on yet and puts uploading first.
- Provider tabs: Gmail, Apple Mail / iCloud, Other. Each has the steps for new mail and for the backlog, and the search to copy where the app has one.
- Privacy advice: forward only order mail; forward-everything is discouraged. Mail that is not an order is discarded after reading (S2.2.2).
- Arrival feedback uses the pending-items count and refreshes while the page is open; it only claims what it knows.
- Works on a phone; every step can be skipped; nothing here blocks the app.
- The forwarding guide is one component, reused by the Settings > Email page (S2.2.9).
- A **welcome email** is sent once, after the address is confirmed: what to do, and a link to /welcome.

## Not in scope
- Revoking or replacing the address (S2.2.9; deferred).
- Outlook and Yahoo guides (later; "Other" advice covers them).
- Importing an Amazon export (F2.3).

## Open questions
- Does Gmail's "apply filter to existing mail" forward old messages? Test with a real account; if yes, it replaces the attachment route for Gmail.
- If someone has 800 old orders, the review queue needs grouping and a "dismiss all like this" path (S2.2.7).
- Sender check for auto-forwarded mail: same open question as S2.2.9.

## Status
Page built: the recommended mailbox card now links a Gmail or iCloud mailbox (S2.2.10), forwarding guide for Gmail, Apple Mail and Other, live arrival count, cards and upload hand-offs, first-run redirect, Account link. Forwarded-as-attachment bundles are unpacked into separate New Items, capped at 20 per message. Not built: the welcome email, Gmail forwarding-confirmation capture, "Add to contacts" file.
