# S9.1.6 Account menu: cards, plans, insurance and settings hang off Account

**Feature:** F9.1 App shell  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S9.1.1 (Responsive layout for phone and desktop)  

As a **user**, I want what belongs to me rather than to one item (my cards, my warranty plans, my insurance, my settings) kept together under Account, so the main navigation is only what I look at every day.

## Acceptance criteria
- The main navigation is Home, My Items, New Items and Notifications. Cards and Settings are no longer tabs.
- The header shows a round avatar with the user's initials (first and last name, or the first letter of the email). Resting the mouse on it, or clicking or tapping it, opens a menu: name and email, Account, then the account sections, then the admin link for an operator, then Sign out. Escape or a second click closes it.
- The account sections today are Credit cards and Settings. Extended warranties and Insurance are added to the same list when their pages exist (S3.4.2, S3.4.1).
- On a phone, where there is no hover, the bottom bar has an Account tab (Home, My Items, New Items, Account) that opens an Account page listing the same sections with a line of explanation each, and Sign out.
- The menu and the Account page read one list, so they cannot drift apart.
