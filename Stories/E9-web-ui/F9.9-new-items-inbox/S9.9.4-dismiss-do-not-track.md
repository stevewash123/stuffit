# S9.9.4 Dismiss: do not track

**Feature:** F9.9 New Items inbox  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S9.9.1 (New Items list); S2.2.5 (Pending items and their API)  

As a **user**, I want to dismiss things I do not want tracked, like a grocery receipt, without it nagging me again.

## Acceptance criteria
- "Dismiss" has a tooltip: WarrantyCat will not track this item; good for things you do not need to bother with, such as a grocery receipt
- Dismissed items leave New Items and never become purchases, items or reminders
- A brief "Dismissed. Undo" message allows reversal; afterwards a "Dismissed" view under New Items lists them with Restore
- The same email forwarded again, or the same order number, does not come back (dedupe still sees it, S2.2.4)
- Dismissed pending items and their stored email or file follow the retention setting (S2.6.1) and account deletion (S1.4.2)
- Optional later: "Stop suggesting items from this store" (S2.2.7)

## Status
Done in part (2026-10-02): calls `POST /api/pending-items/{id}/dismiss`, removes the row and offers Undo (`/restore`). Dismissed view done (2026-10-03, UI only; the API already had `?status=dismissed` and `/restore`): a "Waiting / Dismissed" switch on New Items; Dismissed lists each item with store, date, the day it was dismissed and a Restore button, and says "Nothing dismissed" when empty. Restore returns it to the waiting list with a note; a refusal shows the reason and keeps the row. 5 UI specs. Not done: "stop suggesting this store" (S2.2.7).

Update 2026-10-19: Dismissing an item at or over the minimum that still needs verification asks first, in multi-select too (Select all skips those and says how many). The rules (colours, the two values of a line, per-person minimum, dismiss confirmation) are in S9.2.11.
