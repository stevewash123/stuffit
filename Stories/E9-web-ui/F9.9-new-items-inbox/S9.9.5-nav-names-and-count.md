# S9.9.5 Tab names and new-item count

**Feature:** F9.9 New Items inbox  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S9.9.1 (New Items list); S2.2.5 (Pending items and their API)  

As a **user**, I want clear tab names and to see when something is waiting for me.

## Acceptance criteria
- Primary nav: Home, **My Items**, **New Items**, Cards, Notifications, Settings (replaces "Items" and "Add")
- New Items shows a count badge of pending items; hidden at zero
- The count comes from a cheap API call and updates after accept or dismiss
- A burst of arrivals (several forwarded receipts) raises one notification, not many (S4.2.4)
- "Add a purchase" buttons elsewhere go straight to the blank form at `/add/new`

## Status
Done (2026-10-02): the count badge shows on the New Items tab, desktop and phone, from `GET /api/pending-items/count`; refreshed on open, on focus, every minute, and by the page after it changes something.

Update 2026-10-19: The New Items badge counts Error-level items only, so a long receipt does not read as a dozen jobs. The rules (colours, the two values of a line, per-person minimum, dismiss confirmation) are in S9.2.11.
