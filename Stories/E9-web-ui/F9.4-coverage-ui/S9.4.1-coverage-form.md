# S9.4.1 Add or edit a coverage

**Feature:** F9.4 Coverage add / edit / delete  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M3  
**Depends on:** S3.1.1 (Add or edit a coverage (snapshot of terms)); S3.2.2 (Pick a known manufacturer policy or add a new one)

## Acceptance criteria
- Choose kind: return, manufacturer, store, extended plan, card
- Provider picker (party) and term/start/end fields
- Choose a known policy from a dropdown or enter custom terms (F9.6)
- Shows source: default, user, extracted
- Saving regenerates reminders
