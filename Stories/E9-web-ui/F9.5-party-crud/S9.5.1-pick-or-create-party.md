# S9.5.1 Pick a party or create one inline

**Feature:** F9.5 Stores, manufacturers, card issuers & warranty providers  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M2  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables); S9.1.3 (Inline quick-add, expand for details)  

As a **user**, I want to choose an existing store or manufacturer, or add a new one without leaving my form.

## Acceptance criteria
- Type-ahead search over global and my private parties
- Duplicates warned before creating (e.g. "Home Depot" vs "The Home Depot")
- Inline quick-add asks only name and role (seller, manufacturer, card issuer, warranty provider)
- Expand or pop-out adds website, claim phone/URL, logo, notes
- Created as private to my account, candidate for global promotion (E6)
