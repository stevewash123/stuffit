# S9.2.5 Dashboard: total recovered

**Feature:** F9.2 Items list & dashboard  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S5.4.11 (Record money recovered), S9.2.2 (Dashboard highlights)  

As a **user**, I want to see how much money WarrantyCat has helped me get back, so I can see the app paying for itself.

## Acceptance criteria
- Home shows **Recovered** with the total in dollars, the number of items, and a split of cash (refunds, card reimbursements, warranty payouts) versus store credit and gift cards
- Period switch: this year, last 12 months, all time; default this year; the choice is remembered on this device
- The total counts the recorded amounts (S5.4.11 recovery records), by the date received, not the purchase date
- A refund entered then removed disappears from the total at once; partial refunds count only what came back
- Selecting the total opens the list of recovered items with amount, date and method; each links to its item
- Counts only the viewer's account, and follows the people filter of S1.3.7 once it exists; a private item is never in anyone else's total
- Zero state: when nothing is recovered yet, show a one-line prompt to mark a refund, not a zero or a nag
- Single currency for now (US dollars); a refund in another currency is entered in dollars by the user
- Used in the plan and billing page as a plain fact ("you have recovered $X"), never as a promise; wording follows S5.4.10
- The same number appears in the export (S1.4.1) and in the future insurance report (F5.3)
