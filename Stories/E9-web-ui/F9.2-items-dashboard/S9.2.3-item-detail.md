**Feature:** F9.2 Items list & dashboard  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S9.2.1 (Items list with coverage highlights); S3.1.1 (Add or edit a coverage (snapshot of terms))

## Acceptance criteria
- Shows item info, purchase (seller, date, price, card, receipt)
- Coverage list with dates, terms, status, claim contacts (ties to F5.1)
- Edit and delete actions
- Attached documents viewable
