# S9.2.4 People on an item

**Feature:** F9.2 Items list & dashboard  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S9.2.3 (Item detail page); S1.3.2 (Add and remove people on an item)  

As a **user**, I want to see who is on an item and change it, so that the right people hear about it.

## Acceptance criteria
- The item page shows its people, with the owner marked
- Add a person from a list of the people in my account; remove one; make someone else the owner; take myself off
- With only one person in the account the control is hidden, not shown empty
- The items list can show only the items I am on

## Notes
Quiet by design: most accounts have one user for a long time.

## Status

Done (M4): the People block on the Item page, hidden when the account has one person. Add from the account, remove, take myself off, make owner (owner only). Tests: `item-detail.page.spec.ts`, `ItemPeopleTests`.
