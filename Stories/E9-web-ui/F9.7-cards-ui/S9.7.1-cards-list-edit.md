# S9.7.1 List, add, edit and remove cards

**Feature:** F9.7 Cards screen  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M2  
**Depends on:** S3.3.1 (Add a card); S3.3.2 (Confirm card benefits)  

As a **user**, I want to manage my cards and their benefits in one place.

## Acceptance criteria
- List comes first, one full-width card per row: nickname, `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-0281`, issuer and card name (bank not repeated), and its policies (confirmed, else the ones we know)
- "+ Add a new card" at the bottom unfolds the form (open from the start when there are no cards); the form follows S3.3.6, S3.3.1 and S3.3.2
- A card page offers confirming benefits (S3.3.2), changing the number (S3.3.5) and searching the internet for newer terms (S3.3.4)
- Removing a card keeps existing coverages
