# S7.1.8 Send feedback or report a problem

**Feature:** F7.1 Admin console  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S1.1.1; S7.6.4; S4.5.1  

As a **customer**, I want to ask a question, suggest something or report a problem without leaving the app, so I get an answer and the team hears about what is wrong.

## Acceptance criteria
- A "Send feedback" entry in the account menu and footer opens a short form: type (Question, Suggestion, Problem), message, and an optional screenshot or file
- The form fills in, without asking, what the customer was looking at (page address, item id if on an item page, app version) and shows them exactly what will be sent
- Nothing else about the customer's items, purchases or cards is attached unless they choose to attach it
- A customer sees their sent messages and the replies (status: Received, In progress, Answered), and replies also reach them by email
- **From a notification:** every notification has a "Report a problem with this" action. It opens the same form with that one notification attached (its title, text and time) and nothing else. The customer can remove the attachment before sending
- A report that came from a notification keeps a link back to it, so the customer sees "About: [notification]" on their sent message
- Sending is rate-limited per account so the form cannot be used to flood the queue

## Notes
Feedback and notifications stay separate things: a notification is the app telling the customer something; feedback is the customer telling the team something. Converting is always the customer's choice, because personal data is never shared by default (concept.md). The administrator side is S7.1.9.
