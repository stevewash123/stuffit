# S7.1.10 See how the app is being used

**Feature:** F7.1 Admin console  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S7.1.3 (admin home); S7.1.5 (admin security); S2.4.5 (kept corrections, for the "left alone" rate)  

As an **administrator**, I want to see whether people are using the app and what they do in it, so I know whether it is working for them, where they stop, and what to fix first.

## Questions this answers
- **Are people coming back?** Sign-ins per day, week and month; how many different people; how many came back after their first week.
- **Are they getting receipts in?** Receipts uploaded (photo, PDF, forwarded email, inbox scan); how many were read well, how many came back blank or with a note (blurry, too dark); how many were duplicates.
- **Are they finishing what they start?** New Items that were added to My Items (quick add or after editing), dismissed, or are still waiting, and how long they wait. A growing pile that nobody touches is the signal.
- **Is the reading any good?** For fields the reader filled in, how often the person left them alone and how often they changed them (S2.4.5), by field.
- **Are reminders useful?** Reminders sent, opened, and acted on (see "What counts as acting" below).
- **Where do new people drop out?** A short funnel: signed up, confirmed email, added a card, first receipt in, first item in My Items, first reminder received.

## Acceptance criteria
- An administrator page (a tab on the admin home, S7.1.3) shows the numbers above for a chosen period (last 7, 30 or 90 days), each with a small trend against the period before it.
- Numbers are counts and rates for the whole platform. No names, no receipt contents. A drill-down lists accounts only by an internal id and counts, not by email, unless a person is looking up one account for support (S7.1.5 rules).
- The page and its endpoints are Operator-only and checked on the server, like the other admin pages.
- Numbers come from records the app already keeps where they exist, and from a small usage log where they do not (see below). They are computed on request from summaries, not by scanning every row on each page view.
- Test accounts, demo accounts and the administrator's own account can be excluded with one switch, so the numbers are real people. Test accounts are marked, not guessed from the email.
- Deterministic and covered by tests with a small set of seeded events, no live service.

## What the app already records, and what it does not
- **Already there:** when each purchase, item, New Item and document was created; whether a New Item was accepted or dismissed and when; the source of each New Item (photo, email, inbox); the cost ledger of every reading with its outcome (read, refused, failed); notifications and when each was read (`ReadAt`).
- **Missing, to add:**
  - **Sign-ins.** There is no last-sign-in or sign-in history today. Add a record of each successful sign-in (account, time, how: password, Google).
  - **Quick add versus add with edits.** Both end as an accepted New Item; the difference is not kept. Record which path was used when an item is accepted.
  - **Acting on a notification.** `ReadAt` says it was opened, not that the person did anything about it. See below.
  - **Corrections.** Kept by S2.4.5, which this story depends on for the "left alone" rate.
- Everything added is a plain counter or timestamp. It holds no receipt text, names or other personal content.

## What counts as acting on a notification
Opened is the weak signal; acting is the useful one. A notification counts as acted on when, within a set time after it was sent (for example 7 days), the person does the thing it was about: for a reminder, opens the item or marks the reminder done or snoozed; for an ending-soon warning, opens the item or files a claim; for a New Item notice, accepts or dismisses it. The notification keeps what it refers to (`ReferenceType`), so this can be worked out from the follow-up record, with no tracking of clicks inside email.

## Privacy and limits
- Internal product measurement only, covered by the privacy policy already shown (S7.2.1). No third-party analytics and no tracking pixels are added.
- Counts that fall below a small number (for example under 5 accounts) are shown as "fewer than 5", so a single person cannot be picked out of a breakdown.
- Usage records are kept for a limited time (for example 13 months) and then reduced to the monthly totals.

## Not in this story
- Real-time or live dashboards; a daily summary is enough.
- Per-person behaviour profiles, session recordings, or heat maps.
- Product experiments or A/B tests.
- Exporting to an outside analytics tool. Revisit if the page is not enough.

## Two views (decided: both wanted; how to split them is left open)
1. **Platform view** (everything above): counts and rates across all accounts, for a period.
2. **One account's timeline**, for support and for early on, when there are too few users for percentages to mean anything: a single account's events in order (signed up, confirmed email, added a card, uploaded a receipt and how it read, quick added, dismissed, got a reminder and whether anything followed), so it is plain where that person got stuck. Reached by looking up one account, under the support rules in S7.1.5; it is the one place an email may be shown.

Whether these become two stories (platform view; account timeline) or stay two sections of this one is not decided. They share the same usage records, so the records and their tests would be built once either way.
