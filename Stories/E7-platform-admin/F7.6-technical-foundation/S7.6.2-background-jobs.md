# S7.6.2 Background job scheduler

**Feature:** F7.6 Technical foundation  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M0  
**Depends on:** S7.6.1 (Environments and deployment)  

As a **developer**, I want to run scheduled and queued jobs.

## Acceptance criteria
- Cron-style scheduler for daily and hourly jobs (reminders, digest, sweeps), all registered in one place
- Retryable queue for email sending and LLM calls; delayed jobs supported for debounce (S4.2.4)
- Every run logged: job, started, finished, status, counts; failures visible to the operator
- Jobs are idempotent and claim their work atomically (processing_started_at) so a retry or overlap cannot double-send
- Ordering between jobs is expressed in code (run B after A completes), not by staggering cron times

## Notes
Technical story. ConsignmentGenie uses Hangfire with cron registrations at startup and staggers 2:00, 2:30, 3:00 AM to order jobs.
