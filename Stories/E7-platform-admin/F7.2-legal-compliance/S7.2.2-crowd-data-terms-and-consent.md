# S7.2.2 Crowd-data terms and consent at signup

**Feature:** F7.2 Legal & compliance  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.1.1; S7.2.1  

As a **new user**, I want to understand and agree that shared reference data is community-powered.

## Acceptance criteria
- Signup shows a short plain-language box: StuffIt runs on shared knowledge. Warranty and return policies and official warranty documents you add are shared with other users. Your purchases, receipts, cards and account details are never shared
- The box and the Terms also say plainly that uploaded warranty documents are shared anonymously by default to improve matching and to verify existing records; that personal details are redacted and shown first; and that sharing can be turned off or withdrawn (S6.1.6)
- Agreement to the Terms and Privacy Policy is required to sign up; the terms version and the time are stored on the user
- Terms define exactly what is shared (policy facts: terms, durations, conditions, links, and redacted copies of uploaded warranty documents) and what never is (purchases, card details, account data, and personal information on a document, which is redacted)
- A change to the terms asks existing users to accept the new version
- Aggregate learning from edits (counts of accepted versus changed values) is covered in the terms and carries no personal data

## Notes
Have a lawyer read the final wording. Pre-ticked consent boxes are not valid consent in the EU, so revisit if EU users are ever targeted; shared data here is non-personal facts, and uploads are checked for personal information before sharing.
