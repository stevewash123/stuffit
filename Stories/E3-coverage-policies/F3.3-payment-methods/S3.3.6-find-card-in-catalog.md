# S3.3.6 Find a card in the catalog, or add one we do not list

**Feature:** F3.3 Payment methods & card benefits  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S3.3.1 (Add a card); S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables)  

As a **user**, I want to find my card by typing a bank or card name, and to add it myself if it is not listed, so I am not stopped by a small seed list.

## Acceptance criteria
- A card catalog (`card_products`: issuer + card name, global reference data) lists named cards with or without known benefits. Seeded with about 115 mainstream US cards; a card with no benefit rows still appears
- "Add a new card" opens a **New card** panel marked as not saved yet, with Cancel
- **Card Name** is one editable box with a dropdown chevron. Typing shows matching cards (every typed word matches issuer or card name, typed words shown in bold); nothing is listed until the user types or presses the chevron. Arrow keys move a highlight; Enter picks only a highlighted row; with none highlighted Enter uses the typed text as a new card
- Picking a card shows its **Bank / Issuer** as text with a pencil to change it. Nickname defaults to the card name until the user types their own
- "Add 'X' as a new card" switches Card Name to a plain text box (no lookup, nothing resets while editing) and asks for the bank with a search-or-add picker; "Pick from our list instead" goes back
- **Last 4 is required**: Add card is disabled until it is 4 digits (the API still accepts none, for older cards)
- A listed card does not offer the internet search while being added; its page does, to refresh its terms (S3.3.4). A new card of the user's own naming does (S3.3.4)
- Card names are shown as issuers publish them, and the bank is not repeated when the name already contains it

## Status
Done (M4 session), including the seed list. Seeded names are not verified against issuers; see S3.2.4.
