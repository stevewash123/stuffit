# S3.5.1 Add a vehicle

**Feature:** F3.5 Vehicles
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** the item and purchase model (S2.1); S2.7.2 (partial data); S3.1.1 (coverage)
**Widens:** S2.7.15 (VIN decoding stays there)

As a **user**, I want to add a car, truck, motorcycle, boat or RV as its own kind of thing, so the fields that matter for a vehicle are asked for, and the form is not the appliance form.

## Why
A vehicle is bought once, driven for years and sold on, and what matters about it (when it was first put in service, how many miles it had, the identification number) is not what matters for a toaster. It is still an item: it has a purchase, a receipt or bill of sale, coverages, reminders and parts. So it is an item with a type, not a separate product.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Add a vehicle** is offered next to Add a purchase. It opens its own short form: nickname (required, for example "Dad's truck"), kind (car, truck, motorcycle, boat, RV, other), year, make, model, trim or size, identification number (optional), new or used when bought, date bought, price, where bought (a dealer or a person), and the odometer at purchase (optional)
- One vehicle type covers all kinds. A kind picker changes wording only (miles or hours, "VIN" or "hull number"); it never hides or adds rules until a real difference in behavior is agreed
- The odometer unit is miles or kilometres, chosen per vehicle. Hours are allowed for a boat or a mower. The unit is shown wherever the value is
- A vehicle is an item: it has a purchase, an owner, people who follow it, documents, coverages and reminders exactly like any other item, and it appears in My Items with its kind shown and a filter for Vehicles
- Year must be a sensible year (1900 up to next year). A 17-character VIN on a road vehicle gets its check digit tested; a bad one is a gentle warning ("this does not look like a valid VIN, check it"), never a block, because older, foreign and hand-entered numbers are real
- A date bought later than today is refused. A used vehicle asks for **the date it was first put in service, if known**; "not known" is a normal answer and nothing is guessed (S3.5.4 uses it)
- The bill of sale, window sticker, title copy or any other paper can be added as documents on the vehicle through the normal paperwork reader; what the reader finds (make, model, year, price, date, VIN) is offered as a suggestion, and the person accepts or corrects it
- The vehicle can be edited and deleted. Deleting a vehicle that has parts asks first (S3.5.5)
- Nothing here requires a company, a plan or insurance to be set up
- The vehicle shows in the data export with its fields
- Plain words, no legal advice. The screens say WarrantyCat

## Notes
- Stored as an item plus a one-to-one vehicle detail record (kind, year, trim, identification number, odometer unit, in-service date, condition when bought), so an appliance carries none of it.
- Category: a Vehicles category exists for it; the person does not pick it.
- A mined or decoded value shows as read from the document or the VIN; a typed or corrected one shows as entered by the person (the same rule as S3.1.8).
