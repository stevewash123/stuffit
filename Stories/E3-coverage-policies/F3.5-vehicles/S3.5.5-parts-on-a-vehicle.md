# S3.5.5 Parts on a vehicle

**Feature:** F3.5 Vehicles
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.5.1; the item and purchase model; S3.4.13 (the item page shows the protection stack)

As a **user**, I want a vehicle to list its parts (tires, battery, brakes, a stereo, a lift kit), each with its own receipt and warranty, so I know what is on the vehicle and what is still covered.

## Why
Parts are bought separately, from other sellers, years after the vehicle, and carry their own warranties. A part is always an ordinary item (Steve, 2026-11-05): nothing about a tire's receipt, warranty or reminder is special except that it belongs to a vehicle.

## Acceptance criteria
- An item can have a **parent item**, and only a vehicle is offered as a parent for now. The link does not change anything else about the part: its own purchase, seller, receipt, coverages, people and reminders
- On a vehicle page, a **Parts** list shows each part: name, brand, when it was installed, and its coverage status ("Warranty until Mar 2029 or 50,000 miles"). A summary line reads, for example, "3 parts. 1 warranty ends in 60 days."
- **Add a part** from the vehicle page opens the normal add form with the vehicle already chosen and asks the two extra facts (S3.5.6): installed on, and the odometer when installed. A receipt or order email forwarded to WarrantyCat is read as today and the person can link the resulting item to a vehicle with **Part of a vehicle**
- An item that already exists can be linked as a part from its own page or from the vehicle's, and unlinked again. A part can belong to one vehicle at a time, and a vehicle cannot be a part of itself or of its own part
- A part appears in My Items like any item, with a "Part of <vehicle nickname>" tag and a filter for Parts. It also appears under its vehicle
- A set that is bought and replaced together (four matching tires) is one item; the quantity goes in the name or notes. Different tires on the same vehicle are separate items
- A part's owner and followers start as the vehicle's owner and followers and can be changed
- **Deleting a vehicle that has parts** asks: delete the parts with it, or keep them as separate items. Never silently one or the other. Marking the vehicle returned or disposed asks the same way
- The vehicle's data export includes its parts and each part's coverages
- A part with no warranty is fine and is listed without one; no company, plan or insurance is required
- Reminders for a part come from the part's own coverages; they may mention the vehicle by nickname

## Notes
- Parts of parts are not built; the structure allows it later (the same link would serve an appliance with a replaceable component).
- Selling a vehicle and what its parts mean for each warranty is a later story; here the person decides parts one at a time.
