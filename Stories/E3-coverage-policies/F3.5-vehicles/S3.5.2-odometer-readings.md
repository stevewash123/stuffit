# S3.5.2 Odometer readings and the pace

**Feature:** F3.5 Vehicles
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.5.1 (Add a vehicle)

As a **user**, I want to record what my odometer reads from time to time, so WarrantyCat can tell how many miles of a warranty are left and roughly when they will run out.

## Why
Almost every vehicle warranty is "this many years or this many miles, whichever comes first". The date side is arithmetic; the miles side only exists if the person tells us the miles. Miles are entered by hand. Service invoices and dash photos may later carry a reading too, so the record has a source from the start.

## Acceptance criteria
- A vehicle has a log of readings: date, value, unit (the vehicle's), source (**you entered it** or **read from a document**), and an optional note. The odometer at purchase from S3.5.1 is the first reading
- Add a reading from the vehicle page in two taps: value, date defaulting to today
- Readings are listed newest first with the date; any reading can be edited or deleted
- A reading lower than an earlier reading is not blocked, because odometers get replaced and people mistype. It asks once: "This is lower than the reading on <date>. Save it anyway?" and keeps it flagged "lower than an earlier reading"; the pace and the warranty arithmetic ignore a flagged reading until the person confirms it as correct or removes it
- A reading dated in the future is refused
- The vehicle page shows the **latest reading and its date** ("42,100 miles on Oct 3, 2026"). It is never shown without its date
- **Pace**: with two readings at least 30 days apart, WarrantyCat works out miles per year from the oldest and newest. With fewer it says "Add another reading a month or more later to estimate your pace" and shows no estimate. Nothing is invented from a model default
- **Estimated odometer today**: latest reading plus pace times the days since. It is always labelled "estimated", is shown smaller than the entered reading, and is never saved as a reading
- A reading read from a service invoice (when S3.5.x service records exist) is shown as read from the document with a link to it, and the person can correct it; a person's entry always wins over a mined one for the same date
- Readings are in the data export
- There is no prompt to update the odometer in this story (reminders come with maintenance); a vehicle that never has a reading simply shows date-only coverage

## Notes
- Hours (boats, mowers) behave the same way as miles.
- A stale latest reading is a fact the warranty screens must be honest about: "last reading 14 months ago" appears next to any miles figure that rests on it (S3.5.3).
