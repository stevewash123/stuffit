# S3.5.8 Leased vehicles

**Feature:** F3.5 Vehicles
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.5.1 (add a vehicle), S3.5.7 (one Add screen), S4.6 (to-dos), the notification catalog
**Origin:** Steve, 2026-11-08.

As a **user** who leases a car, I want to say so, so the lease end is not missed and I am reminded to check my mileage against the allowance.

## Decisions (Steve, 2026-11-08)
- **Bought or Leased is its own choice** ("You have it"), not a payment type. A leased car is still paid by a payment plan, card or cash.
- **Warranty is tracked the same as any vehicle.** Nothing about coverage changes for a lease.
- **Lease end date** is shown on the vehicle with this note: "If you bought GAP coverage or an extended warranty with this car, you may be owed a partial refund. Check with your dealer or lender." The same note is in the lease-end to-do.
- **We do not track mileage** (not our job). Instead, a vehicle with a yearly allowance gets two kinds of reminder: **halfway through each lease year** and **three months before the lease ends**, each asking the person to check their odometer against the allowance. It is its own notification type, **Lease mileage check**, so it can be turned off in the notification settings.

## Acceptance criteria
- Add a vehicle and the vehicle page's details form have **You have it: Bought / Leased**. Leased asks for **lease end date**, **allowed per year** (in the vehicle's unit) and **fee per unit over** (optional)
- A lease cannot end before the date the vehicle was got. Lease fields are kept only on a leased vehicle: changing to Bought clears them
- A leased vehicle with an end date gets one **lease-end to-do** ("<vehicle>: your lease ends soon", due on the end date, nudging from 90 days before) carrying the refund note. Moving the end date moves the to-do (no second one); changing to Bought or clearing the date finishes it
- The vehicle page shows "Leased until <date> · N miles a year allowed" and the refund note
- The reminder job sends **Lease mileage check** at: 6 months after the lease began, then every 12 months while before the three-month point, and once three months before the end. Only for a leased vehicle with a yearly allowance that is still owned. Each point is sent once per owner; a point missed by more than 14 days (job down, or the vehicle added late) is skipped, not sent late. The message states the allowance (and the fee if given) and says WarrantyCat does not track the person's miles
- The notification can be turned off per channel in the settings grid (category Expiring)

## Not built
- Warning from actual odometer readings against the allowance (decided against: not our job)
- A monthly payment, lessor and payoff fields; lease-agreement reading; a "bought it out" shortcut (edit the vehicle and choose Bought)
- Returning a leased vehicle at lease end uses the normal Mark returned

## Tests
- Unit: the check-in points (three-year lease, short lease, midpoint too close to the final), the grace window, ids, wording
- Integration: a leased vehicle keeps its lease and gets one to-do with the note, moving and buying out; a lease cannot end early and a bought vehicle keeps none; the job sends halfway and three-months-left once each, none without an allowance or for a bought vehicle, and the preference is respected
- UI: the form asks for lease fields only when leased and sends them; the vehicle page shows the lease line and note
