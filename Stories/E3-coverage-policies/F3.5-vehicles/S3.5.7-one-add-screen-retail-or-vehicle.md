# S3.5.7 One Add screen: retail purchase or vehicle

**Feature:** F3.5 Vehicles
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.5.1 (add a vehicle), S9.3.1 (add a purchase)
**Origin:** Steve tried adding a car through Add a purchase (2026-11-08): the card, order number, "Store" and "Item" fit badly, and nothing steered him to the vehicle form. People will do the same unless they are steered.

As a **user**, I want one Add screen that asks what I am adding, so a car gets the vehicle form and an ordinary purchase never shows me vehicle choices.

## Decisions (Steve, 2026-11-08)
- **One entry, a type choice at the top.** Two tiles: **Retail purchase** (default) and **Vehicle** (help line: car, truck, motorcycle, boat, RV). The tile swaps the form beneath it. The two existing forms stay; this screen hosts them. One "Add" button replaces "Add a purchase" and "Add a vehicle"; `/add/new` and `/add/vehicle` both open it (with the matching tile chosen).
- **Retail never offers vehicle categories.** The Category list on the retail form leaves out the Vehicles category.
- **A vehicle brand on a retail purchase is allowed, after a confirm.** Picking Ford, Toyota and so on as the brand asks "Is this a part or accessory, not a vehicle?" and goes on if they say yes (they sell accessories, tires, floor mats). If they say it is a vehicle, the form offers the switch.
- **A vehicle model on a retail purchase is refused.** If the model typed for a vehicle brand is one of that brand's known vehicle models (Ford: Bronco), the form rejects it with a plain message and a button to the vehicle form, carrying over what was typed. The list of vehicle models is shared reference data (the `models` seed, category Vehicles), not code.
- **Payment on a vehicle:** *Payment plan* first, then *Credit card* and *Cash or check*. A card is picked only when Credit card is chosen. The vehicle form has no order number.
- Wording on the vehicle side: "Dealer or seller" (not "Store"); the thing is a vehicle, named by its nickname.

## Acceptance criteria
- My Items, Home and the empty states show one Add button. It opens the Add screen with Retail purchase chosen; the vehicle tile opens the vehicle form without leaving the page or losing the screen's place
- Arriving with `?pending=` or `?vehicle=` (a New Item, or a part for a vehicle) goes straight to the retail form with no tiles
- Retail Category list has no Vehicles entry. A New Item or saved purchase that already holds a vehicle category still shows and saves it
- Brand confirm: a manufacturer that has vehicle models in the shared data asks once per form; answering "a part or accessory" is remembered for that form
- Model refusal: a model that matches (case and spacing ignored) a vehicle model of the chosen brand shows the message "Bronco is a Ford vehicle. Add it as a vehicle so its odometer, parts and mileage coverage work." with Switch to the vehicle form; Save is blocked until the model is changed or the form is switched. The same check runs on the server so a direct call is refused too
- Switching carries over brand/make, model, price, date, seller and attached papers
- Vehicle form: Paid by (Payment plan, Credit card, Cash or check; none selected is allowed); a card list only for Credit card. Stored on the purchase. Shown on the vehicle page and in the export
- A plan's lender and monthly amount are not asked in this story (see Not built)

## Not built (later)
- Payment-plan details: lender, monthly amount, term and payoff date; reminders on them
- Vehicle model data beyond the makers already in the shared data; the list is extended as makes are added

## Tests
- Add screen: tile swaps form; pending/vehicle arrivals skip tiles; retail category list has no Vehicles; brand confirm; model refusal and switch carry-over
- API: a retail purchase with a vehicle brand's vehicle model is refused; a vehicle with each Paid by kind saves, a card only with Credit card
