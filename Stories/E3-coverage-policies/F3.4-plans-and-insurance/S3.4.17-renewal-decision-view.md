# S3.4.17 A plan is ending or renewing: what I decide, with the facts in one place

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans, plan-level reminder built 2026-10-03); S3.4.6 (renewal terms); S3.4.8 (requests paid out); S3.4.15 (cancel); S4.1.1 (reminders)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03.

As a **user** who is told a plan renews or ends in 30 days, I want the reminder to open a page with what I need to decide, so "cancel or compare" is a real choice and not a nag.

## Why this matters
The reminder exists (plan level, 30 days ahead; "renews on ... cancel or compare" / "ends on ... renew or replace it"). It links to the plan page, which is a form. The facts that make the decision are scattered: how long it has run, what it cost in total, what the items are now worth and how old they are, whether it was ever used, what the renewal price is, and how to cancel.

## Acceptance criteria
- The reminder, the notification and the daily digest link to a **Plan decision** panel at the top of the plan page while the plan is within its lead time (default 30 days; the existing setting).
- The panel shows, with sources: the renewal or end date; **the renewal price when the contract or the person gives one** (blank otherwise, "ask the company"); what the plan has cost so far (price paid plus service fees, S3.4.8); what it has paid out or replaced (service requests, S3.4.8); the covered items with price, purchase date, age, and any that are past the maker's warranty; whether any item is also covered by another layer (card, maker, insurance).
- Actions on the panel: **Cancel it** or **Stop it renewing** (S3.4.15); **I renewed it** (new term from the renewal date, price update, files); **Replace it with another** (starts the S3.4.10 sheet with these items pre-ticked); **Remind me later** (snooze 7 / 14 days, bounded by the end date); **Nothing to do**.
- **No verdict.** The panel never says whether to renew. No red/green on value.
- An auto-renewing plan also reminds a few days before the date (S3.4.2); the second reminder opens the same panel, with *Stop it renewing* first.
- **Home tile** (S3.4.12) lists plans in a decision window, soonest first.
- **After the date.** A plan that passed its end or renewal date without an answer is flagged "Did this renew?" with *Renewed* / *Ended* / *Cancelled*; a plan is not left active past its end silently.
