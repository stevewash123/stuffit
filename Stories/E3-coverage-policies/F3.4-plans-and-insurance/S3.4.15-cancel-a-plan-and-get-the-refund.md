# S3.4.15 Cancel a plan and get the refund

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S2.4.13 (a refund of a plan cancels it); S5.4.11 (money recovered); S3.4.5 (cancel rules on a plan type); S3.4.6 (renewal)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03.

As a **user** who no longer wants a plan (it auto-renews, I returned the item, I found a better one), I want to know whether I can cancel it for money back and how, then record it, so I stop paying and I know what was returned.

## Why this matters
Today a plan can only be ended with a date ("It ended on"), which is a record, not a cancellation: it says nothing about refund rights, how to cancel, or what came back. S2.4.13 handles one path (a refund email for a plan alone). The common paths are cancel-within-30-days-for-full-refund, cancel mid-term for a prorated refund, and stop an auto-renewal before it bills.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Cancel this plan** (separate from "End this plan") on the plan page. It is a short flow:
  1. *What do you want to do?* **Stop it renewing** / **Cancel it now** / **It already ended or I cancelled it** (records the date only, as today).
  2. For the first two, shows what we know: the plan's cancellation terms (read from the contract or the plan type, quoted, S3.4.5), the days left in a cancel-for-full-refund window if one is stated, how to cancel (phone, site, address for a written notice), and the contract number to quote. Never states what the person is entitled to; only what the document says and where. State differences are noted (S3.4.9) when known.
  3. *Done it?* **I cancelled** records the cancellation date and how, and sets the end (or turns off renewal). **I got a refund** records the amount, date and method.
- A refund joins **Money recovered** (S5.4.11) as plan refund, not as a returned item, and the plan shows "Cancelled <date>, refunded <amount>" (as S2.4.13 does for receipts).
- The yearly cost counts only what is still billed: a plan cancelled mid-term costs the months up to the cancellation.
- An auto-renewing plan stopped before its renewal date says "Ends <date> (will not renew)", the renewal reminder is replaced by none, and the end reminder remains.
- **Refund pending:** cancelling without a refund recorded keeps a to-do, "Check that your refund arrived" due in 14 days.
- The covered items follow: coverage ends on the cancellation date (not the original end), as for ending a plan (S3.4.2).
- Undo within the session; later, a cancelled plan can be re-opened with a note.
