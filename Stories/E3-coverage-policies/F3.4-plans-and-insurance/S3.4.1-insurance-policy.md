# S3.4.1 Renter's or homeowner's insurance policy

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.1.1 (Add or edit a coverage); S2.1.2 (Attach receipts, manuals, photos); S4.1.1 (Generate reminders from coverage dates); S2.4.1 (Document reader)  

As a **user** who rents or owns a home, I want to record my insurance policy and see which of my things it covers, so I know what protection I actually have and can prove what I own if I need to claim.

## Why this matters
Many people own a lot of modest things (instruments, amps, electronics, fishing gear) and have given up on warranties because the return is small. For them the protection that counts is often an insurance policy, and the useful thing StuffIt can offer is a dated, documented inventory of what they own: purchase date, price, model and serial, receipt. That is what a claim asks for, and it is what most people do not have when they need it.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Add a policy.** Kind (renter's, homeowner's, condo, or other), insurer (picked from stores and brands like any party, or added), policy number, start and end dates (or renewal date), how it renews, premium, deductible, personal property limit, and claim phone and website. Notes. Attach the policy document and the declarations page (S2.1.2).
- **Start from the document.** The same way as plans (S3.4.2): upload the policy or its declarations page and reading it fills in insurer, policy number, dates, premium, deductible, personal property limit and the special limits it lists. Each field read from the document is marked with its quote and page, for the user to confirm or fix; unclear fields stay blank, and nothing is saved until the user accepts.
- **Special limits.** Add the limits the policy sets for kinds of items, as free entries the user types in (for example a single-item or category limit for instruments, electronics or jewelry), because policies differ. StuffIt does not invent or look up limits; it shows what the user recorded.
- **Scheduled items.** Mark an item as listed separately on the policy (a rider or schedule) with its insured value. A reminder to update the value is optional.
- **Which items it covers.** An item can be linked to the policy. The item page then shows an "Insurance" line: policy, deductible, the relevant limit the user recorded, and any scheduled value. The link can be made one item at a time, or "all my items" as the default, with exceptions.
- **A gap hint, not advice.** Where the user recorded a limit and an item's price is higher, or the total price of items is above the personal property limit, show a plain note ("This item cost more than the limit you recorded for instruments"). Never say what the user should buy.
- **Renewal reminders** through the existing reminder system (S4.1.1), with the policy's own lead time (default 30 days), because renewal is when the user compares or updates it.
- **Inventory for a claim.** One action produces an inventory of items with photos, dates, prices, models, serials and receipts. This is the same output as the insurance report (F5.3); this story only makes sure the policy is on it.
- **Several policies** are allowed (for example a separate umbrella or an instrument policy). Ending or replacing one keeps the old one as history.
- The policy is private to the account, like purchases. It is not shared reference data.

## Data
`insurance_policies` (account-owned): kind, insurer party id, policy number, start, end or renewal date, auto-renew, premium, deductible, personal property limit, claim contacts, notes, status. `insurance_limits` (policy id, label, amount). `item_insurance` (item id, policy id, scheduled value). Documents use the existing document owner type (add `InsurancePolicy`).

## Not in this story
- Buying, comparing or recommending insurance.
- Making or tracking an insurance claim (E5), though a claim should be able to point at the policy.

## Status

Built 2026-10-03 without document reading (API and UI; migration InsurancePolicies applied to dev and test; not browser-checked). Account > Insurance (`/insurance`) lists policies with status, kind, insurer, items covered and deductible, and what the running ones cost a year. A policy (`/insurance/new`, `/insurance/:id`) has kind, insurer, policy number, term and auto-renew, premium, deductible, personal property limit, claim phone and website, notes, limits typed in by the user (kept by name; dropping one clears the pointer from items), and a choice of covering all items (with items left out) or only the items linked. An item can be linked, left out, listed at its own value, or put under one of the limits. The item page has an Insurance card (`GET /api/items/{id}/insurance`) showing the policies that cover it today, the deductible, the limit and listed value, and a plain note when it cost more than a limit the user recorded or is listed below its price. The policy page notes when the covered items cost more than the personal property limit. Never advice. Ending a policy stops it covering items and counting toward cost; deleting an item drops it from the policy. Documents upload to the policy. The renewal reminder is a general reminder (`todos.ref_id`, source system, due on the end date, shown 30 days ahead, reworded for auto-renew) kept by `RenewalReminders`; it follows the end date, comes back when a new term is set after being marked done, and goes when the policy ends or is deleted. Warranty plans (S3.4.2) now get the same reminder, one per plan. The export has `insurance_policies.csv`, `insurance_limits.csv` and the documents under `files/insurance`. Tests: `InsuranceTests` (8), `insurance.page.spec.ts`, `policy.page.spec.ts`, `item-insurance.spec.ts`.
"Start from the document" built 2026-10-05 (API and UI, tests pass; not browser-checked, and nothing has run against the real Anthropic API yet, so the readings come from the fake provider in tests). On a new policy (`/insurance/new`) a "Start from your policy" box takes the policy or declarations page (PDF or photo). `POST /api/document-readings/upload` cleans photos the way an upload is cleaned (upright, no location or device data, HEIC to JPEG), reads them as kind `insurance_policy`, and keeps no copy: only the result is stored, by hash, so the same file again is instant and free. The page shows "What we found" (`reading-review` component, shared with the other readers): each value with the words copied from the document, the page and how sure it is; clear values start ticked, unsure ones and ones that failed their checks start unticked; what the document did not say is listed. Nothing is filled in until the person presses the button, and nothing is saved until they save the policy. Mapping (`policy-reading.ts`, unit tested): insurer (matched to a known insurer by exact name, otherwise a note to pick or add it), policy number, kind from the policy type, dates, premium and whether it is monthly or yearly, deductible, personal property limit, claim phone and website, and the special limits split into a name and an amount (entries that cannot be split are shown as written). Items the document lists separately are shown in a note, not applied: they need linking to the person's items after saving. The files read can be kept with the policy when it is saved (ticked by default). If the document cannot be read (provider off, over the daily limit, a failure) the page says so plainly and the form is typed as before.
Later the same day: a matched insurer's known claim phone and website fill only what the policy left blank, and the page says they came from the insurer's record; and editing an existing policy shows "Update from a newer document", which lists what the document changes ("Deductible: $500.00 → $750.00", "Limit for Musical instruments: $2,500.00 → $3,000.00", "New limit: Jewelry $1,500.00") before anything is saved, never swaps the insurer, and adds the document to the policy when saved.
Scheduled items applied 2026-10-06 (UI only, tested, not browser-checked): after a policy is read, "Listed separately on the document" lists each entry on the policy page with its value (parsed like a limit); "Choose the item" opens the item picker and records the listed value on the picked item (an entry with no value opens the link editor); Skip drops it. The list lives on the page, so leaving the page forgets it.
Not done: the inventory output for a claim (F5.3), a "Plans and insurance" combined list page (they are two pages under Account today), several policies shown together with a total-value gap check, the insurer role (insurers are picked as warranty providers), and reminders actually being sent (S4.6.3).
