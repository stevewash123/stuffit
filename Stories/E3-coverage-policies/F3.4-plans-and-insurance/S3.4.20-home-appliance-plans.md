# S3.4.20 Home appliance plans: one plan, many appliances, each with its own age

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S3.4.7 (covered home and lines); S3.4.8 (limits, fees, requests); S3.4.10 (add from the item); S3.4.16 (item replaced)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03: "home appliances are as well."

As a **user** with a plan on my appliances (a store's multi-appliance plan, a warranty company's appliance plan, a home warranty with appliance add-on), I want each appliance listed under the plan with its own age, limit and history, so I can tell at the moment something breaks whether this appliance is covered and what the call will cost.

**The model example is a home-service plan such as American Home Shield:** a third party sells a monthly plan covering the appliances (and often systems) in your home. On each covered appliance it appears as Add-on protection (S3.4.12).

## Why this matters
Appliances are bought over many years; the plan is bought once. Which are covered, how old each is, and what has been spent on each differs per appliance. Covering items by picking them from the item list (S3.4.2) fits appliances you bought with a receipt, but not the ones that came with the house, and does not carry per-appliance limits, ages or request history.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Appliance lines.** A plan lists appliances (refrigerator, range, dishwasher, washer, dryer, microwave, water heater, freezer) either as links to owned items or as lines with only a name and age (S3.4.7 lines). Per line: brand and model (optional), approximate age ("about 6 years old", S2.7 partial dates), limit per appliance, exclusions or an age limit the contract states ("not covered if over 12 years").
- **Add the appliance later.** A new item whose category matches an active plan's covered kinds offers "Add to your <plan>?", never silently (S3.4.2). A plan that says "everything in a category" lists the matched items for confirmation.
- **A line the person unticks** ("my contract does not cover the second fridge") is recorded as excluded, not forgotten.
- **Appliance age.** Age comes from the item's purchase date when known, else from the person's estimate or the home's. Appliances beyond an age limit stated in the contract are flagged with the quote, not removed.
- **Request service** (S3.4.8) from a line: shows the service fee, the limit remaining for that appliance, requests used this term, and the claim phone and contract number. The history for one appliance reads in one place (its requests, replacements, fees, decision).
- **Replaced under the plan** (S3.4.16): the replacement inherits the line; the plan records the date and, when the contract caps replacements, how many are used.
- **Cap and reset.** Per-appliance annual limits and any overall limit reset on the plan's anniversary and show "$180 of $2,000 used".
- **Cost view**: price x billing periods, plus fees, against the appliances' prices and ages; one plan counted once.
- **Not a single-item plan.** A plan covering exactly one appliance still uses this layout when it is sold by a warranty company; a store plan on one appliance stays the simple S3.4.3 form.
