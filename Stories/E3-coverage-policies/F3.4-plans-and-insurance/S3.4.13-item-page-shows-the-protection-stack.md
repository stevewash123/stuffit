# S3.4.13 The item page shows what the plan adds to the stack, and its true state

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S3.1.7 (item page view and edit); S5.1.2 (order of coverages); S3.4.6 (waiting period)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03; UAT FS-31 steps 10, 18; FS-40 step 6.

As a **user** who elected a plan, I want the item page to say plainly what the plan gives me beyond the maker's warranty and the card, when each starts and ends, and what to do when something breaks, so I can act without opening the contract.

## Why this matters
With a plan, an item can have a return window, a maker warranty, a card extension and a plan at once. The page shows them as a row of coverages with dates. It does not say that the plan takes over after the maker's warranty, what it costs per claim, or who to call. And an ended plan still reads "Ends today" and keeps the item "Expiring soon" (open since UAT 2026-11-02, build d1ed2c0, still failing in 982952c).

## Acceptance criteria
- **State is true.** A plan ended today, or ended any day, shows as Ended on the item (not "Ends today") and does not count as running coverage. The item's badge, "Next to end", and the plan's own badge agree. Fixes FS-31-18, FS-40-6.
- **Plan card on the item**, in view mode: company and plan name; status (waiting, active, ending soon, renews, ended, cancelled); start and end or next renewal; **what it adds** in one line ("Covers accidental damage; maker's warranty does not"); service fee; claim phone and site as tap targets; contract number with a copy button; a link to the plan.
- **How the layers stack.** A one-line explanation under the list when more than one extended layer exists: "Your Chase card adds 12 months to the maker's warranty; the Geek Squad plan runs from Oct 10, 2026 to Oct 10, 2030." If a plan starts when the maker's warranty ends (S3.4.3 open question) say so and show the computed date.
- **The next useful thing is first.** The coverage that applies today is marked "Try this first"; while the maker's warranty runs it is first and the plan is "Backup" (the claim wizard, S5.4.12, uses the same order).
- **A plan needing details** (bought without price, contract number or claim contact) shows "Add the rest" (S3.4.10) rather than blank fields.
- **Evidence and source.** The line "From your receipt: Paid $299.99, plan number GS-88104471, covers SKU 6571204" stays, with a confidence label when the reader was not sure (FS-41 step 5).
- **No advice.** No wording that suggests the plan was or was not worth buying.
