# S3.4.4 A warranty company's page leads to its plans

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S9.5.3 (view a shared party); S9.6.1 (add a policy for a party)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-10-27, adding 2-10 Home Buyers Warranty as a company and finding nothing to add for it.

As a **user** who just added a warranty company, I want its page to tell me what to do next, so I am not left at a page with nothing to add.

## Why this matters
A company page shows an "add" section only for the roles it knows how to hold policies for: a store (return policy), a brand (warranty) and a card issuer (benefits). A company with only the role "warranty provider" shows none of them, so the person who added 2-10 sees a page with a name and no way forward. The plans story (S3.4.2) already lets them add a plan, but nothing on this page says so.

## Acceptance criteria
- A party with the role warranty provider shows a **Plans** section on its page, with the plans this account holds from the company (name, dates, status, items covered) and **Add a plan from this company**, which opens the plan form (S3.4.2) with the company already chosen.
- With no plans yet, the section says what a plan is in one line ("A contract that covers several things you own, such as a home warranty") and offers "Upload the contract" and "Type it in".
- The shared plan types the company offers (S3.4.5) are listed under it when there are any, each with a "Use this plan" button that starts the plan form filled from the shared terms.
- A company with more than one role shows each role's section; a company with no role yet asks which of store, brand, card issuer or warranty provider it is, instead of showing nothing.
- Adding a company from the plan form or a document (S3.4.2) sets the warranty provider role without asking.
- The company's website, claim phone and claim link stay editable here and fill the "Make a claim" help (S3.4.2).

## Open questions
- Whether "Plan types" (S3.4.5) are edited by the account that holds the plan or only by an operator, as for store return policies.
