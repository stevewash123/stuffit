# S3.4.12 One front door for extended warranties

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S9.1.6 (account menu); S3.4.10 (add from the item)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03.

As a **user**, I want one clear place and one clear word for "extended warranty", so I can find, add and review what I hold without learning the app's internal split between plans, coverages, card benefits and insurance.

## Why this matters
The same real-world thing shows under four names: "Extended warranties" (Account menu and `/plans`), "Extended plan" (a coverage kind), "protection plan" (receipt and claim wizard wording), and "Extended warranty" the card benefit. They are different records. A person who elected a plan expects one list; today they get a menu entry that is nearly hidden, and an item page that does not link back to it.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Words.** One name for a paid plan everywhere: **protection plan** (matching what stores print), with "extended warranty" shown as the page title and as a search/synonym. The card benefit is always **"Card extended warranty"**, never plain "Extended warranty". Item-page labels: "Protection plan · Geek Squad", "Card extended warranty · Chase Freedom". Update the claim wizard, reminders and notifications to match.
- **Where it lives.** Extended warranties stays under Account (users open it a few times a year, S3.4.2) **and** gets a **Home tile** ("Protection plans: 3 running, 1 ends within 60 days", linking to the page) once the person holds one, hidden otherwise.
- **My Items filter** gains "Has a protection plan" and "Plan ending soon"; a small plan icon on item rows.
- **From an item to its plan and back.** The plan row on the item page links to the plan (S3.4.2); the plan page lists covered items with links to each. A single-item plan opens as the plan page too, not a dead end.
- **One list.** `/plans` shows every protection plan, including single-item ones bought with an item (it does today for plans made by S3.4.3; confirm after S3.4.10). Plan-less extended coverages appear in it as "Needs details".
- **Empty state** explains the difference in two lines (one item vs many) and gives S3.4.10's four ways in.
- **Card benefit contrast.** A short "What is the difference?" disclosure on the page: a plan is bought; a card benefit comes with the card and is added automatically when the card is confirmed (F3.3).
- Search finds plans by company, name, contract number.
