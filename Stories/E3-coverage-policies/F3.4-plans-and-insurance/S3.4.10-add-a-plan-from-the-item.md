# S3.4.10 "I have a protection plan": add one from the item page

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S3.4.3 (plan bought with an item); S2.4.14 (warranty document belongs to an item); S3.1.1 (coverage)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03: "How do I add one?"

As a **user** who elected an extended warranty or protection plan on something I own, I want to say so on that item and be walked through it, so the plan lands on the item without my knowing which page or form is the right one.

## Why this matters
Today there are three ways in and none says it is the answer:
- **Add coverage > "Extended plan"** on the item page: a bare coverage form (provider, dates). It makes a coverage with no plan record, so no contract number, price, fee, claim contacts, renewal reminder or files.
- **Account > Extended warranties > Add a plan**: right for a plan covering many items. The empty page says a single-item plan "is added on that item instead", but the item page never says how.
- **A receipt line** (S3.4.3): the only automatic path, and FS-41 found it drops the plan when the item is added with Add Details.

A person holding a Geek Squad card in one hand and a TV page in the other has nowhere to start.

## Acceptance criteria
- The item page (view mode) has an **Add a protection plan** button in the coverage section, beside Add coverage. It is also the first suggestion in an item's empty-coverage state when the item's category usually has plans (TVs, laptops, phones, appliances).
- It opens one sheet, **How did you get the plan?**, with these choices, each a sentence long:
  1. **I have the paperwork or a photo** (contract, receipt line, card, confirmation email): upload or scan; read as in S3.4.2 / S2.4.14; review before saving.
  2. **I'll type it in**: the plan form (S3.4.2) with the company, the item and its purchase date already filled.
  3. **It covers more than this item** (home warranty, a plan for many things): goes to the plan page with this item pre-ticked.
  4. **It came free with my card or store**: that is a benefit, not a plan; shows the card or store coverage already proposed (S3.1.2) and how to add the card.
- Whatever path is used creates the same thing: a `WarrantyPlan`, a `PlanItem` for this item and an extended-plan coverage written from the plan (S3.4.3: no separate code path).
- **Add coverage > Extended plan** is removed from the generic form, or opens this sheet. The item page never creates a plan-less extended coverage. Existing plan-less extended coverages stay and show "Add the plan's details" that upgrades them to a plan in place.
- The sheet asks only what the person cannot skip: company, name or length, and the date it started or will start. Price, contract number, fee and claim contacts are optional on the first pass and offered afterward as "Add the rest" (a to-do, S4.6.1, dismissible).
- The saved result is visible at once: toast "Geek Squad plan added to your TV", and the item shows the plan beside the maker's warranty.
- The company is matched to a known warranty provider, store or maker (the roles that sell plans, S3.4.5); a company not on the list is kept as private and marked "not verified".
- The empty state on Extended warranties names the same four ways and links straight to each.
- Works on a phone with the camera first (S2.2.11).

## The receipt missed it (Steve, 2026-11-03)
A new phone's receipt was uploaded; the reader did not see the insurance bought with it. The person must be able to add it right on the item without any set-up.
- The item page has an **Insurance / extended warranty** section (the same button, opened inline on the page, not a trip to another screen or an admin-like company form).
- **Company is just a typed name.** A box with suggestions from known providers as they type; anything typed is accepted as-is. No "add a company" step, no role, no website, no claim details required. The app keeps it as a private, "not verified" company behind the scenes and matches it to a known one later if it can (S3.4.3, S6.1.1).
- The smallest save is: **who** (typed), **what** (insurance or extended warranty, a drop-down defaulting from the item's category: phone gets insurance), **price and how paid** (monthly, yearly, once; optional), **starts** (default today or the purchase date, asked), **how long** (optional; blank = until cancelled). Everything else comes later through "Add the rest".
- If the company name matches one the person typed before, it is reused, not duplicated.
- A phone, tablet or laptop item also offers the device fields of S3.4.19 (IMEI or serial, deductibles) in "Add the rest".

**Checked against build 982952c (2026-11-03): what already exists.** The item page's **Add coverage** button (`item-detail.page.ts`, drawer) offers kind *Extended plan*, a "Who provides it" picker whose last row **Add "name"** creates a private, unverified company inline (`party-picker.component.ts`, no setup screen), starts, ends, length in months, terms, claims link and phone. So typing a new company already works. What is missing:
- It is called "Extended plan", not insurance or protection plan, and a phone item gets no hint.
- No price or billing (monthly, yearly, once), no contract number, no IMEI or deductibles: nothing for a cost view, renewal reminder or claim help to use.
- It makes a plan-less coverage: not in Extended warranties, no plan page, no files, no plan-level reminder, no "Add the rest".
- The button is "Add coverage" among generic kinds, not an obvious "Insurance / extended warranty" entry; the receipt's missed plan gives no prompt.
Build: relabel and reshape this drawer into the sheet above (insurance / protection plan first, price and billing added, saves a plan record), rather than a new screen.

## Not in scope
- Recommending that the person buy a plan, or saying one is worth it.
- Insurance policies (S3.4.1).
