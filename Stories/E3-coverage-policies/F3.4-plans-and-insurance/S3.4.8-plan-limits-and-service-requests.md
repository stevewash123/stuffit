# S3.4.8 Plan limits, service fees and asking for service

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans, "Make a claim" not yet built); S3.4.7 (covered lines); S5.2 (claim log)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-10-27, from the 2-10 sample contract.

As a **user** with a home warranty, I want to see what each covered thing is worth under the plan and what asking for service will cost me, and keep a record of each request, so I know before I call whether it is worth it and I can show what happened afterwards.

## Why this matters
A home warranty pays up to a limit per covered item ($5,000 for heating and air, $2,000 per appliance, $1,000 for access through concrete, $10 per pound of refrigerant) after a service fee that varies ($0 to $350) and with exclusions that decide whether a breakdown is covered at all. The existing plan record has one service fee and free-text "what it covers". The decision the user needs to make at the moment of breakdown cannot be made from that.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Limits per line.** Each covered line (S3.4.7) can carry a limit (an amount, per item or per category), special limits as notes, and exclusions. The plan reader (S3.4.2) fills them from the covered-item table of the agreement, each with its quote and page. A limit that depends on type (the $1,500 limit for hot-water heating against $5,000 for others) is a note on the line, not a guess.
- **Service fee** is a number or a range ($0–$350), with the note of what sets it (plan, trade, or visit). The claim help shows it as a range when it is one.
- **Request service** from a covered line or item: shows the plan's phone, website or "request online 24/7" link, the contract number, the waiting period state (S3.4.6), the limit, the service fee and the exclusions, so the person has what the company asks for. It then records the request.
- **A service request** is a record on the plan: date, line or item, what broke, who was sent (a name, phone), the fee paid, the company's decision (covered, denied, partial), what they paid or replaced, and any follow-up. It joins the same claim log as other claims (F5.2), marked as a plan request, so the history of one appliance reads in one place.
- **Count and remaining limit.** The plan page shows requests per year and, where a limit is per-item per-term, how much of it a line has used.
- **Denied or disputed.** A denied request keeps the reason in the company's words and the contract text it points to, and offers the claim wizard (S5.4.12) later.
- The yearly-cost view (S3.4.2) adds service fees paid, so "what the plan cost me" is price plus fees, beside "what it paid out".
- A request's evidence (photos, the company's email, invoices) attaches to it like other documents (S2.1.2).

## Open questions
- Whether service fee paid is tracked as part of the request or as an expense record of its own.
- How much of the agreement's "what is not covered" to turn into per-line structured exclusions versus keeping the original wording one tap away (S3.4.2).
