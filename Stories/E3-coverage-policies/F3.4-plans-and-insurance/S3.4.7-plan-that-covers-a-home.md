# S3.4.7 A plan that covers a home and its systems

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M9 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S3.4.5 (shared plan types); S3.4.1 (insurance policy: shares the home)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-10-27, from the 2-10 sample contract.

As a **homeowner**, I want a home warranty to cover things that are part of my house (the heating system, the plumbing, the electrical) as well as appliances I own, so the plan shows what it covers without my having to list items that were never purchases.

## Why this matters
S3.4.2 has a plan cover items the person already has, picked from their items. A home warranty covers a home: the contract names a covered address and home type, then lists systems (heating, air conditioning, ductwork, electrical, plumbing, garage door openers) and appliances (washer, dryer, dishwasher, range, refrigerator) plus add-ons (roof leak repair, well pump, septic, pool equipment). Most of the systems come with the house and have no receipt. A person can own the plan and have no item to attach it to.

## Acceptance criteria
- A plan can name a **covered home** (address, type, any listed square-footage limits) instead of or as well as items. The same home is the one the insurance policy (S3.4.1) uses, entered once.
- A plan lists **covered systems and appliances as lines of its own**, each with its category, its limit (S3.4.8), what is excluded, and a link to an owned item when there is one. A line with no item still counts: the plan page shows "Heating system: covered up to $5,000" and the line shows in the claim help.
- An owned item the plan covers (a dishwasher bought with a receipt) is matched to a line by category and offered "This plan covers it", never attached silently. The item page then shows the plan with its limit and waiting period (S3.4.6).
- A plan type (S3.4.5) fills the lines in one step; the person unticks what their contract leaves out (the Summary page lists the actual covered items, which may be fewer than the plan type).
- **Additional coverages** (roof leak, well pump, septic, pool) are lines, ticked only when the contract's summary lists them.
- Items the person adds later that fit a covered line are offered the plan (S3.4.2: always asked).
- The yearly-cost view (S3.4.2) counts a home plan once, not once per line.

## Open questions
- Whether the home becomes its own record (with systems, ages and notes) now or stays a few fields on the plan until a later "my home" feature. This story leans toward a few fields on the plan.
- A plan that covers a rental property, a second home or several homes.
