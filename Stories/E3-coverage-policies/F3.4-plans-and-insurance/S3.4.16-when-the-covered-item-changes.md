# S3.4.16 When the covered item is returned, replaced, sold or dies

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S2.4.13 (plan refund); S5.4.20 (mark returned); S3.4.15 (cancel a plan); S3.4.8 (service requests)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03.

As a **user** whose item has a plan, I want the app to ask what happens to the plan when the item changes, so I do not keep a plan on a thing I returned, or lose a plan that carried over to the replacement.

## Why this matters
Plans often replace a broken item with a new one and keep running on the replacement. Other plans end with the item. Today deleting an item silently drops it from the plan (S3.4.2), and Mark returned is silent about a plan bought with the item (S5.4.20 lists "ending a protection plan returned with its item" as not built).

## Acceptance criteria
- **Mark returned** on an item with a plan asks: *Did you return the plan too?* **Yes, refunded** (records the refund, S3.4.15; ends the plan on the return date), **Yes, no refund**, **No, I'm keeping it** (the plan stays and is flagged "covers an item you returned": a to-do to cancel or move it), **Not sure** (to-do). A plan bought with the item defaults to "Yes, refunded" when the plan type says it is refunded with the item (S3.4.5).
- **Replaced under the plan.** A *Replaced by the plan* action on a covered item (from a service request, S3.4.8, or the item page): creates the replacement item (brand, model, serial editable, date today), and asks whether the plan **continues on the replacement** (default, with the plan's own dates unless the person changes them), **ends**, or **restarts** (new term from the replacement date). The old item is marked replaced, not deleted; its history and files stay.
- **Sold, given away, thrown away, lost** (S9.2 item states): asks the same *what about the plan* question; offers cancel (S3.4.15) when the plan is not transferable (plan type or contract says), and a transferable plan can be left on the new owner (ends covering this item, plan continues for others).
- **Deleting an item** that has a plan asks before dropping it from the plan, naming the plan.
- A plan left covering no items is shown as "Covers nothing" with options *Add items* / *Cancel* / *Keep*.
- Nothing here changes a plan without an explicit answer, and every answer is undoable for the session.
