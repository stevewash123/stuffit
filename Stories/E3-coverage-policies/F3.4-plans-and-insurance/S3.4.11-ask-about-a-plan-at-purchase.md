# S3.4.11 Ask "did you buy additional protection, an extended warranty or insurance?" when an item is added

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.3 (plan bought with an item); S2.4.7 (plan lines on a receipt); S3.4.10 (add from the item page)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03; UAT FS-41 (pass 2) steps 3 and 4.

As a **user** adding a purchase, I want to be asked once whether a protection plan came with it, and I want a plan the receipt showed to survive the Add, so the plan is not lost at the moment it is easiest to record.

## Why this matters
Plans are bought at the register, in the last second, and are the thing most often forgotten. UAT pass 2 (build 982952c) found that the reader sees the Geek Squad plan on the receipt and shows it under the item, but **Add Details has no plan section** and **the full Add drops the plan**; Save with nothing ticked now does nothing and says nothing. S3.4.3 specified all of this; none of the Add Details half is built.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Receipt showed a plan (S2.4.7):** Add Details lists it in a "Protection plan" section, ticked when the reader was sure, with company, name, term, price, contract number and start editable (S3.4.3). What is ticked on Save becomes a plan on the item. Nothing is dropped silently: an untouched plan is either created or left as a to-do, "Add your Geek Squad plan" (S3.4.3).
- **Receipt showed no plan, store sells them:** for a store known to sell plans (S3.4.5) and a category it usually covers, Add Details and the by-hand Add purchase form show one unobtrusive line: **"Did you buy additional protection, an extended warranty, insurance or something similar with this?"** with Yes / No / Not sure. Yes opens S3.4.10's sheet. No is remembered for that item and never asked again. Not sure creates a to-do "Check for additional protection, extended warranty or insurance" due in 14 days, dismissible.
- **Quick add** never asks (S3.4.3 rules stand); an unmatched plan becomes the to-do.
- **Save with nothing ticked** says so ("Tick at least one coverage, or choose Add without coverage") instead of doing nothing.
- A plan whose company is not in our list shows "not verified" (FS-41 step 7); a plan with no company shows a prompt to pick one.
- What the plan says it covers (a model number or product name; a store SKU alone is not shown or counted, decided 2026-11-03) is checked against the item; a mismatch is flagged, not attached (FS-41 step 2, 8).
- The question is never shown for services, subscriptions, or items below the tracking minimum (S9.8.5) unless a plan is on the receipt.

## Status
Receipt plans on Add Details, the covers check and "not verified" built 2026-11-03 (push 4). The question itself (Yes / No / Not sure) is being built; the Add Details tick is kept per browser.
