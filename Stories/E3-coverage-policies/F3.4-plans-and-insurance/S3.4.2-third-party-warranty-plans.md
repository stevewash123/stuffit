# S3.4.2 Plans from third-party warranty companies

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.1.1 (Add or edit a coverage); S4.1.1 (Generate reminders from coverage dates); S2.1.2 (Attach receipts, manuals, photos); S2.4.1 (Document reader)  

As a **user** who bought a home protection plan, or a plan from a warranty company that covers many items, I want one page to add and manage it, so every item it covers shows that protection, and I hear about renewal before it lapses.

## Why this matters
A plan bought at checkout covers one item and already fits a coverage on that item (kind: extended plan). Plans sold by warranty companies are different: one contract covers many things (the major appliances in a home, or most of what a household owns), with its own term, renewal, fee per service call and claim process. Entering it once per item would be tedious and would drift out of date. The plan is the record; the items point at it.

## Acceptance criteria
- **A "Plans and insurance" page** lists everything the user holds that spans items: these plans and the insurance policies of S3.4.1, with status (active, ending soon, ended), end or renewal date, and how many items each covers. Add, open, edit and end from here.
- **Add a plan.** Company (a warranty provider, picked from parties or added), plan name, contract number, start and end dates, how it renews (auto-renews or not), price and billing (monthly, yearly or one payment), service fee per claim, what it covers in the user's own words, exclusions or limits the user notes, claim phone, claim website, and the contract document.
- **Start from the document.** "Add a plan" opens with "Upload the contract or terms of service" (PDF, photo or several pages; the phone scan and share paths of S2.2.11 work here too). Reading it fills in what it can: company, plan name, contract number, start and end dates or term, renewal terms, price and billing, service fee per claim, claim phone and website, what is covered, exclusions and limits, and any action the contract asks for (such as "register within 30 days", which becomes a suggested reminder, S4.6.1). The user can skip the upload and type everything.
  - **Match the company.** The company named in the document is matched to a known warranty provider (for example American Home Shield, or any other company already in the shared list); if there is no match it is offered as a new one, with the role "warranty provider". A match brings in what we already know about that company (website, claim phone and claim link) for any field the document left blank, marked as coming from the company's record, not from the contract. The same goes for insurers in S3.4.1.
  - **Whatever the document is.** A welcome letter, a contract, terms and conditions or an order confirmation all work; each field is filled from whichever document states it, and several uploads for one plan add to each other (the contract for the terms, the invoice for the price and dates).
  - **Review before it counts.** Every field read from the document is shown as read from it, with the quote and page it came from, for the user to confirm or fix. A field the document did not make clear stays blank; nothing is guessed. Nothing is saved until the user accepts the form. Confidence and evidence are kept as for reference policies (S3.2.5).
  - **The document stays attached** to the plan as its contract. Uploading a renewal or a newer version later offers to update the plan, showing what changed.
  - **Coverage text is summarised, not replaced.** "What it covers" and "exclusions" get a short plain-words summary with the original wording one tap away, so the user is not relying on a summary alone when it matters.
  - Uses the same document-reading service as receipts (S2.2.2) and its per-account cost cap (S7.6.5); over the cap, the plan form opens blank with the document attached.
- **Choose what it covers.** Pick items from the user's items (search, or "everything in a category"). Each covered item then shows this plan on its item page as an extended-plan coverage with the plan's dates and claim contacts (S3.1.1), marked as coming from the plan.
- **Edit once.** Changing the plan's end date, claim phone or renewal updates every covered item. Ending the plan ends all of them on that date. Removing one item from the plan leaves the others.
- **Add items later.** When the user adds a new item, an active plan that lists the item's category (or "everything") offers to cover it, and the user confirms; it is never added silently.
- **Renewal reminders** through the reminder system: at the plan level (one reminder, not one per item), with the plan's lead time (default 30 days). A plan that auto-renews also reminds the user a few days ahead, because that is when people cancel or compare.
- **Cost is visible.** The page shows what the plans cost per year in total, next to the count of covered items and what those items cost. A "is it worth it" verdict is out of scope.
- **Claim help.** From a covered item, "Make a claim" shows the plan's phone and website first, then the contract number and service fee, so the user has what the company will ask for. Later this feeds the refund and claim wizard (S5.4.12).
- A plan is private to the account. The company is a shared party, so a company's known claim details can help other users later (E6).

## Data
`warranty_plans` (account-owned): company party id, name, contract number, start, end, auto-renew, price, billing period, service fee, coverage notes, exclusions, claim contacts, status. `plan_items` (plan id, item id). The coverage on each item is a normal coverage of kind `ExtendedPlan` with a `PlanId` that points back; its dates and contacts are written from the plan whenever the plan changes. Documents: add owner type `WarrantyPlan`.

## Open questions
- Whether the Plans and insurance page is a top-level tab or sits under Settings. Users open it a few times a year, so Settings is likely enough.
- Whether a plan that covers "everything" should attach to every item automatically, or always ask. This story leans to always ask.

## Status

Built 2026-10-03 without document reading (API and UI; migration WarrantyPlans applied to dev and test; not browser-checked). Account > Extended warranties (`/plans`) lists plans with status (active, ending soon, ended), company, item count, yearly cost, and totals: what the running plans cost per year next to what the covered items cost. A plan (`/plans/new`, `/plans/:id`) has company, name, contract number, term, auto-renew, price and billing (a year, a month or one payment), fee per service call, claim phone and website, what it covers and exclusions, covered items, contract files, and End this plan (a date) and Delete. Cover items from a searchable list; each gets an extended-plan coverage written from the plan (`coverages.plan_id`), and editing the plan rewrites them all and re-syncs reminders. Removing an item removes only its coverage; ending sets every end date; deleting the plan removes its coverages and files; deleting an item drops it from the plan. The export has `warranty_plans.csv` and the contracts under `files/plans`. Yearly cost: monthly times 12, yearly as is, a one-off price spread over the term (none without an end date). Tests: `PlanTests` (9), `plans.page.spec.ts`, `plan.page.spec.ts`.
"Start from the document" built 2026-10-05 (UI only: the reader endpoint of S2.4.1 already existed; tests pass; not browser-checked, and nothing has run against the real Anthropic API, so only the fake provider has been exercised). On a new plan (`/plans/new`) a "Start from your contract" box takes the contract, terms, welcome letter or invoice (several files read as one document) as kind `warranty_contract`. The shared review panel shows each value with its quote, page and confidence; clear ones start ticked; nothing is filled in until the person presses the button and nothing is saved until they save the plan (`plan-reading.ts`, unit tested). Mapping: company (matched to a known warranty provider by exact name, otherwise a note to pick or add it), plan name, contract number, dates, auto-renew, price and billing (a month, a year or one payment), fee per service call, claim phone and website. When the contract gives a term and no end date, the end date is counted from the start date and the page says so. "What it covers" gets the coverage text, waiting period, claim steps and claim time limit; "Exclusions and limits" gets the limits, exclusions and pre-existing conditions, each labelled in the contract's own words. Renewal terms, cancellation terms, transferability and an action the contract asks for (with its deadline) have no field, so they appear as notes after reading; the action note says to add a reminder. A matched company's known claim phone and website fill only fields the contract left blank, and the page says they came from the company's record. The files read are kept with the plan as its contract when it is saved (ticked by default). Update from a newer document built the same day: editing an existing plan shows "Update from a newer document" (same read and review), and after the ticked values are applied the page lists what is different, one line each, as "Ends: Jan 1, 2099 → Jan 1, 2100" (or says nothing in the document differs). Nothing changes until the plan is saved; saving updates every covered item as any edit does, and the new document is added to the plan beside the old one (kept unless unticked). The company is never swapped from a document: if it names another company the page says the company was not changed. Not done from this part of the story: a short plain-words summary of the coverage text with the original one tap away, several uploads that add to each other across separate sessions, turning the required action into a suggested reminder automatically (S4.6.1). (The same company-record fill for insurers is done under S3.4.1.)
Not done: one reminder per plan instead of one per item, an early reminder for auto-renewing plans, offering a plan when a new item is added, "Make a claim" from a covered item, everything-in-a-category cover, and S3.4.1 insurance, which will share the Plans and insurance page.

Update 2026-10-03: a plan with an end date now gets one renewal reminder (a general reminder, plan level, 30 days ahead; "renews on ... cancel or compare" for an auto-renewing plan, "ends on ... renew or replace it" otherwise), kept by `RenewalReminders` and following the end date. This covers the plan-level reminder and the auto-renew nudge listed under Not done above; the per-item coverage reminders still exist as well.
