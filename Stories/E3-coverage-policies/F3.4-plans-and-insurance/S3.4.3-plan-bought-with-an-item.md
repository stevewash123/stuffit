# S3.4.3 Add the plan that was bought with an item

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S2.4.7 (the receipt's plan is read); S9.9.3 (accept into My Items)  

As a **user**, I want the plan on my receipt to become a plan on my item when I add the item, with its end date in my reminders, so I do not have to enter it again and I hear before it ends.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Quick add** creates the plan and attaches it to the new item only when all of these hold: the company is matched or kept, the plan has a name, a term (or end date) and a price, and the reader was sure of it (the same bar as the coverages a quick add attaches). Otherwise nothing is created, and the item is added as before.
- **A plan that quick add could not make is not lost.** The item gets a suggested to-do ("Add your Geek Squad plan: check its term") that opens the plan form prefilled with what was read (S3.4.2). Dismissing it is allowed.
- **Add Details** shows the plans found as a section on the form, ticked by default when the reader was sure, each editable (company, name, term, price, contract number, start). What is ticked when the form is saved is created and attached.
- **Dates.** The start is what the document says: "on delivery" starts on the delivery date (while that is only an estimate, such as "Oct 6 - Oct 8", the earliest day is used and the plan is marked as estimated until the delivery is known), "on purchase" or nothing stated starts on the purchase date, a printed date is used as printed. The end is the start plus the term. The person can change both; a plan with no known end is allowed (S3.4.2) but a quick add never makes one.
- **What gets created** is the same as adding a plan by hand: a `WarrantyPlan`, a `PlanItem` for the item, an extended-plan coverage on the item written from the plan, and the reminders. No separate code path for plans from receipts.
- **Nothing is made twice.** Repeating the accept (a retry, a second tap) finds the plan already there and creates nothing more. A plan made from a receipt is private to the account.
- **The receipt stays attached** to the plan as well as the item, as its proof of purchase.
- **The result is visible.** The toast says what was added ("Samsung TV and its Geek Squad plan added to My Items"); the item page shows the plan beside the maker's warranty, each with its own end and claim contact; Extended warranties (`/plans`) lists it.
- A plan whose company is private to the account (unlisted) shows a small "not verified" mark until the company is recognised.

## Code changes (planned)
- **Reader.** Add the plan fields to the `document` kind in `document-kinds.json`; bump its prompt version; tell the items field to leave plan lines out. Extend `ReadingChecks` with the price rule.
- **Pending item.** A new account-owned table `pending_item_plans` (pending item id, name, company text, company party id, price, term months, end date, contract number, confidence, for-item text), like `PendingItemPolicy`. One migration. `PendingItemView` carries the list.
- **Reading.** In `ApplyReadingAsync` / `FillFromReadingAsync`, write the plan rows (replace, not append, so a re-read does not duplicate), match the company through a new `PartyMatcher.FindPlanCompanyAsync`, and create an unlisted company the way `RememberNewBrandAsync` creates a brand: before the item is touched, because saving shared data changes the item's row version.
- **Accept.** In `AcceptOnceAsync` and `CompleteOnceAsync`, after the item exists, call `PlanService.CreateAsync` and `AddItemsAsync` for each plan that qualifies. Both already run in one transaction, and a repeat is already answered early when the pending item is accepted. `CompleteAsync` also takes the ids the person ticked.
- **Not changed:** `WarrantyPlan`, `PlanItem`, coverage sync and reminders. They are reused as they are.
- **UI.** New Items row line and edit; the Add Details section; the quick add toast; the to-do link. The API's `PendingItem` shape gains `plans`.
- **Tests.** Unit: the reading fields, checks, term to end date. Integration (fake provider): a receipt with a TV and a plan gives one item and one plan row, the name has no plan in it; quick add creates `WarrantyPlan`, `PlanItem`, coverage and reminder; a plan missing its term leaves a suggested to-do instead; repeating the accept makes one plan; an unlisted company is kept for the account. UI specs for the row and the form.

## Open questions
- A plan that says it starts when the maker's warranty ends: the start is then the maker's warranty end, if we know it; otherwise the person is asked.
- When delivery is only an estimate, whether to re-date the plan when the delivery arrives (the pending item already tracks an estimated delivery). Leaning yes, with the person told.
- Whether to show the plan on the New Item at all when the reader was not sure of it, or only when it is sure. This story shows it flagged and never quick adds it.
