# S3.4.19 Phone and device insurance: monthly, per claim, and it follows the phone

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S3.4.10 (add from the item); S3.4.6 (monthly, recurring); S3.4.8 (service fee, requests); S3.4.16 (item replaced)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-03: "phone insurance is a great example."

As a **user** who pays monthly for phone insurance (carrier, AppleCare+, Asurion, a card or bank add-on), I want the plan recorded against my current phone, with its deductible and claim limits, so I know what a cracked screen or a lost phone will cost before I call, and the plan moves when I upgrade.

## Why this matters
Phone insurance is the commonest plan most people hold and fits none of the plan assumptions:
- **No receipt.** It is a line on the carrier bill or an app setting; the phone was bought on a different day, often at a different place.
- **Monthly, open-ended** (S3.4.6), cancelled when the phone is traded in.
- **Deductible per claim that depends on the kind of loss** (screen $29, other damage $99, loss or theft $199), a **cap on claims per 12 months** (two or three), and replacement may be a refurbished phone.
- **Follows the device, not the purchase.** Upgrade to a new phone and the plan moves, or ends and a new one starts. Identifiers (IMEI, serial, phone number) matter; a receipt does not.
- The same pattern covers tablets, laptops, watches, earbuds, cameras.

## Acceptance criteria
- Add a protection plan (S3.4.10) on a phone offers **Phone or device insurance** as a starting point: asks for the provider (carrier, Apple, Asurion, card, other), monthly price, start date, and the device identifier (IMEI or serial, optional but recommended). Bill-based plans accept a photo or PDF of the bill line or the enrolment email as the document.
- **Deductibles by kind of loss** are recorded as a small table on the plan (screen, other damage, loss, theft, other) with the amounts, and shown together on Make a claim (S3.4.8). A range is allowed ("$29-$199") when only a summary is known.
- **Claim cap.** The plan can state "N claims per 12 months"; requests (S3.4.8) count toward it and the plan page shows "1 of 2 used; resets <date>". A request that would exceed the cap is flagged, not blocked.
- **Replacement type** noted (new, refurbished, same model or equivalent) and the time stated ("typically next business day"), as quoted from the document.
- **Upgrade flow.** Adding a new phone, or marking the old one traded in (S3.4.16), asks: *Move the plan to the new phone* (updates device identifier, keeps monthly billing, records the date), *Cancel it* (S3.4.15), or *Not sure* (to-do). The old phone keeps its history.
- **Waiting period** (S3.4.6) is honoured: many carriers cover from day 1, some after a short wait; both display correctly.
- Claim help shows the provider's claim phone/site, the contract or account number, the IMEI, and the deductible for the loss type the person chooses.
- Cost view: monthly price x 12 plus deductibles paid (S3.4.8), beside what the phone cost. No worth-it verdict.
- A phone with both manufacturer warranty (1 year), a card extension and insurance shows the three as distinct layers (S3.4.13): the warranty covers defects, the card extends warranty, the insurance covers accidents and loss. The explanation in the item page names the difference in plain words, quoted from each document, without advice.
- Works from the phone itself: scan or share-to-StuffIt of the carrier's confirmation (S2.2.11).
