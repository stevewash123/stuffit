# S3.4.6 Waiting period, monthly billing and open-ended renewal

**Feature:** F3.4 Plans and insurance you hold  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S3.4.2 (plans); S4.1.1 (generate reminders from coverage dates)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-10-27, from the 2-10 sample contract.

As a **user** with a home warranty, I want WarrantyCat to know when coverage actually starts and that the plan keeps renewing until I cancel, so I am not told I am covered during the waiting period and I am reminded before another year is billed.

## Why this matters
The plan form (S3.4.2) has start and end dates. A home warranty contract adds three things those cannot say:
- **Initial coverage waiting period.** The sample contract: effective January 1, 2025, with a 30-day waiting period for initial plans and none for renewals. A breakdown on January 10 is not covered. Showing the plan as active from January 1 would be wrong at exactly the moment it matters.
- **Recurring until cancelled.** The term is 12 months, the renewal "annually or monthly, recurring until cancelled". There is no end date; there is a renewal date, and the plan keeps going.
- **Monthly payments on a yearly term.** Price and billing are already recorded; the yearly cost shown next to it must count twelve payments, not one.

## Acceptance criteria
- A plan has an optional **waiting period** (days, or a covering-starts date) and a flag for initial versus renewal. The plan and its covered items show "Covered from <date>" and "Waiting period until <date>" while it runs; status reads "waiting" for that time, not "active".
- A plan with the waiting period still running has its covered items marked the same way, and "Make a claim" (S3.4.2) says coverage has not started yet and when it does.
- A plan that renews until cancelled has a **next renewal date** and no end date. Its covered items follow that date: they show "renews <date>", not "ends <date>", and the renewal reminder (S3.4.2) fires ahead of the renewal date and repeats each cycle, with "cancel or compare" wording.
- Cancelling the plan sets the end date; the plan then ends like any other.
- The plan reader (S3.4.2) fills the waiting period, initial or renewal, term, renewal terms and billing frequency from the plan summary page, each with its quote and page.
- A bracketed or alternative value in a sample contract (`[Monthly] [One-Time]`, `[30 days]`) is read as not stated, not as a choice, and flagged so the person picks.

## Open questions
- Whether "waiting" is a status of its own or a note on an active plan. This story leans to its own status, because a covered-item list that says active during a waiting period is wrong.
