# S3.2.11 Say when something cannot be returned

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference data & versioned policies  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** next  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1; S3.2.5 (confidence and bands); S3.1.2 (coverage proposal)  

As a **user**, I want an item to say plainly when its store does not take returns on it, so I do not count on a return that is not there. As an **operator**, I want to record "no returns" for a whole store or for one kind of product, as the store states it.

## Why
A store's terms often exclude kinds of product (AJ Madison: air conditioners, accessories, special-order items, commercial laundry equipment). When a receipt email arrives for one of those, the item must show "not returnable", with where that comes from, not a return window that is wrong or one that has silently run out on the day of purchase.

## Acceptance criteria
- A seller policy can state **no returns** explicitly. It is a different thing from "no fixed window" (a satisfaction guarantee or conditions only) and from a 0-day window. A zero is never read as "no returns" by accident
- It can apply to a whole store (general) or to one category, like any other store policy, with its own source, quote, band and versions (S3.2.5)
- Conditions the store attaches stay on the policy ("special order", "installed or plugged in", "commercial use") and are shown with it
- When an item's category matches such a policy, the purchase shows **Not returnable at {store}**, with the reason, the band and the source, and no return window or return reminder is created for it
- When the kind of item is not known, or the exclusion depends on something a receipt does not show (special order, installed, plugged in), the item shows "{store} does not take returns on {kinds}; check yours" with the confidence of the match (S3.1.2 applicability), and the owner can tick that the exclusion applies or does not
- The store's other windows list (the one shown when the item type is unknown) includes "no returns" entries beside the day counts
- The owner can still record that the store accepted a return anyway; their own coverage wins for their item
- Admin store and company pages show "No returns" in place of a number, in the policy forms (checkbox, which clears the days and fee) and in the confirm step of the AI reading
- The AI reading of a store's saved pages can return "no returns" for a product kind (a carve-out, F6.9-adjacent), with the exact sentence as the quote, checked against the page as for other terms
- Reports and exports of return windows show "No returns", never 0

## Notes
- Needs a field on the seller policy and a migration; Render does not apply migrations on deploy, so apply it to the shared database by hand.
- The same idea may be wanted for a manufacturer (no warranty on a use or a region). Not part of this story.
- Related gap: carve-outs found on a page still arrive as one set of fields. Reading them as separate category rows with their own confirm is a separate story.

## Status
Built 2026-10-29 (API and UI, uncommitted): `seller_policies.no_returns` (migration SellerPolicyNoReturns), separate from `return_days` null (no fixed window) and 0 (a window that closes the same day). `ReferenceService.RequireSellerTerms` is the one rule: no returns excludes days and a fee, 0 days is a real window, a blank is not a policy. Applied in policy create/correct, the store confirm (admin stores page and Process saved info) and the page-saver confirm, where a window alone never switches it off and a missing window never switches it on. A coverage proposal for such a policy is `NotReturnable`: it counts as closed, so it is never ticked, auto-added or accepted (adding it by key is refused), and it reads "Not returnable at {store}" on New Items and the add-purchase form. The unknown-kind caution and the store's other-windows list say "no returns". Admin policy forms have a No returns checkbox that clears the days and fee. Checked the live data first: 168 seller policies, none with 0 days.
Tests: unit NoReturnsTests (5), integration WebDataTests No_returns_is_its_own_answer (confirm path), UI format spec. 
Carve-outs built 2026-10-29: the return-policy reading (prompt return-policy-page-v3) also lists, one per line, each kind of product the page gives no returns or its own window, as "what | term | sentence". Each line is kept only when its sentence is on the page and its term says plainly "no returns" or a number of days (never both, never a bare 0); `CarveOutParser` maps the words to one category (category words, then the words being part of a category's name) or leaves the choice to the operator. Process saved info shows them as rows under the general policy, each with its own category picker and Confirm, which writes a category-scoped store policy (`ConfirmReturnPolicy.CategoryId`) with official-source evidence and leaves the general policy alone. Conditions that are not a kind of product (installed, special order, damaged on arrival) stay in the warning text. Categories added: Room air conditioners, Commercial laundry, Accessories (imported to the shared database; keywords topped up). Tests: CarveOutParserTests (5), StoreReturnPolicyTests carve-out end to end.
Not done: the item page does not show "not returnable" for an item that already exists; the stores page (old confirm form) does not list carve-out rows, only Process saved info does.
Migration SellerPolicyNoReturns is applied to the shared and test databases.
