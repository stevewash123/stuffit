# S3.2.9 Grow the shared stores, brands and cards; research their terms in rounds

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference policies
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.2.3 (seed data), S3.2.5 (source and confidence), S3.2.7 (unverified)
**Status:** Round 1 done 2026-10-18 and imported to the dev database only (nothing in production reference data).

Shared data lives in text files in `reference-data/` and is loaded with `dotnet run --project src/StuffIt.Api -- --import-reference <file or folder>`, not in code. Existing rows are never edited in place (policies are versioned); an official policy needs `verifiedOn` and `sourceUrl`; component names are at most 80 characters (unit tests check the files).

## Entities (round 1, no terms)
About 310 names in `entities-*.json`: 98 US retailers (including appliance and electronics sellers), 75 appliance, electronics and tool brands, 25 cooking brands, 18 mattress brands, 80 brands known for marketing warranty lines, 15 warranty providers, and Goldman Sachs with 10 card products.

## Research pass: round 1
- `returns-round1.json`: 21 store return policies from the stores' own pages; only Harbor Freight is confirmed, the rest are marked unconfirmed. Kohl's (sources disagree) and Menards (in-store window not found) skipped.
- `manufacturers-appliances-round1.json`: 6 official policies read from the makers' documents: LG refrigerators and washers, Sub-Zero, Wolf, Cove, Fisher & Paykel.
