# S3.2.4 Verified policies for the most common cards

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference policies  
**Phase:** MVP (data, before launch)  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S3.2.3 (Seed initial reference data); S3.3.6 (Card catalog)  

As an **operator**, I want real, sourced purchase benefits for the top 50 or more cards, so most users see policies the moment they pick their card.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each of the 50+ most common cards has either its extended warranty, purchase protection and return protection terms with an issuer source URL and verified date, or a recorded fact that it has none (for example Wells Fargo Active Cash, Discover)
- Terms are read from the issuer's Guide to Benefits (or the network tier's guide), not from review sites
- "No purchase benefits" is stored as a fact, not as a missing row: a flag on `card_products` so the card page can say "This card has no purchase protection" instead of "No policies on file yet"
- Card names and issuers in the catalog are checked against the issuer's own list; renamed or discontinued cards are marked
- Rows are versioned and audited like other reference data (S7.1.1)

## Status
12 of the top 20 seeded in October 2026 (Chase Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, Freedom Unlimited, Flex, Rise, Amazon Prime Visa; Amex Gold, Platinum; Capital One Venture X; Citi Double Cash, Strata Premier, Costco). Of these, the Amex Platinum and the two Citi rows were read from search summaries of issuer pages and must be confirmed once against the page. Remaining cards, sources and findings: `docs/card-policy-worklist.md`.
