# S3.2.16 The category list learns from what you pick at each store

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference policies
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.2.15 (categories follow the store)
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-11-10

As a **person who shops at the same stores**, I want the categories I have used at a store to come first the next time, so the list fits me and not only the store's usual stock.

## Why
Store links (S3.2.15) can only be seeded for chains someone has thought of. A hardware shop nobody linked still has a pattern: someone who files three purchases there under Power tools wants Power tools on top. Their own history covers the long tail without anyone maintaining a mapping.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Ordering, top to bottom:** (1) categories this account has used at this store, most used first; (2) the store's linked categories (S3.2.15); (3) "Other categories…" with the rest. A category in both (1) and (2) appears once, in (1).
- **A store with no links but with history** shows the history first, then the full list (no "Other categories…" fold is needed when nothing is linked; the rest follows below a divider).
- **Counts come from saved items only,** per account: purchase seller + item category on real items. Nothing is recorded from a dropdown click that was never saved, and nothing is shared with other accounts. This is personal data and stays personal; it is never a shared claim and never feeds operator review.
- **Derived, not stored.** The history is computed from the account's existing items (a grouped query by seller and category), so there is no new table, nothing to go stale, and a deleted or re-categorised item corrects the order automatically.
- **A threshold avoids noise:** a category counts as "used here" after 2 saved items at that store (setting, default 2), so one stray pick does not rearrange the list.
- **Business accounts** use the active account's items only, as everywhere else.
- **Never hides anything.** Same guarantee as S3.2.15: the full list is always one step away and a saved value always shows.

## Out of scope
- Learning across accounts to suggest new store links to operators (possible later through the review queue, S6.1.1; counting is only a trigger, never evidence).
- Per-brand history.

## Tests (standing suite, deterministic)
- History query: counts by seller and category for the active account only; threshold respected; deleted items excluded.
- Ordering: history before links; de-duplication; no-links-with-history case.
