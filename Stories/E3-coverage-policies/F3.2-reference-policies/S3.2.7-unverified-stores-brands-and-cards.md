# S3.2.7 Stores, brands and cards a user adds show as Unverified until verified

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference data & versioned policies  
**Phase:** v2 (the badge is built; the verification queue and any reward are not)  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables); S3.2.2 (Policy dropdown, add new); S3.2.5 (Policy source and confidence); S3.3.6 (Find a card in the catalog)  
**Needs a migration:** the queue and any reward do; the badge does not.

As a **user**, I want to add a store, brand or card that StuffIt does not know yet and carry on, and to see plainly that it is not verified yet, so I know why nothing was suggested for it and that it is mine alone until it is confirmed to exist.

## Principle
"Verified" means StuffIt has confirmed the store, brand or card product exists. A party a user added belongs to that account only (it has an owner account); a seeded or promoted one is shared. So unverified is derived, not stored: private to one account means unverified. The disclaimer already says store names and model names a user enters may be added to the shared data, with no personal information in them.

## Built 2026-10-04 (no migration)
- An **Unverified** badge (small, amber, tooltip "Not in our shared list yet. Only you can see this store until it is verified.") on a store, brand or card the user added, wherever its name shows: the picker list and the chosen value, the parties page (it replaces "Mine"), the Item and purchase panel (brand, bought from, paid with) and the card list.
- A card is unverified when its bank is one the user added, or no product is named, or the product name is not in the shared card catalog (so "Chase Freedom", which is not a listed product, shows the badge even though Chase is real). Case is ignored.
- A short note under Receipts and files (on the item page) and Receipt and paperwork (on the add form) when something picked is unverified: "<names> is not in our shared list yet. Official paperwork helps us confirm it for everyone." It names what is unverified and promises nothing else.
- Policies under an unverified party stay weak (user-typed evidence is low), so they are not ticked by default (S3.1.2, S3.2.5).

## Not built yet (needs a migration, after the desktop merge)
- **Verification queue for operators:** each unverified party with the evidence collected (how many independent accounts added the same normalised name, any documents, a website check) and approve, merge into an existing party, or reject.
- **Promotion (claims, S6.1.4):** every upload also records the store, brand or alias name as a claim. At N distinct accounts (3 for testing) the claim is ready for an operator, who approves, merges into an existing party or rejects. Until then it is the uploader's private entry only. The user's private entry is merged into the shared one on approval and their data keeps working; the badge goes away. Anything that is not a plausible business (for example a person's name) goes to an operator and is never promoted automatically. The shared record shows only the name and website, never who added it.
- **An automatic first check** before an operator looks: does the website exist and match the name.

## Deferred: a reward for helping verify
The idea: official paperwork added for an unverified item that leads to its verification earns credit toward free months. Parked by the owner on 2026-10-04 ("some simple incentive maybe, or come back to this"). Notes for when it returns:
- Pay out only after verification, never on upload; reversible if the evidence proves false; capped per account per year; one credit per party.
- Open choices: how many independent accounts count as enough (suggested 3, each with a document); the size of a credit; whether only the first contributor earns it; whether user-typed policies on an already-verified store earn anything.
- Needs a credit ledger and the billing side. Until it exists the note says nothing about credit.
- A receipt shows a store exists; a warranty card or the store's policy page shows the terms. The wording should point at official paperwork.

## Acceptance criteria (built part)
- A store, brand or card that is not in the shared data shows the Unverified badge everywhere its name appears; a shared one never does.
- The note appears only when something on the item or form is unverified, and names it.
- Nothing is blocked: an unverified party can be used like any other.
