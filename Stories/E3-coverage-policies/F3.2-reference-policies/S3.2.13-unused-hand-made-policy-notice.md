# S3.2.13 A policy you made by hand that nothing uses: tell the person once

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference data & versioned policies  
**Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S3.2.12 (a typed maker warranty stays with the maker); S3.2.1 (versioned policy, retire); S3.2.5 (policy source and confidence); S4.5.4 (answer a notification from the notification); S4.6.3 (the reminder sweep's nudges)  
**Related:** S3.2.2 (policy dropdown, add new); S3.2.7 (own stores and brands)  
**Decided by:** Steve, 2026-11-06.

As a **user** who typed in a warranty or return policy by hand, I want WarrantyCat to tell me, once, when no item of mine uses it any more, so I can delete it or keep it, and never be asked about it again.

## Acceptance criteria
- **No question at replace time.** Nothing is asked when a document's warranty replaces a typed one or when an item or coverage is deleted. The existing reminder sweep finds the policy later.
- **Which policies.** The account's own policies (OwnerAccountId = the account), maker warranties and store return policies, that are open (not retired) and that the person made by hand: every piece of evidence for the policy is a person's own entry, Manual (typed on an item, S3.2.12) or UserTyped (entered in settings). A policy that a document, a card, a source or a community report also backs is never reported. A policy with no evidence is not reported.
- **In use** (the policy is left alone) when any item of the account that is not deleted (and whose purchase is not deleted) has either a coverage taken from it (SourcePolicyId), or a coverage that matches it by the same maker (or store), the same kind of product and the same length:
  - A maker's warranty: a maker coverage whose provider (or, with none, the item's maker) is the policy's maker, or the company that owns that maker's brand; the item's kind of product is the policy's kind or inside it (a general policy: any kind); the coverage's term in months, as stated or worked out from its exact dates, equals the policy's.
  - A store's return policy: a return coverage whose provider (or the purchase's seller) is the policy's store, the same kind-of-product rule, and the same number of days between its start and end. A store policy with no window (no returns) is in use while any item was bought there of that kind of product.
  - Vehicles and parts of one count as using (an item is an item): they are never a reason to say "unused". S3.2.12 does not keep a policy from a vehicle's coverage, so nothing more is needed.
  - S3.2.12 deliberately stores no SourcePolicyId on a typed coverage, so the typed item itself is found by the match above, not by a link.
- **Seven days.** A policy younger than 7 days is not reported, so typing one and then deleting the item does not nag at once.
- **One notification, at most, per policy.** It goes to the person who made the policy (the evidence's entered-by user) when they are still on the account, otherwise to the account's earliest member, at that person's own local send time like the other nudges. Any earlier notice about the policy, in any state (waiting, answered, or deleted by the person from their list), stops a new one. A second sweep makes none.
- **The notice** is in the Notifications page, type "Info needed" (purchase_needs_info), unread and waiting for an answer. Its words name WarrantyCat, the maker and the term: "WarrantyCat noticed that your own Whirlpool warranty (24 months) is not used by any item. Delete it? If you keep it, WarrantyCat will not ask again." (a store: "return policy (30 days)"). It links to the store or brand page.
- **Delete it** retires the policy with the existing retire (it ends yesterday, or its start if later), so it is no longer offered; coverages that already took its terms do not change; the notification is completed. **Keep it** stores the answer on the policy (UnusedNoticeDismissedAt) and cancels the notification; the policy is never mentioned again. Only the account's own policies can be answered.
- **Used again finishes it.** A notice still waiting when the policy is used again (a typed term, a document's, a purchase), retired, answered or gone is cancelled by the next sweep. The notice is not repeated if the policy goes unused again.
- **Private to the account.** Another account's items never count as use of mine, and its sweep never reports mine.

## Out of scope
- Prompting at the moment of replacing or deleting.
- Reporting a policy any document, source or community report also backs.
- A reminder to come back to a notice that was ignored: it stays in the list until answered or deleted.

## Notes
- The sweep is a step of ReminderSendJob (hourly; the existing run-reminders maintenance trigger runs it, with the simulated clock and an optional accountId): no new endpoint, job or secret. The run summary has a new count, UnusedPolicies.
- The notification is the memory for "once"; UnusedNoticeDismissedAt on the policy (seller and manufacturer policies) is the stored dismissal.
- Kind registration reuses purchase_needs_info with two reference types (unused-maker-policy, unused-seller-policy) and the quick answers delete-it and keep-it, as the card-benefits nudge does with not-now. No new catalog type, so no new preference row.
- The rules are pure (UnusedPolicyRules in Core); the sweep and the two answers (UnusedPolicyService) only read and write.
