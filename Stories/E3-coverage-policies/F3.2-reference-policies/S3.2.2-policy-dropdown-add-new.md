# S3.2.2 Pick a known manufacturer policy or add a new one

**Feature:** F3.2 Reference data & versioned policies  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M2  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables); S9.5.1 (Pick a party or create one inline); S9.6.1 (Add a policy for a party)  

As a **user**, I want to choose from known policies for this manufacturer and category, or add my own.

## Acceptance criteria
- Dropdown filtered by manufacturer + category
- Each option shows term and "as of" date
- Add-new is one short form: term, start rule, link
- User policy works immediately, private to the account
- Submission enters the candidate queue (E6)


## Status
Done (M2). Known policies are listed on the store or brand page with their as-of dates; a user adds their own private version. Candidate submission (S6.1.1) is not wired yet; arrives in M7.
