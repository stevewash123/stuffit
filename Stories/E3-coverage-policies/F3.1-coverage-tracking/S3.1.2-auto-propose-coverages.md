## Acceptance criteria
- Return coverage proposed from seller policy in effect on purchase date
- Manufacturer coverage proposed from manufacturer policy if known
- Card coverage proposed from the matched card's confirmed benefits
- Each proposal must be confirmed or edited; unknown shows a check-later reminder

## Built 2026-10-04: confidence = verified x applicability
- **Verified** is about the policy (it exists, is offered publicly, on stated terms): the S3.2.5 score, unchanged. **Applicability** is about this purchase and is computed when suggestions are built, never stored. **Confidence** is the two multiplied; a card benefit the user confirmed counts as verified (1).
- Doubts and their first-guess factors (tune against real data): general category not exact 0.80; our records start after the purchase date 0.70; purchase date approximate 0.85; window runs from delivery, delivery unknown 0.90; a store's usual window can differ for an item 0.97; warranty starts on registration 0.90; card extended warranty with no maker warranty to extend 0.60.
- A suggestion is ticked by default at 80% or more (a first guess to tune on real data): an official source alone (85%) with a clean fit passes; a general-category fallback, an approximate date or a weaker source does not. Hover the score for its two parts and the reasons.
- Built as well: checkbox and one "Add N" button, icon buttons, shorter notes, no "Start a return" or empty policies block until something is saved, blank rows hidden outside edit mode, and "Suggest coverage" in edit mode brings back what was set aside.
