# S3.1.11 A warranty that starts at installation or registration asks for its date

**Feature:** F3.1 Coverage tracking
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.1.1 (coverage); S3.1.2 (proposals); S3.2.1 (policy start rule); the reminder job (E4)
**Status:** Built 2026-10-18 (API migration `WarrantyStartPending`, UI). Committed and pushed 2026-10-19. Verified by tests only, not on a phone.

As a **user**, when I add something whose maker's warranty starts at installation or registration, I want to be told at once and asked for that date, because only I know it.

## Acceptance criteria (built)
- A policy start rule is `purchaseDate`, `registration` or `installation` (new). A proposed coverage from a registration or installation policy is counted from the purchase date and flagged `pendingStart`, with a note saying so.
- Adding that coverage (accepting the suggestion, Add Details, Quick Add) creates one system to-do, "Enter the start date of your {maker} warranty on {item}", due 14 days later, and sends the `warranty_start_needed` notification immediately (email and app) to the people on the item. It does not wait for a reminder date.
- The date can be entered from the notification (`enter-date` action), the to-do, or the item page (`POST /api/coverages/{id}/start`). Saving moves the warranty, its component parts (for example the sealed system) and any card extension that follows it, then finishes the to-do and notification.
- Editing the start date by hand in the coverage editor counts as entering it. The to-do cannot be marked Done without a date; Dismiss and Not applicable still work. Deleting the coverage removes the to-do.
- The existing reminder job nudges once more the day before the due date.
- Sub-Zero, Wolf and Cove (S3.2.9) use the installation rule.

## Not built
- Re-raising a dismissed start-date to-do once, later (recommended, needs a second timer).
- A `delivery` start rule (nothing in the data needs it yet).
- Backfill: coverages created before this do not ask for a date. A script could flag those whose policy starts at installation or registration.
