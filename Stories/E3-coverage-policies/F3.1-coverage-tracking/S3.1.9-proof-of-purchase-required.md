# S3.1.9 What proof of purchase a warranty asks for

**Feature:** F3.1 Coverage tracking
**Phase:** v2
**Depends on:** S3.1.1 (Add or edit a coverage); the warranty reader (S2.4.1); S3.1.8 (same family of stated-term fields)

As a **user**, I want to know what proof a warranty asks for, so I know whether the photo or email I have will do or whether I should keep the paper.

## Acceptance criteria
- A coverage records the proof it asks for: any proof of purchase, the original receipt, registration (or a registration record), a serial number or photo of it, a dealer stamp, or not stated
- More than one can apply (for example receipt and serial number)
- The warranty reader fills it from the document and leaves "not stated" rather than guessing; the person can correct it. Most documents will be "not stated" or plain "proof of purchase"
- "Original receipt" wording is kept, shown as stated by the document, and is never turned into "any proof"
- The item page shows it in plain words, for example "Asks for: proof of purchase. Nothing says the original paper is required." and, when the proof is the original receipt, "Asks for the original receipt. Keep the paper."
- The item page lists what StuffIt holds that could serve as proof: the receipt image, the original order email, the card statement line if linked
- Where only paper will do, a reminder to keep it (thermal paper fades in a year or two) can be sent once, with a photo as backup
- This describes what the document says, not what a company will accept in practice (see S5.2.2)

## Notes
- Samples: the `SampleData/warranties` set; `edge-registration-required.pdf` and `edge-photo-of-warranty-card.png` (not valid without dealer stamp) have stated proof requirements; most of the standard ones just say proof of purchase.
