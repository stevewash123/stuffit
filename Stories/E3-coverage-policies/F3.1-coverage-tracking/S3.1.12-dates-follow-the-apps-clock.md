# S3.1.12 Every date and "days left" comes from the app's clock

**Feature:** F3.1 Coverage tracking
**Phase:** MVP
**Depends on:** S3.1.7 (item page); T1 (simulated clock); S4.6.2 (to-dos)
**Status:** Open (UAT 2026-11-02, FS-24-9). Not built.

As a **user**, I want "days left", "Expired", "Due in N days" and the Start a return button to agree with each other, whatever the time of day or my time zone, so that I am never told a window is both open and closed.

## Acceptance criteria
- One source of "today" for every date calculation the person sees: the server's clock (which honours the simulated clock in testing) in the person's own time zone. The browser does not compute coverage or to-do dates from `new Date()`.
- The item page, the items list, the home page, /todos, /returns and notification times all agree for the same item at the same moment.
- When every coverage has ended: the badge, the "Next to end" line, the "N days left" chip, the Policies card and the Start a return button all say so (no "Expired" next to "30 days left", no "Covered 1 policy", no Start a return).
- A to-do whose due date has passed says "Overdue", not "Due in 1 day".
- A test sets the simulated clock past an item's window and checks the item page and /todos.

## Found in UAT
At a simulated Dec 3 (real date Nov 2) the items list correctly said Expired while the item page showed "Expired" beside "30 days left", "Covered 1 policy", and Start a return; /todos said "Due in 1 day" for a to-do due Nov 3. Notification times kept the real time. Real users would see the same mismatch at midnight and across time zones if the item page uses the browser's date.

## Notes
Likely a small shared helper (UI) fed by a server "today" in the person's time zone, replacing the `new Date()` calls in `item-detail.page.ts`, `returns-in-progress.page.ts`, `format.ts`, `home.component.ts`.
