# S3.1.1 Add or edit a coverage (snapshot of terms)

**Feature:** F3.1 Coverage tracking  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M3  
**Depends on:** S7.5.3 (DDL: purchases, items and coverage schema); S2.1.1 (Add a purchase with one or more items)  

As a **user**, I want to record a coverage with its own term, start and end dates.

## Acceptance criteria
- Kinds: return, manufacturer, store, extended_plan, card
- Coverage stores its own terms; later policy changes never alter it
- Records source: default, user or extracted, and user_edited flag
- Start rule supports purchase date or a later date (registration)


## Status
Done (M3): coverage stores its own snapshot (dates, terms, claim contacts, provider, source, user_edited). Reminders are generated in M4.
