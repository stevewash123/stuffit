# S1.4.3 Canceled accounts: 90 days, then purge

**Feature:** F1.4 Data export & account deletion  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.3; S1.2.6  

As a **user**, I want to know what happens to my data if I cancel.

## Acceptance criteria
- After cancellation the account stays read-only for 90 days
- Notices at 30 and 7 days before purging, by email and in the notification center
- Resubscribing within the window restores full access
- After 90 days all data and files are purged like a deletion (S1.4.2)
