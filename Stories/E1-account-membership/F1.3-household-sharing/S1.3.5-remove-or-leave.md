# S1.3.5 Remove a person or leave an account

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S1.3.4 (Invite a person to my account)  

As an **account holder**, I want to remove someone, and as a **member** I want to leave, with their things handled sensibly.

## Acceptance criteria
- Removing a member takes them off every item they follow
- Items they own are handed to the account holder, or to a chosen member, before they go; nothing is left without an owner
- A leaving member keeps their own login but loses access to the account's items
- Their past notifications stay with them; they are not shown to others
- The account holder cannot be removed, only transferred (decision for later)

## Notes
