# S1.3.2 Add and remove people on an item

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** MVP (API); the people control is S9.2.4  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S1.3.1 (Every item has an owner)  

As an **item's owner**, I want to add other people in my account to an item, so that they hear about its coverage too, and to take them off again.

## Acceptance criteria
- An item has people: one owner and any number of followers, each a user in the item's account. A person is on an item once
- Add a person: `POST /api/items/{id}/people`. Remove: `DELETE /api/items/{id}/people/{userId}`. Anyone on the item can add or remove followers; anyone in the account can add themselves; only a member of the account can be added (400 otherwise), and someone not on the item cannot add others (403)
- Change the owner: `PUT /api/items/{id}/owner`, by the owner only (403 otherwise). The previous owner stays on the item as a follower
- The owner cannot be removed (409 with a message to make someone else the owner first); the last person on an item cannot be removed
- A person can take themselves off an item they follow
- `GET /api/account/members` lists the people in my account (id, name, email) for the picker
- The item returns its people (user id, name, owner or follower)
- Deleting an item (or its purchase) removes its people; removing a person from the account removes them from every item (S1.3.5)
- Following is about who is told. Who can see an item is S1.3.7 (draft); until that is settled everyone in the account can see every item
- Every action is limited to the signed-in user's account

## Notes
Table: `item_person` (account_id, item_id, user_id, is_owner), unique on (item_id, user_id). Dad follows the car, the son follows the PlayStation.

## Status

Done (M4, API): `GET/POST /api/items/{id}/people`, `DELETE /api/items/{id}/people/{userId}`, `PUT /api/items/{id}/owner`, `GET /api/account/members`; the purchase returns each item people. Rules as above, incl. 403 for someone not on the item and a transaction for the owner hand-over. A second person can only be put in an account by hand until S1.3.4; tests do that directly. Tests: `ItemPeopleTests.cs`.
