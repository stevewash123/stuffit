# S1.3.7 Whose items I see, and private items

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** not scheduled  
**Status:** PARKED (low priority). Working answers below; nothing is built for it.  
**Depends on:** S1.3.2 (Add and remove people on an item)  

As a **household member**, I want to see my own things by default, look at other people's when I choose to, and keep some things private.

## Direction so far (Steve, 2026-10-01)
- The items list shows only my items by default
- I can switch to see other people's items on the same account
- An item can be marked Private

## Two different things, kept apart
- **A filter** is convenience. Everything is still reachable; the list just starts narrow (mine). Lives in the UI and a query parameter.
- **Private** is access control. A private item must stay hidden from the people it is hidden from everywhere, not only in the list. The server must enforce it in every place an item can leak: the items list, search, the dashboard and its counts, purchase pages, receipts and other documents, the people picker, notifications, exports, and any link or id that points at it. An item someone cannot see answers 404, as an item from another account already does.

## Working answers (Steve, 2026-10-01, a quick shot, then parked)
- It is a family account, so the people on it are assumed to have a connection. Little is expected to be private (a kid might hide an R-rated game). Do not over-build this
- Private items are visible to the people on them
- The account owner sees a COUNT of private items and must tick a box to actually see them. A deliberate act, not a default
- Followers cannot see a private item, so they cannot ask to be added: the item's owner adds them. Self-follow therefore applies to non-private items only
- Unease noted: a partner might want to track a warranty on something hidden from the other partner. Accepted as an edge case, not designed for
- Everything else (per item or per purchase, defaults, forwarded receipts, counts) is decided when this is picked up; keep it simple

## Original open questions
1. **Who can see a private item?** Only the owner? The owner and the people on it? The account holder as well? (The account holder pays; should that give any view?)
2. **What does "mine" mean?** Owner only, or everything I am on (owner or follower)? Suggest: everything I am on.
3. **Can a private item have followers?** If yes, private means "only the people on it". If no, private means "only me" and it is simpler.
4. **Whole purchases or single items?** A purchase can hold several items (a receipt for a gift and a gadget). Does private apply per item, per purchase, or per item with the purchase page hiding the rest?
5. **Receipts and documents** on a private item: same rule, so a receipt is private with its item. A receipt covering a private and a non-private item needs a rule.
6. **Reminders and notifications** for a private item go only to the people who can see it. Email text must not name the item to anyone else. (Already true: notices go to the people on the item.)
7. **Changing it:** who can mark an item private or public, and what happens to people who could see it a moment ago?
8. **Default for new items:** public to the account, or private until shared? A forwarded receipt (S1.3.6) could default to private to its sender.
9. **Counts and totals** on the dashboard: do they include items a viewer cannot see? (They must not leak them.)
10. **Is an account holder override ever needed**, for example a parent seeing a child's items? This is a policy decision, not only a technical one.

## Acceptance criteria
To be written once the questions above are answered.

Until this story is settled the code treats every item as visible to everyone in the account; that is safe today because an account has only one user.
