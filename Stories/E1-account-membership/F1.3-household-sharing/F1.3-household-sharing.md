## Design questions (Steve, 2026-10-01), to settle before any story

Settled in the stories above. Until S1.3.4 exists every account has exactly one user.

- Who gets which notice? A household (Mom, Dad, Son, Daughter) does not share one interest in everything: Dad cares about the car warranty, the son about the PlayStation. Reminders probably need an owner or a follow list per item, not "everyone on the account".
- Nobody should be forced to acknowledge every notice. Only mandatory system alerts need an acknowledge, and only before they can be deleted.
- Read/unread, hidden, deleted and preferences are per person; items and purchases may or may not be shared.
- Open: is this feature wanted at all?

## Direction (Steve, 2026-10-01)

Household sharing is only worth building if items belong to people: an item has an owner (whoever entered it, or whose email address received the receipt), and more than one person CAN be added to an item. Reminders then go to the people on that item, not to everyone on the account. Fits S2.2.1 (a unique inbound address per user). Still no stories; nothing built.

Cheap thing to keep true now: record who created each purchase and item, since that cannot be recovered later.
