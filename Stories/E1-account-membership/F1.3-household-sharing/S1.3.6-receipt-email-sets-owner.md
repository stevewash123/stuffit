# S1.3.6 A forwarded receipt belongs to the person who sent it

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S1.3.1 (Every item has an owner); S2.2.1 (Unique inbound address per user); S2.2.2 (Extract purchase details from email)  

As a **household member**, I want a receipt I forward to land as mine, so that its reminders come to me.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each person has their own inbound address (S2.2.1); the purchase and its items are created by that person and owned by them
- Anyone else in the household can be added to an item afterwards (S1.3.2)
- A receipt sent from an address that is not verified for any person is dropped, as S2.2.1 already says
- Items entered by hand are owned by whoever entered them

## Notes
Keeps to S2.2.1: only mail from the user's verified emails is accepted.
