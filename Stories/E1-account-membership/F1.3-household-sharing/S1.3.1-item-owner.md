# S1.3.1 Every item has an owner

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** MVP (the data; the sharing around it is v2)  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S7.5.3 (DDL: purchases, items and coverage schema); S1.1.1 (Sign up and log in with email)  

As a **user**, I want each thing I own to be mine, so that the reminders about it come to me and, in a household, not to everyone.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every purchase and item records who created it (`created_by_user_id`). It is set from the signed-in user, never from the request, and never changes
- Every item has exactly one owner. The owner starts as the person who entered it (or, for a forwarded receipt, the person whose inbound address received it: S1.3.6)
- The owner is stored with the item's people (S1.3.2) as the row marked owner, so reminders find them
- Existing items are filled in by a data migration: creator and owner are the account's earliest user
- An item can have its owner changed (S1.3.2); the creator never changes
- An account today has one user, so every item belongs to that one user and nothing visible changes

## Notes
Why now even with one user: who entered something cannot be recovered later. Who can SEE an item is not settled: see S1.3.7 (default to my own items, a filter for others, a Private flag). Until then every item is visible to everyone in the account, which is safe while an account has one user. Ownership and following decide who is told.

## Status

Done (M4, API): `created_by_user_id` on purchase and item; `item_person` table with one owner per item (partial unique index); migration `ItemOwnersAndPeople` fills existing rows (tested against real data). New items are owned by whoever creates them. Tests: `ItemPeopleTests.cs`.
