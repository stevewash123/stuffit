# S1.3.4 Invite a person to my account

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S1.3.2 (Add and remove people on an item); S7.6.4 (Transactional and inbound email)  

As the **head of the family** (the account holder), I want to invite someone in my household, so that we can share the things we own.

## Acceptance criteria
- Only the head of the family sends invites; members cannot (decided). The head is the person who created the account
- Invite by email; the link is single use, unguessable and expires
- Following the link proves the invited address, so a new person is not asked to verify it again (S1.1.1). They sign up or sign in and join as a member of the account; they keep their own login, notification preferences and timezone
- Invitations can be revoked before they are accepted
- An invited person starts on no items; they are added to items (S1.3.2) or become the owner of items they enter
- Billing and plan stay with the account holder (S1.2.x); nothing here changes who pays
- Open decisions: how many members a plan allows (S1.2.7); whether a member may leave and take their items (S1.3.5)

## Notes
Do not build before S1.3.1 to S1.3.3 are in use. Until this exists an account has one user and following does nothing visible.
