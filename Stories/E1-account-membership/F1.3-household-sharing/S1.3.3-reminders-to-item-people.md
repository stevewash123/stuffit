# S1.3.3 Reminders go to the people on the item

**Feature:** F1.3 Household sharing  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M4  
**Depends on:** S1.3.2 (Add and remove people on an item); S4.1.2 (Daily job sends due reminders); S4.4.3 (Dispatcher: store preferences and enforce them on every send)  

As a **household member**, I want to hear only about the things I am on, so that I am not buried in notices about everyone else's things.

## Acceptance criteria
- The reminder job tells the people on the item (owner and followers), not every user in the account
- The "not sure yet" follow-up goes to the same people
- Each person is reached at their own local time and under their own notification preferences; one person's settings never affect another's
- A reminder is marked sent only once every person on the item has been reached or has nothing left to be told
- Notifications, read state, hidden state and preferences are per person already; nothing a person does to a notice (read, delete, acknowledge) changes anyone else's
- Nobody is forced to acknowledge a notice. Acknowledging applies only to mandatory system alerts (payment failed, security, legal), and only before the user deletes one
- An item with no people (should not happen) sends nothing and logs it

## Notes
Replaces the earlier "everyone on the account" behavior. Account-level notices (billing, legal) still go to the account owner or each member by their own type rules; they are not item reminders.

## Status

Done (M4, API): `ReminderSendJob` reads the people on each item (`item_person`), for both reminders and the check-later follow-up. An item with no people logs a warning and keeps the reminder pending. Tests: `ItemPeopleTests.cs`.
