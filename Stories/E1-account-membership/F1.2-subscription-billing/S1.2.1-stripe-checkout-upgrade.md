# S1.2.1 Upgrade to paid via Stripe Checkout

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.1.1 (Sign up and log in with email)  

As a **free or trial user**, I want to upgrade to a paid plan.

## Acceptance criteria
- Monthly and annual prices offered, resolved through Stripe price lookup keys (never hardcoded or fallback price IDs)
- Checkout session carries the account id in subscription metadata
- Successful checkout flips the account to paid
- Cancelled checkout leaves the account unchanged
- Founder price honored when the account has it (S1.2.5)
