# S1.2.2 Manage billing in Stripe Customer Portal

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.1 (Upgrade to paid via Stripe Checkout)  

As a **paying user**, I want to update card, change plan, view invoices, or cancel myself.

## Acceptance criteria
- Backend creates a portal session with a return URL to the plan page and returns the URL; client redirects to it
- Button shows an Opening state and disables while the session is created; friendly error if it fails
- Portal handles invoices, payment method, plan change, cancellation and billing address
- Cancel at period end is the default; access continues until the period ends
- No custom billing UI to maintain
