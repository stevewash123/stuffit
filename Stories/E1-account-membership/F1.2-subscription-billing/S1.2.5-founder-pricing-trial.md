# S1.2.5 Founder pricing and trial

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.1 (Upgrade to paid via Stripe Checkout); S4.4.1 (Message type catalog)  

As a **early adopter**, I want to lock in a discounted price and try before paying.

## Acceptance criteria
- Trial created at signup without a card: Stripe customer plus subscription with a trial period, default payment method saved on subscription, and end-of-trial behavior set to cancel if no payment method was added
- Founder price tiers defined in one configuration (slots and prices), never in code; a slot is taken when the account gets the founder flag
- Price lookup keys follow a pattern such as base_founder1, base_founder2, base_regular; founder accounts are grandfathered on those prices
- Founder flag and tier stored on the account and in subscription metadata; shown on the plan page
- Trial length is configuration, not code
