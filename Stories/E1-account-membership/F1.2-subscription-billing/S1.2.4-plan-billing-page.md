# S1.2.4 Plan and billing page

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.1 (Upgrade to paid via Stripe Checkout); S1.2.2 (Manage billing in Stripe Customer Portal); S9.8.1 (Settings page)  

As a **user**, I want to see my plan and billing status in one place.

## Acceptance criteria
- Shows plan name, status badge (Active, Trial Membership, Past Due, Canceled), trial end or next billing date, amount, item usage against the limit, and included features
- Manage Subscription button opens the Stripe portal (S1.2.2)
- Lists what the portal can do: invoices, payment method, plan change, cancel, billing address
- Loading and error states
- Free accounts see an Upgrade button instead

## Notes
Modeled on the ConsignmentGenie license.component screen.
