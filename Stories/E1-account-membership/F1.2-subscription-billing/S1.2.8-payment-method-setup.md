# S1.2.8 Add a payment method before the trial ends

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.1 (Upgrade to paid via Stripe Checkout); S1.2.5 (Founder pricing and trial)  

As a **trial user**, I want to add my card so the subscription continues.

## Acceptance criteria
- Stripe Checkout in setup mode collects and saves the card for the subscription
- Plan page and trial-ending notification link to it
- After success the subscription converts at trial end with no further action
- Missing card at trial end cancels cleanly and the account moves to the free tier
