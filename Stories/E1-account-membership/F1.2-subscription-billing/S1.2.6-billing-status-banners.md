# S1.2.6 Past-due and canceled handling

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.3 (Stripe webhooks keep account status in step); S4.4.3 (Dispatcher: store preferences and enforce them on every send); S4.5.2 (Header bell and dropdown)  

As a **user**, I want to know about a payment problem and keep my data.

## Acceptance criteria
- invoice.payment_failed raises a mandatory notification (S4.4.3) and an in-app banner
- Grace period before limits apply; after it the account is read-only, never deleted
- Canceled accounts keep read access to existing data for a set period
- Banner links to the Stripe portal
- Trial-ending reminder sent 3 days before the trial ends, driven by the trial_will_end webhook
