# S1.2.7 Feature and limit gating

**Feature:** F1.2 Subscription billing (Stripe)  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.3 (Stripe webhooks keep account status in step)  

As a **system**, I want to decide what an account can do from its cached subscription.

## Acceptance criteria
- One check: hasAccess(account, feature) and withinLimit(account, limit)
- trialing gets full access; active gets the plan base features plus any add-on features; past_due and canceled follow S1.2.6
- Base features are a fixed list; add-ons come from subscription line items by price lookup key
- Item limit enforced here, including the free tier cap
- Upgrade prompt text per feature, e.g. Upgrade for unlimited items
- Reads the cached status (S1.2.3), not Stripe
