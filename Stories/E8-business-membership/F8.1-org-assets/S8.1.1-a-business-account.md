# S8.1.1 A business account

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S1.1.1 (sign-up); S1.2.5 (trial); S8.1.5 (free year)

As a **small business owner**, I want to start WarrantyCat as a business, so the account is named for my business and set up for my people.

## Acceptance criteria
- At sign-up the person chooses **For myself** or **For my business**. A business gives its **business name** (shown in the header and in emails the account sends; never "household").
- An existing personal account can be **changed to a business account** from Settings (owner only, one tap with a confirm); nothing in it is lost. A business account cannot be turned back into a personal one while it has other people on it.
- The person who creates the account is its **owner**. A business account has exactly one owner.
- Everything else about items, purchases, receipts, documents, plans, vehicles, reminders, export and claim guidance is **identical** to a personal account (no second product): the business name appears where the person's name did.
- The wording says **WarrantyCat**; "business" is used, not "organization" or "tenant".
- The data export includes the business name and the people list (S8.1.2) with their roles.

## Notes
The account is already the tenant (account and user schema exist). This story adds an account type and a name; it does not change how data is filtered.

## Status
Built (branch feat-business, API and UI, not merged). Account kind (personal/business) and business name on the account (the account name is the business name); sign-up choice For myself / For my business with the name (held on the pending sign-up and applied when the emailed link is opened); change a personal account to a business from Settings > Profile (owner, confirm, nothing lost; refused if others share the account); change back to personal only while nobody else is on it and no invitation is waiting; rename (owner). The header shows the business name; the export (account.json) carries the business name and the people list with roles, and items.csv gains asset_tag and location (business only). Existing accounts migrate as personal accounts. Roles: owner or employee (a personal account only member is its owner, S8.1.6).
Also built (branch feat-bizgaps, not merged): the business name is on notification and digest emails too. When the account in the notice is a business, or the person has two or more accounts, the email subject starts with the account name ("Acme Plumbing: Your free year ends in 30 days") and the email shows a one-line "For Acme Plumbing" under the brand. A personal-only person's emails are unchanged. The invitation email was checked: plain and friendly, names the business and the person who invited them and why, one link, no tracking, address used only for the invitation.
Tests: BusinessAccountTests (API integration), BusinessRulesTests (unit), business-account.spec.ts and account-membership.spec.ts (UI).
