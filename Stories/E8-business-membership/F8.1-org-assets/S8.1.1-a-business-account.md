# S8.1.1 A business account

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S1.1.1 (sign-up); S1.2.5 (trial); S8.1.5 (free year)

As a **small business owner**, I want to start WarrantyCat as a business, so the account is named for my business and set up for my people.

## Acceptance criteria
- At sign-up the person chooses **For myself** or **For my business**. A business gives its **business name** (shown in the header and in emails the account sends; never "household").
- An existing personal account can be **changed to a business account** from Settings (owner only, one tap with a confirm); nothing in it is lost. A business account cannot be turned back into a personal one while it has other people on it.
- The person who creates the account is its **owner**. A business account has exactly one owner.
- Everything else about items, purchases, receipts, documents, plans, vehicles, reminders, export and claim guidance is **identical** to a personal account (no second product): the business name appears where the person's name did.
- The wording says **WarrantyCat**; "business" is used, not "organization" or "tenant".
- The data export includes the business name and the people list (S8.1.2) with their roles.

## Notes
The account is already the tenant (account and user schema exist). This story adds an account type and a name; it does not change how data is filtered.
