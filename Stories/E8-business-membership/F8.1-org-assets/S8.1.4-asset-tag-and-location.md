# S8.1.4 Asset tag and location on an item

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S8.1.1; S9.2.9 (lists group, filter, search)

As a **business owner or employee**, I want to put the shop's own tag and the place on each item, so I can find "the drill in the van" or "tag 0042" and see what is where.

## Acceptance criteria
- Two optional text fields on an item in a **business account**: **Asset tag** (the business's own label, e.g. "0042", up to 40 characters) and **Location** (free text, e.g. "Van 2", "Front counter", up to 80 characters). Not shown on personal accounts.
- Both can be entered when adding an item and on the item page. **One item at a time in MVP** (no bulk edit).
- **Search** finds an item by asset tag or location. **Filters** on My Items: by location (the places already used on this account are offered) and "Has no asset tag".
- **Asset tags are unique within the account** when given: a duplicate is refused with the item that has it named.
- The location list is just the distinct locations typed so far (no setup, no locations table the person must manage); editing a location on one item does not rename it elsewhere.
- Both fields are in the **data export** (items.csv) and shown on the item page and the list row when present.
- A part of a vehicle (S3.5.5) and a plan do not need tags; a vehicle can have one.

## Not in MVP
Printing labels or QR codes; scanning a tag; moving many items between locations at once; "assigned to".
