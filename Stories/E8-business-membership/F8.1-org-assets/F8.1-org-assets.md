# F8.1 A business account: owner, employees, asset tags and locations

**Epic:** E8 Business membership  
**Phase:** v2 (Steve, 2026-11-06: "will it support small business" is the first question prospects ask)

Kept deliberately simple. The business account **is** the business: anything in it is business use, so there is no business-use flag and no "assigned to" (both dropped for MVP, Steve 2026-11-06). The existing data export serves the accountant.

This is the **first shared account** in WarrantyCat. Until now every account has one user (F1.3 household sharing is deprioritized and nothing invites a second person), so the business slice builds the invite and the people list that F1.3 only specified.

## Not in MVP
"Assigned to"; a business-use flag; a manager role (add one only if a specific task needs it, S8.1.3); several businesses per person (the membership list allows it, but the MVP builds one business plus one personal account); seat pricing and invoices (F8.2, after Stripe is live).
