# S8.1.3 Owner and employee: who can do what

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S8.1.2; S1.3.7 (whose items I see)

As the **owner**, I want only two roles, so there is little to learn: me (owner) and my people (employees), and a third role only if some task truly needs it.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Two roles: Owner and Employee** (Steve, 2026-11-06). There is **no manager role** in MVP. A manager is added later only if a specific task is found that should belong to a manager and not to the owner.
- **Employees see everything in the business account** (all items, purchases, documents, plans, vehicles) and can **add, edit and delete items, purchases, receipts, coverages and documents, open claims, and set their own notification preferences**. Nothing is private per employee in MVP (S1.3.7's private items are not built for business).
- **Owner only:** invite, remove, resend or revoke people; change the business name; billing, plan and payment method; **delete the business account** or **hand ownership** to someone else; export of the whole account's data; close the account.
- An employee **cannot** see or change billing, cannot see the other people's email addresses beyond their names, and cannot export the account's data (their own notification settings and sign-in are theirs).
- **Reminders and notices** go to the people on the item (owner and followers, S1.3.2); an item entered by an employee starts with that employee as the item's owner and the business owner as a follower, so the owner is told about the business's warranties. Notification preferences are per person.
- **Ownership transfer** (owner to an employee): the new owner accepts by email; the old owner becomes an employee; billing moves with ownership.
- Every item records **who entered it** (already stored); it is shown on the item page for a business account.
- If during build or use a specific task appears that the owner would not want an employee doing, list it in this story as the first task for a manager role and decide then. Do not add the role speculatively.

## Open (decide when built)
Whether an employee may delete items the owner entered (default: yes, with the usual confirm and the undo that exists for deletes).

## Status
Built (branch feat-business, not merged). Two roles, Owner and Employee, stored per membership and checked on the server from the database (not from the token): owner-only = invite, resend, revoke, remove, rename, convert, ownership offer, plan page (GET /api/business/plan), billing portal, all export endpoints, deleting the account (OwnerOnly filter and BusinessService.RequireOwnerAsync; an employee gets 403). Employees see all the business items and add, edit and delete items, purchases, receipts, coverages and documents, open claims and set their own notification preferences. Employees see names only: /api/account/members, the People list and item people hide email addresses. An item an employee enters is owned by the employee with the business owner as a follower; the item page shows Entered by in a business account. Ownership transfer: the owner offers it to an employee, who accepts from an emailed link (/accept-ownership, signed in); the old owner becomes an employee. Billing belongs to the account, so it moves with ownership. No manager role.
Open question left as built: an employee may delete items the owner entered (with the usual confirm).
Tests: BusinessAccountTests (roles matrix, entered-by/follower rules, ownership transfer), BusinessRulesTests.
