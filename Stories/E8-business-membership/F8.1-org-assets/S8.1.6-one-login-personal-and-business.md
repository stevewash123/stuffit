# S8.1.6 One login, a personal account and a business: choose after signing in

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S8.1.1; S8.1.2 (invites); S1.1.1 (sign-in)

As a **person who uses WarrantyCat at home and is invited to use it at work**, I want one login for both, so I keep my own things and see the business's in the same app, without a second email.

## Why now (Steve, 2026-11-06)
A lot of people use their personal email for work, and people who use a tool at work like it enough to use it at home. Auth is tracked by email. Changing "a user belongs to one account" to "a user has memberships" later is much harder than building it with the business accounts, so it is built now even though it is not strictly MVP.

## Acceptance criteria
- **One login per email** (as today). Instead of one account per person, a person has **memberships**: each is an account (their own personal account, or a business) and a role in it (Owner, Employee). A personal account's only member is its owner.
- **Joining a business with an email that already has a WarrantyCat login:** the person opens the invite link, signs in with their existing login (which proves the address), accepts, and the business is **added to their memberships**. Their personal account and its items are untouched. A new address signs up and gets the business membership **and** a personal account (empty), so they can start their own list at home too.
- **After sign-in:** with one membership the person goes straight in. With two or more, a **one-screen chooser** lists them ("Personal" and each business name); picking one opens the app in that account. The app **remembers the last one used** and goes straight there the next time (the chooser is still one tap away).
- **A switcher in the header** (shown only with two or more memberships) re-issues the session for the other account; nothing from the previous account stays on screen. Signing out signs out of both.
- **The two never mix:** each account's items, purchases, plans, documents, exports and settings stay separate. Data is filtered by the account the session is for (the existing tenant filter); an export from a business is the business's, owner only (S8.1.3).
- **Roles are per account:** a person can be Owner of their personal account and Employee of a business at once; what they may do is decided by the role in the account the session is for.
- **Notifications and email:** notification preferences, time zone and the digest stay **per person**. A notice, reminder or digest line names its account when the person has more than one ("Acme Plumbing: the warranty on <item> ends in 7 days"). The header's unread count is for the account in use; the switcher shows when the other account has unread notices.
- **Removing or leaving:** an employee who is removed or leaves loses only that membership; their personal account and login are untouched (S8.1.2). The owner cannot leave without handing ownership over first.
- **Billing stays with the account**: a business's plan and free year belong to the business; a personal account's belong to it (S8.1.5, S1.2.x).
- **Existing people migrate** with exactly one membership: their own account, as owner. Nothing changes for anyone who never joins a business.
- **Security:** switching and the chooser only list memberships the person truly has; a session can never be issued for an account the person is not a member of; invite links remain single use and expire.

## Notes
Replaces S8.1.2's MVP fallback ("an email that already has a WarrantyCat account cannot join").
