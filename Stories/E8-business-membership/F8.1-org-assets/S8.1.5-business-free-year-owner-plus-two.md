# S8.1.5 Business free for a year: owner plus two employees

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S8.1.1; S1.2.5 (trial and founder pricing); S1.2.7 (entitlement gating); S8.1.2

As the **operator**, I want business accounts to be free for a year for the owner and two employees, so small businesses try WarrantyCat and tell us what they need (Steve, 2026-11-06: "we want to encourage business test users").

## Acceptance criteria
- A business account gets **a free year** from creation (length in configuration; the same mechanism as the existing free-year license from sign-up, Scenario 58), **for the owner plus two employees (three people)**. No card is needed to start.
- The limit (three people, counting the owner and accepted employees; pending invites count) is a **configuration value**, enforced where invites are made (S8.1.2) with a plain message.
- The plan page shows "Free until <date>" and that the business includes you and two employees.
- **Before the year ends** the owner is told (the existing billing banners and reminders, S1.2.6), with what happens next. **Pricing after the year, and seat-based pricing for more than three people, are not decided here**: they belong to F8.2 and to the Stripe launch work (S1.2.x). Until F8.2 exists nothing is charged and nothing is cut off when the year ends without an operator decision: the account shows "Free year ended" and stays readable.
- The operator can **extend** a business account's free year and **raise** its person limit from the admin screen (a setting on the account, recorded with who and when).

## Open questions (Steve)
What a business pays after the free year and for the third employee onward (F8.2); whether the free year starts at sign-up or when the first employee accepts.
