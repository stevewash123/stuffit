# S8.1.5 Business free for a year: owner plus two employees

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S8.1.1; S1.2.5 (trial and founder pricing); S1.2.7 (entitlement gating); S8.1.2

As the **operator**, I want business accounts to be free for a year for the owner and two employees, so small businesses try WarrantyCat and tell us what they need (Steve, 2026-11-06: "we want to encourage business test users").

## Acceptance criteria
- A business account gets **a free year** from creation (length in configuration; the same mechanism as the existing free-year license from sign-up, Scenario 58), **for the owner plus two employees (three people)**. No card is needed to start.
- The limit (three people, counting the owner and accepted employees; pending invites count) is a **configuration value**, enforced where invites are made (S8.1.2) with a plain message.
- The plan page shows "Free until <date>" and that the business includes you and two employees.
- **Before the year ends** the owner is told (the existing billing banners and reminders, S1.2.6), with what happens next. **Pricing after the year, and seat-based pricing for more than three people, are not decided here**: they belong to F8.2 and to the Stripe launch work (S1.2.x). Until F8.2 exists nothing is charged and nothing is cut off when the year ends without an operator decision: the account shows "Free year ended" and stays readable.
- The operator can **extend** a business account's free year and **raise** its person limit from the admin screen (a setting on the account, recorded with who and when).

## Pricing after the free year (decided by Steve, 2026-11-06; billing not built, F8.2)
**$19.99 for up to 3 employees; $39 for unlimited employees** (assumed per month: confirm). Not charged or enforced in this story; recorded here so the limits in the code are written as plans (`business_small` = 3 employees, `business_unlimited` = no limit) that F8.2 prices. The free year includes the owner plus two employees (S8.1.5), the paid small plan up to three employees.

## Open questions (Steve)
Per month or per year for the two prices; whether the free year starts at sign-up or when the first employee accepts.

## Status
Built (branch feat-business, not merged). A business gets a free year from creation (Business:FreeYearDays, default 365; stored as Account.FreeYearEndsAt, and the existing Stripe free-year license job is started with the same length when Stripe is on). Person limit = owner plus two employees, Business:FreePersonLimit (default 3), pending invitations count, enforced when inviting with the message "Your business account includes you and two employees." The plan page shows Free until <date> and what is included; after the date it shows Free year ended, charges nothing and nothing is cut off (no Stripe work). The operator has Admin > Businesses: extend the free year (30 days / a year, or a date through the API) and raise the person limit (PUT /api/admin/businesses/{id}/free-year and /person-limit, fresh second factor, written to the audit trail with who and when). The plans are named in configuration only: business_small (up to 3 employees) and business_unlimited (no limit); no pricing or billing.
Also built (branch feat-bizgaps, not merged): the owner is reminded before the free year ends, from the account's own end date (Account.FreeYearEndsAt), not from Stripe. The hourly reminder job sends a notification and an email to the owner at 30 and at 7 days before (Business:FreeYearReminderDays, default 30 and 7), at the owner's own local time, once per point per account and end date (the notification itself is the memory, so no new table; if the operator moves the end date the reminders start again for the new date; a business with a short year gets only the closest point). New business-only notification type business_free_year_ending (Account category, on the preference grid, email and in-app, links to the plan page); personal accounts never get it. Text: "Your free year ends on <date>. WarrantyCat will tell you about plans before anything is charged." No price is named and nothing is charged. The plan page reads "Free until <date>", turning to "Free year ends in N days" in the last 30 days, then "Free year ended". Still not built: a separate banner on every page (the plan page, the bell and the email carry it). Open questions for Steve unchanged (per month or per year; when the year starts).
Tests: BusinessAccountTests (limit, pending count, operator override with audit, ended state), business-account.spec.ts; for the reminder: FreeYearReminderRulesTests (unit), BusinessGapsTests via ReminderSendTests (once per point, moved end date, personal never, preference off), free-year.spec.ts and the plan page specs.
