# S8.1.2 The owner invites employees by email

**Feature:** F8.1  **Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S8.1.1; S1.3.4 (invite a person, built here for business accounts); S1.3.5 (remove a person or leave); S7.6.4 (transactional email)

As the **owner**, I want to invite my employees by email, so they can add and see the business's things under their own login.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Only the owner invites** (decided, Steve 2026-11-06). Employees cannot invite anyone.
- **Invite by email**: the owner enters an address; WarrantyCat **sends an invitation email** from the business ("<Business name> invited you to WarrantyCat") with a single-use, unguessable link that expires (7 days, configuration). The owner sees who is invited and can **resend** or **revoke** an invite before it is accepted.
- **Following the link proves the address**, so the person is not asked to verify it again (S1.1.1). They sign up or sign in and join as an **employee**. They keep **their own login, notification preferences and time zone**; the owner never sees their password.
- **MVP (Steve, 2026-11-06): one person belongs to one account.** An email address that already has a WarrantyCat account cannot join a business: the owner is told at invite time, and the person opening an invite link is told, in plain words ("That address already has a WarrantyCat account. Ask your employer to invite a different address for now."). So login never has to ask which account. Many people use a personal email for work, so expect this message to be seen; it is accepted for the MVP. **Later:** one login that can hold a personal account and a business membership, with an account chooser at sign-in and a switcher in the header; their personal items stay theirs.
- **Limit during the free year: owner plus two employees** (S8.1.5). Inviting a fourth person is refused with a plain message ("Your business account includes you and two employees"); nothing else is blocked.
- **The People page** (Settings): every person with role, status (invited, active) and joined date; owner actions: resend, revoke, **remove an employee**. An employee can **leave** the business (with a confirm); they lose access to its items and keep their own personal account.
- **What happens to an employee's items** when they are removed or leave: items stay with the business (they were the business's); the item's "entered by" keeps their name as history; reminders for items they followed go to the owner.
- **Email addresses** are used for the invite only; no marketing; the invite says plainly who invited them and for what.
- The owner is told (a notification) when an invite is accepted.

## Notes
Do not let an invited address see any business item before accepting. Rate-limit invites per account per day (configuration).
