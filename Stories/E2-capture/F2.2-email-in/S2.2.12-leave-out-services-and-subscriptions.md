# S2.2.12 Leave out services and subscriptions, and notice the repeaters

**Feature:** F2.2 Email-in capture  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M8 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S2.2.5 (Pending items and their API); S9.9.4 (Dismiss: do not track)  
**Related:** S2.2.7 (groceries and throwaway goods, suggested after the fact)  

As a **user**, I want receipts for services I subscribe to (an AI tool, streaming, software, a utility) kept out of New Items from the start, so my list holds things that can have a warranty.

## Why
A connected mailbox brings in every receipt. Many are monthly charges from the same service. They have no product, no warranty and nothing to claim, and a monthly one returns every month. S2.2.7 suggests skipping goods that are not worth tracking. This story is about what is not a product at all, and it acts earlier: the item never reaches New Items.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Recognised on arrival.** A receipt is treated as a service when it says so (a subscription, a plan, a renewal, "billed monthly or yearly", an invoice for usage) and names no product. This is judged by the plain parser first; the model is not called for it.
- **Left out, not lost.** A service receipt is not added to New Items. It is kept where the person can find it (a "Left out" list, with the reason), and can be moved into New Items with one click.
- **Repeaters are noticed per account.** When the same sender sends receipts of the same shape twice or more (the same amount, or the same plan, about a month or a year apart), that sender is marked "repeats" for that account. Later ones from it are left out without any reading.
- **Seen and removable.** The person sees which senders are left out and why, with a count and the last date, and can stop leaving one out. Rules are per account and never shared.
- **A product from the same sender still counts.** A store that sells goods and also has a membership (Amazon, Apple, a hardware chain) is not left out as a whole: only the receipts that look like the service are.
- **Quietly, never silently.** The person is told once, at the first time, that receipts like this are being left out, and where to see them.
- **Less cost.** A left-out receipt is not read by the model and not processed (S3.1.10).

## Not in this story
- Groceries, restaurants and other goods not worth tracking (S2.2.7).
- Tracking subscription spending or renewals as a feature. This only keeps them out of the way.
