# S2.2.7 Suggest skipping receipts that do not need tracking

**Feature:** F2.2 Email-in capture  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S2.2.5 (Pending items and their API); S9.9.4 (Dismiss: do not track)  
**Related:** S9.8.5 (the person's own amount below which small lines are suggested, not pre-ticked)  

As a **user**, I want groceries and other throwaway purchases to stop cluttering New Items.

## Acceptance criteria
- Pending items that look like food, restaurants or other consumables are marked "Probably not worth tracking" and sorted below the rest
- "Stop suggesting items from this store" on Dismiss saves a per-account rule; later items from that store are dismissed automatically and can still be found under Dismissed
- Rules are listed in settings and can be removed
- Rules are never shared between accounts

## Note
The user's own habit is the model: decline the receipt unless it is an appliance or similar. A confirmation for shoes or groceries should be suggested for skipping the same way, and the suggestion should learn from what the user dismisses.
