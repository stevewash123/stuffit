# S2.2.11 Capture from the phone: camera first, and the share sheet

**Feature:** F2.2 Email-in capture  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.2.6 (A receipt upload becomes a pending item); S9.1.4 (Installable on a phone (PWA))  

As a **user** standing at the till, in the driveway or at the kitchen table with my phone, I want to photograph a receipt, or share a photo, screenshot or email to StuffIt from my phone, and have it land in New Items in a couple of taps.

## Why this matters
About half of use is on a phone, and the phone is where receipts happen: a paper receipt, a box with the model number, a screenshot of an order page. Taking or sharing a picture is the easiest input a user has. The desktop UX (forms, lists, filters) must stay solid, and this is the phone-first path beside it, not an extra.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Camera first.** On a phone, New Items opens with a large "Scan a receipt" button that opens the camera directly. Choosing from the photo library or files is one tap away. On desktop the same button is "Upload a receipt" and offers a file picker and drag-and-drop.
- **One receipt, several photos.** A long receipt or one with a back page can be several photos added to one pending item. "Add another page" after each shot; "Done" sends them together.
- **Review before sending.** Show the photo with a retake option and a crop or rotate if it is cheap. A blurry or too-dark shot is flagged before it is sent (S2.6.2 has the clean-up).
- **Goes straight to New Items.** Sending shows a short confirmation with a link to the new row. It does not walk the user through a form; reading the photo fills in the fields later (S2.2.2).
- **Share sheet intake (Android).** Installed, StuffIt appears in the phone's share sheet (Share on a photo, screenshot or PDF, next to the messaging apps) for images and PDFs (web share target in the manifest). Sharing opens StuffIt on a "What is this for?" screen showing the shared file and two choices:
  - **An item I already have:** a searchable list of my items (most recently added first), with a type for the file (receipt, manual, warranty or registration paperwork, photo of the item, other). Picking one attaches the file to that item. This is for the common case of a warranty card or manual photographed later.
  - **Something new:** creates a pending item in New Items, as an upload does (S2.2.6).
  - Several files shared at once are handled together. Cancel throws nothing away without asking. A file shared while signed out waits through sign-in, then shows the same screen.
- **Share sheet intake (iPhone).** An installed web app cannot be a share target on iOS. The native wrapper (S9.1.5) delivers it, planned after the proof of concept and before going live. Until then the iPhone path is the in-app "Choose from Photos" and forwarding an email (S2.2.9), and the how-to says so plainly.
- **Flaky connection.** A photo taken with no signal is kept on the phone and sent when the connection returns, with a visible "waiting to send" state. It is never silently lost.
- **Wi-Fi only option.** Settings has "Send photos only on Wi-Fi" (per user, off by default). When on and the phone is on mobile data, a new photo is kept on the phone and shows "waiting for Wi-Fi", the same waiting state as no signal. A "Send now anyway" button on that item overrides it for one send. Photos are the heavy part; the small data (a forwarded email, edits to a pending item) always goes through.
- **Large photos.** Shrink before upload (a full-resolution phone photo is several MB). HEIC is accepted (already handled on the server).
- **Desktop and scanners.** On a computer there is no "Take a photo" button (the browser would only open a file picker); the box says "Drop files here, or choose them" and accepts dragged PDFs and images. A web page cannot drive a scanner, so a scan comes in one of two ways, both explained in the hint under the box: save the scan as a PDF and drop it in, or use the scanner's scan-to-email and send it to the user's private StuffIt address (S2.2.1). Printers that scan-to-email are common, and that route needs no new code. A webcam option is not planned; it is poor for receipts.
- **Both layouts get tested.** Phone-width and desktop-width checks for this flow, and a real-device pass on one iPhone and one Android phone (not yet done for any of the PWA).

## Notes
- Detecting Wi-Fi: browsers cannot do it reliably. Chrome on Android reports the connection type; Safari on iPhone does not. In the browser app the option works on Android and is hidden on iPhone, with a line saying why. In the native wrapper (S9.1.5) a network plugin reports it on both, so the option works everywhere there.
- What already exists: the camera button (`capture="environment"`) on file attach, `POST /api/pending-items/upload` (up to 10 files, content-hash dedupe), the manifest and service worker, and the iPhone add-to-home-screen how-to.
- Barcode scan of a product's model or serial number is a separate idea that is a separate story once the native wrapper (S9.1.5) exists.

## Status

Built 2026-10-03 (not yet tried on a real phone):
- **Share target (Android PWA):** manifest `share_target` plus `public/share-sw.js` (wraps Angular's service worker, keeps shared files in a cache) and the `/share` "What is this for?" page: attach to one of my items as receipt, warranty or registration paperwork, manual or other, or add as new. Survives a sign-in in between.
- **Scan a receipt (New Items):** several photos or PDFs as the pages of ONE receipt, with Add another page, Retake, Remove, and Send. `POST /api/pending-items/upload?together=true` makes one pending item holding every page in order; the same pages again return the same item; a refused page leaves nothing behind. Big photos are shrunk to 2000 px first (`shrinkPhoto`). On a computer the camera button is replaced by a file picker.
- **Desktop and scanners:** camera button on touch screens only; drop files on the box; scanner and scan-to-email guidance in the hint.
Not done: blur and too-dark warning before sending, crop and rotate, the "waiting to send" queue for no signal, the Wi-Fi-only setting, iPhone share sheet (needs S9.1.5), dragging pages to reorder, and a real-device pass on one iPhone and one Android phone. The email path choice (new item or document for an existing item) is S9.9.6.

Update 2026-10-03 (later): built the waiting-to-send box and the Wi-Fi-only choice, and a too-dark warning (UI only; not tried on a real phone or on mobile data).
- **Waiting to send.** Photos taken with no connection, or while Wi-Fi only is on and the phone is on mobile data, are saved on the phone (the browser's own database, so they survive closing the app) and sent by `UploadSyncService` when the connection returns, when the app opens or gets focus, and on a one-minute timer. A send that fails for lack of a connection keeps the pages instead of showing an error. New Items shows "N receipts are waiting to send from this phone" with Send now. A batch the server will never accept is dropped with the reason; a busy or down server keeps it for later.
- **Send photos only on Wi-Fi.** Settings > Profile > Photos. A choice for this device (not per user as first written: mobile data is a property of the device), off by default, kept in the browser. On mobile data the scan panel says so and offers "Send now anyway". The browser has to say whether it is on Wi-Fi: Chrome on Android does, Safari on iPhone does not, so there the setting is not offered (with a line saying why) and nothing is ever held back on a guess. It becomes available on iPhone in the native app (S9.1.5).
- **Too dark.** A page whose photo is dark overall shows "Looks too dark to read. Retake it?" and is never blocked. A blur warning is not built: telling a blurry receipt from a plain one needs real receipt photos to calibrate, and a wrong warning is worse than none.
Still not done: crop and rotate, dragging pages to reorder, and the real-device pass on one iPhone and one Android phone (the share target, the camera, the queue and the Wi-Fi choice have all only been tried in a test browser).
