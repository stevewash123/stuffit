# S2.2.6 A receipt upload becomes a pending item

**Feature:** F2.2 Email-in capture  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.2.5 (Pending items and their API); S2.1.2 (Attach receipts, manuals, photos); S2.2.2 (Extract purchase details from email)  

As a **user**, I want to upload a receipt photo or PDF without filling a form and have it show up in New Items.

## Why this matters
Many purchases never produce an email receipt (in-store buys where the person declines one, or takes the paper copy). The photo or PDF upload is then the only capture path for them, alongside the order-confirmation emails in S2.2.2. It is a main path, not a fallback.

## Acceptance criteria
- "Upload a receipt" on New Items accepts photos and PDFs (several at once); photo clean-up applies (S2.6.2)
- Each file creates a pending item with whatever extraction finds (store, date, total, line items); a file nothing can be read from still creates a row, all fields missing
- A multi-item receipt makes one pending item per line item, sharing the receipt
- The stored file is the receipt attached on Add Details or Accept
- Extraction cost is limited per account (S7.6.5); over the limit the row is created unread
- Photo OCR beyond text receipts is F2.4

## Status
Minimal version done (2026-10-02, API and UI): `POST /api/pending-items/upload` takes up to 10 PDFs or photos at once (photo clean-up applies); each becomes a pending item with every field missing, and the file is stored against it (new document owner type `pendingItem`). The same file sent twice returns the item we already have (content hash dedupe key). A refused file is reported on its own and leaves nothing behind. On Accept or Add Details the receipt moves onto the purchase. New Items has an "Upload a receipt" button with a per-file result list. 4 API integration tests and 4 UI specs.
Not done: reading anything from the file (needs S2.2.2 and S7.6.5, so every upload arrives blank), one pending item per line item of a multi-item receipt, the extraction cost cap.

Multi-item receipts and emails (2026-10-18, API and UI; committed 2026-10-19): a receipt or order email that lists several things makes one New Item for each, with its own price as the total (the order's total is not used or checked), its own copy of the receipt pages, and the same store, date, order number and card. The reader lists every line with a kind (`products`, prompt document-v9): product (a durable thing you could return or claim on) and repair (labour or installation, which may not carry a guarantee) become items, and a repair waits on the "Not sure of" list until the person names it; consumable (food, fuel, cleaners, paint, lumber, screws), service, fee, tax and discount lines are left out. A receipt of only consumables keeps the store and date and names no item. Items after the first are found again by the first item's key plus the line number (`#2`, `#3`), so the same receipt or a second email for the order never doubles them. Not done: showing the lines that were left out ("2 other lines not tracked"); a later shipping email updating the second and later items (only the first is updated); grouping the items into one purchase on Quick Add (S2.4.2); an order email split by kind (emails give names only, so consumables in an email are still items).


Update 2026-10-19 (not committed): consumables are no longer dropped. With at least one product or repair on the receipt they are kept as doubtful lines (at most 6, dearest first), and the keys `#2`, `#3` are replaced by a receipt key and a line key, so a second scan decides each line on its own (a deleted line comes back; waiting, added and dismissed lines are not made again). See docs/plan-worth-tracking-rule.md (S9.8.5).

Update 2026-10-19: the reader prompt is document-v11. Each product line also gives a quantity, so a line price can be told from a unit price (24 studs at 110.16 is 4.59 each). New Items shows "x24, 4.59 each" on such a line (S9.2.11).

Update 2026-11-02 (UAT FS-33-3; branch uat-r3, not merged): the per-receipt cap no longer drops anything. Consumables beyond the cap (6, dearest first) are still made as New Items, flagged over the cap (stored as line kind `consumable-extra`, no migration), and wait under Smaller things with the reason "Cheaper than the lines kept" (or their own "Under $N · ..." reason when that applies). They are never raised above Warning, so they do not count in the nav badge. `leftOut` in the upload result now means "lines shown under Smaller things"; the upload note reads "N more lines are under Smaller things". Cap and order are unchanged.
