# S2.1.1 Add a purchase with one or more items

**Feature:** F2.1 Manual purchase entry  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M3  
**Depends on:** S7.5.3 (DDL: purchases, items and coverage schema); S9.5.1 (Pick a party or create one inline)  

As a **user**, I want to record what I bought, where, when and for how much.

## Acceptance criteria
- One purchase can hold several items
- Seller, date, total, order ref, payment method captured
- Item has name, manufacturer, category, model/serial, price
- Saving proposes coverages (see F3.1)


## Status
Done (M3): create a purchase with one or more items; seller, card, date, total, order number; duplicate order numbers are refused with a pointer to the existing purchase.

Update (S1.3.1): the purchase and each item record who created them, from the signed-in user. The creator of an item is its first owner.
