# S2.6.1 Choose how long receipts are kept

**Feature:** F2.6 Receipt retention & upload processing  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S9.8.1; S7.6.2; S4.4.3  

As a **user**, I want to decide how long my receipts and files are kept.

## Acceptance criteria
- Settings > Privacy & data offers: Keep until I delete (default), or Delete files one year after the last coverage on the purchase ends
- Per purchase override: Keep this one
- Auto-delete removes the files only; the purchase and coverage data stay
- A notification (mandatory type) is sent 30 days before any file is deleted, with a link to keep it
- Delete a single file, or all files, at any time
- The nightly job is idempotent and logs what it removed

## Notes
Default chosen so a missing receipt never surprises someone on the day a claim needs it.

## Status

Done (M5): Settings > Privacy & data (keep until I delete, the default, or a year after the last coverage ends); per-purchase Keep these files; delete one file or all files at any time; nightly `ReceiptRetentionJob` (03:00 UTC). A mandatory notice (`files_deletion_notice`) goes out at least 30 days before anything is deleted; deletion needs a notice for the same date that is at least 30 days old; changing the setting or the coverage dates cancels earlier notices. Files only: purchases and coverage stay. A file that cannot be removed keeps its record and is retried. No coverage, or any coverage with no end date, means never. Tests: `RetentionPlannerTests`, `RetentionTests`, `privacy-security.spec.ts`, `purchase-files.spec.ts`.
