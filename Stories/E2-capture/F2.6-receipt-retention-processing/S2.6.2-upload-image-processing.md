# S2.6.2 Strip location data and shrink photos on upload

**Feature:** F2.6 Receipt retention & upload processing  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S2.1.2  

As a **user**, I want to have photos cleaned up when I upload them.

## Acceptance criteria
- Orientation is applied from the photo metadata first, then all metadata (GPS, device, time) is removed
- Long edge limited to about 2000 px and recompressed; HEIC converted to JPEG; screenshots kept readable
- Only the processed copy is stored
- PDFs are stored untouched (checked by content as today)
- The user sees the size saved; originals are not kept

## Notes
Phone photos of 3 to 8 MB should end up well under 1 MB.

## Status

Done (M5): `MagickImageProcessor` (ImageMagick, Apache-2.0). Orientation applied first, then every piece of metadata removed (EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, comments), colour converted to sRGB, long edge limited to 2000 px (never enlarged), JPEG quality 85; PNG screenshots with flat colour and text stay PNG; transparency flattened onto white; WebP and HEIC become JPEG; PDFs stored untouched. The original is never kept; `original_size_bytes` lets the purchase page show what was saved. A picture that cannot be read is refused and nothing is stored. A real HEIC sample converted (894 kB to 160 kB) on Windows. NOT YET CHECKED on the Linux host: HEIC reading depends on the ImageMagick build there; verify on Render before relying on it. Tests: `ImageProcessorTests` (unit), `UploadProcessingTests`.
