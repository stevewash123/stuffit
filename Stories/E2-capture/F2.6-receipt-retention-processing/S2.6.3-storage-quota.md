# S2.6.3 Fair-use storage cap

**Feature:** F2.6 Receipt retention & upload processing  
**Phase:** MVP  
**Milestone:** M5  
**Depends on:** S1.2.7; S2.1.2  

As a **operator**, I want to bound how much one account can store.

## Acceptance criteria
- Per-account total cap (for example 1 GB on the free tier, configurable per plan) read from entitlements
- A clear message before the upload, never a silent failure
- Usage shown in Settings > Privacy & data
- Over-limit accounts keep what they have and can delete to make room
