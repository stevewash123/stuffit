# F2.6 Receipt retention & upload processing

**Epic:** E2 Capture: getting purchases in  
**Phase:** MVP (before launch)

Decided 2026-10-01: keep files until the user deletes them, with an opt-in auto-delete; strip location data and shrink photos on upload; fair-use storage cap. Storage cost is not the driver (roughly $1.50 a month per 100 GB); privacy and trust are.
