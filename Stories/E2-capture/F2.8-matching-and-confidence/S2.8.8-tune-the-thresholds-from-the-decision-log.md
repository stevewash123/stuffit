# S2.8.8 Tune the thresholds from the decision log

**Feature:** F2.8 Matching and confidence  
**Phase:** future  
**Depends on:** S2.8.1; S2.8.5

As a **operator**, I want thresholds to move with evidence.

## Acceptance criteria
- A weekly view over `match_decision`: where people pick the runner-up, where they accept option one, how often each rule is overridden.
- Thresholds are settings per stake, changed in one place, with the change recorded.
- Raise a bar where the runner-up is often chosen; lower it where option one is nearly always accepted.
