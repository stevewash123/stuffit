# S2.8.3 A partial name stops binding on its own

**Feature:** F2.8 Matching and confidence  
**Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S2.8.2

As a **person scanning receipts**, I want a printed name that only starts like a store or brand not to be taken for it.

## Acceptance criteria
- A word-prefix or brand-inside-the-line match is a candidate, not a bind. Only an exact key or an explicit alias binds on its own.
- Short or common names (K2, Echo, Smith, Target, Apple) are marked restricted: they match only as a whole printed key, never as a prefix or inside a line.
- The wrong binds found in the review stop: `Target Practice Supplies` is not Target, `Smith & Wesson` is not Smith Optics, `Echo Dot` does not take Echo's lawn-and-garden category, a stray `K2` in a line is not the K2 brand, and `SAMS CLUB #6452` is Sam's Club. Each is a standing test.
- A candidate that does not bind is offered to the person (S2.8.4), never dropped, and nothing becomes a private party as a side effect of a guess.

## Notes
More questions at first is expected and accepted: conservative by default, loosened from S2.8.1 data.
