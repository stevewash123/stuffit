# S2.8.2 One name normalizer and an alias table

**Feature:** F2.8 Matching and confidence  
**Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S2.8.1

As a **person scanning receipts**, I want the same store to be recognised however the receipt prints it.

## Acceptance criteria
- One normalizer is used by the matcher and by the duplicate check: lower case, punctuation, store numbers (`#6452`), legal suffixes (Inc, LLC) and common receipt noise removed. `SAMS CLUB #6452` and `Sam's Club` give one key everywhere.
- Seller and manufacturer aliases live in an indexed table with a unique normalized key; an exact match is one indexed lookup. The matchers stop loading every seller and manufacturer to scan them.
- Existing curated aliases are migrated unchanged; a check fails the build if two sellers share a key.
- Behavior is otherwise identical: this story fixes the Sam's Club split and removes the full-table scans, nothing more.
