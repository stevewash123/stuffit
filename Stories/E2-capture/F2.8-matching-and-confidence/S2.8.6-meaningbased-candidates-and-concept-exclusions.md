# S2.8.6 Meaning-based candidates and concept exclusions

**Feature:** F2.8 Matching and confidence  
**Phase:** future  
**Depends on:** S2.8.1; S2.8.5; S2.8.1 data showing misses spelling cannot fix

As a **person scanning receipts**, I want a store's name, and what it does not sell, to be understood by meaning and not only by spelling.

## Acceptance criteria
- Candidate lookup adds spelling-variant search (trigram) and meaning search (embeddings in Neon) for what the exact and alias lookups miss.
- A concept exclusion (Target: not firearms) is one stored concept that excludes every close input or item line (gun, rifle, ammo, target practice) for that merchant, without listing words.
- Candidates and exclusions only feed the same `decision` of S2.8.4; nothing here binds on its own.
- Built only if S2.8.1 shows a meaningful share of misses the earlier stages cannot fix. The embedding model, and whether merchant embeddings include category and typical lines, are decided then.

## Notes
Needs the `pg_trgm` and `vector` extensions on Neon: check they are available before planning.
