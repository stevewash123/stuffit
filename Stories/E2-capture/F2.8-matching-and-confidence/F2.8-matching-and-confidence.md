# F2.8 Matching and confidence

**Epic:** E2 Capture: getting purchases in  
**Phase:** v2 (S2.8.1 to S2.8.5), future (the rest)

How a printed store, brand, return policy or warranty line becomes a party and a coverage: exact and alias first, candidates when unsure, the person's pick remembered, every decision recorded. The current matcher is deterministic and fine on the small curated catalog, but it binds on partial names, learns nothing from corrections and keeps no record of why it chose.

Source: the 2026-11-11 design reviews in `Testing/Scenarios/Results/` (`DESIGN-REVIEW-2026-11-11-StuffIt_UI-code-review.md`, `DESIGN-REVIEW-2026-11-11-StuffIt_API-matching-confidence-scoring.md`). Finding ids (F1...) are the UI review's. Check each claim against the code before starting: the reviews were read-only and the code moves.

S2.8.1 to S2.8.3 need no AI and no UI change. S2.8.4 is the contract the UI stories in F9.10 wait for.
