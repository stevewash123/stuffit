# S2.8.7 A model orders short lists when nobody is there

**Feature:** F2.8 Matching and confidence  
**Phase:** future  
**Depends on:** S2.8.4; S2.8.6

As a **person whose email is read in the background**, I want a likely match to be suggested, never bound, while I am away.

## Acceptance criteria
- For background arrivals (email in), the model may rank the short candidate list; the item is flagged for review and nothing binds silently.
- The model never writes a store or brand name and never creates a party. Its input candidates, choice and reason are logged with the decision.
- In interactive screens the model may only order the options and explain, never choose.
