# S2.8.5 A pick or a rejection teaches the matcher

**Feature:** F2.8 Matching and confidence  
**Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S2.8.4

As a **person who corrects a match**, I want my correction to be remembered, so the same receipt is never wrong twice.

## Acceptance criteria
- `POST /api/match-feedback` takes the field (store, brand, policy), the printed text, and either a chosen party id (confirm) or none (reject).
- A confirm writes a user-confirmed alias for that printed key. A reject writes an alias-scoped exclusion: that printed key never binds to that party again. Both belong to the account and never change the shared catalog.
- Dismissing a proposal for one item (`notApplicable`, `unknown`) is unchanged and writes no alias.
- Repeated rejections of one party for similar items are listed for an operator as a proposed shared exclusion; nothing shared changes without review.
- A business account has its own aliases on top of the shared catalog, never merged into the global list.
