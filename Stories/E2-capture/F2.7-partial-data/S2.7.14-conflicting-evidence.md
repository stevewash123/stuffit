# S2.7.14 Two sources disagree

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.2  

As a **user**, I want to be told when two sources give different dates or models, and choose which to trust.

## Acceptance criteria
- When evidence for a field conflicts beyond its tolerance (for example a decoded year later than the user's remembered year), the item shows a conflict, not a silent pick
- Both values and their sources are shown side by side; the user chooses or keeps both as a range
- Impossible combinations are flagged (bought before it was made; coverage start after expiry)
- The choice is recorded as evidence ("user decided")
- Until resolved, coverage uses the more cautious (earlier) end date
