# F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts

**Epic:** E2 Capture: getting purchases in  
**Phase:** v2

Most real items arrive with gaps: a manual and an unfilled registration card for the AC compressor, "a Cooper, installed by Home Depot", but no purchase date. This feature makes partial information a normal case: dates and other facts can be approximate, every fact carries its evidence and confidence, coverage is worked out as a range, and the app helps recover what is missing.

Two kinds of story: **pattern** stories that apply to any fact (S2.7.1, S2.7.2, S2.7.10, S2.7.11, S2.7.12, S2.7.13, S2.7.14, S3.1.6) and **specific** stories for a situation or product type (manual or card capture, items already owned, registration cards, installers, serial decoding, vehicles).

Principle: plan on the cautious end, never invent a precise fact, show why we believe each fact, and never promise coverage.
