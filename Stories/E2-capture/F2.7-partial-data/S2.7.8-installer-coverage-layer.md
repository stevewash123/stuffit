# S2.7.8 The installer is part of the coverage

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S3.1.2; S3.2.1; S9.5.1 (Pick a party or create one inline)  

As a **user**, I want "installed by Home Depot" to bring its labor warranty alongside the maker's parts warranty.

## Acceptance criteria
- Item and purchase carry an installer (a party) and an install date (date precision per S2.7.1); install date may differ from purchase date
- Warranty clock follows the policy: some start at purchase, some at installation; policy data says which
- Installer policies (for example installation workmanship terms) propose a coverage like any other (S3.1.2); labelled "installer"
- The coverage list shows the layers separately: maker parts, installer labor, store return, card benefit, and who to call for each
- Installer unknown but the work was professional: record "professionally installed" so S2.7.9 can suggest finding out who
- Applies to HVAC, water heaters, flooring, roofing, windows, appliances and similar

## Notes
Specific: installer layer; pattern for any labor warranty.
