# S2.7.12 Firming up a fact recomputes coverage

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.2; S3.1.6; S4.1.2 (Daily job sends due reminders)  

As a **user**, I want better information to update my coverage and reminders, and to see what changed.

## Acceptance criteria
- Changing a date, model, installer or registration status recomputes proposed coverage from the new facts
- Coverage the user already confirmed is never overwritten; changes appear as "proposed update" to confirm (same as S9.4.2)
- Reminders are rebuilt from the new end dates; superseded reminders are cancelled, not duplicated
- The item history keeps what was believed when and why (evidence stays)
- A short notice when the change matters: "This is now covered until June 2029", or "This ended in 2024"

## Notes
Pattern: recompute on new evidence.
