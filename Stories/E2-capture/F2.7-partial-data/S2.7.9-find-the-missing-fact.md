# S2.7.9 Next steps to recover a missing fact

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.2; S2.7.1  

As a **user**, I want practical steps to recover a missing date, serial or proof of purchase.

## Acceptance criteria
- Per missing fact, a short checklist chosen from what is known: the seller (retailer account or order history, loyalty or phone number lookup, store contact with the order details), the card (statement for the likely months), the maker (serial lookup), the installer (job records), the home (closing documents)
- Steps are reference data per seller and per maker, versioned, with a source URL and a last-checked date
- Each step can be ticked off; finding the answer opens "Add evidence" (S2.7.2)
- A short script for calling a seller or maker (what to say, what to have ready)
- Never claims the seller keeps records; states what is commonly possible
- Shares the checklist pattern with S5.4.3 (Preflight checklist) if that is built first

## Notes
Pattern: per seller / per maker recovery steps.
