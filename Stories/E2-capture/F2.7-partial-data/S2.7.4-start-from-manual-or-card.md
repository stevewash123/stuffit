# S2.7.4 Start from a manual, nameplate photo or registration card

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.5; S2.2.5 (Pending items and their API); S2.1.2; S7.6.5 (LLM integration and cost control)  

As a **user**, I want to photograph a manual, a nameplate or an unfilled registration card and have an item started from it.

## Acceptance criteria
- Upload creates a pending item (S2.2.5) whose source is "document", with maker, model and serial read when legible; no store or date required
- Handles the three common sources: the owner manual cover, the rating plate on the unit, a warranty or registration card
- Each read value has a confidence and is confirmed by the user before it is trusted; low-confidence reads are shown editable
- The document is stored as the item's manual or card (S2.1.2) and as evidence (S2.7.2)
- Maker and model are matched to known makers/models (S2.7.6); an unknown maker offers "add it"
- A card with registration terms (deadline, phone, address) feeds S2.7.7
- Extraction cost is limited per account (S7.6.5)
