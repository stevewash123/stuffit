# S2.7.6 Model number fills in the rest

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables); S2.7.2  

As a **user**, I want entering a model number to tell me the maker, the product type and the standard warranty.

## Acceptance criteria
- Reference data maps model patterns to maker, category and the maker's warranty terms for that product line and year range
- Entering or reading a model suggests maker and category; the user confirms
- Warranty terms by model family (for example 10-year compressor, 1-year labor) become a proposed coverage that depends on the date range (S3.1.6)
- Unknown models fall back to the maker's general policy (S3.2) and say "general terms"
- Crowd-contributed model data enters unverified and is confirmed the same way as policies (F6)
- Pattern: any model-level fact (recalls, manual links) hangs off this same lookup
