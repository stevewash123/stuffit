# S2.7.3 Add something I already own (no purchase)

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.1; S2.7.2; S1.3.1 (Every item has an owner)  

As a **user**, I want to track the water heater that came with the house, or a gift, without inventing a purchase.

## Acceptance criteria
- "I already own this" path: item without a seller or receipt, with how it arrived (bought, came with the home, gift, inherited, used purchase, unknown)
- An age estimate is accepted instead of a date: "about 8 years old", "installed in 2016", "came with the house in 2021"
- The data model allows an item with no purchase record; a purchase can be attached later (S2.7.13)
- Coverage proposals run on what is known; with nothing known the item still appears, flagged incomplete
- Counts as an item for lists, reminders and totals, with its source shown
- Used items and gifts: manufacturer coverage may not transfer; the proposal says so where the policy says so (policy data, S3.2)
