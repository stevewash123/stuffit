# S2.7.15 Vehicles: VIN gives the rest

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** future  
**Milestone:** M8  
**Depends on:** S2.7.2; S2.7.6; S2.7.1  

As a **user**, I want to enter a vehicle's VIN and get its make, model, year and warranty terms.

## Acceptance criteria
- VIN entry (or photo of the door-jamb sticker) decodes make, model, year and plant using a standard decoder; check digit validated
- Vehicle coverage is time-and-mileage ("5 years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first"); the item carries a mileage reading and its date so the mileage side can be estimated
- Open recalls are listed from a public recall source with the lookup date shown
- Purchase date estimated from the model year and the in-service date when unknown (S2.7.1)
- Own vehicle type and fields; not forced into the appliance form

## Notes
Specific: vehicles. Coverage rules differ enough to be their own feature if pursued.
