# S2.7.7 The registration card nobody filled in

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.4; S3.1.6; S3.2.1  

As a **user**, I want to know what an unfilled registration card means for my coverage and what to do now.

## Acceptance criteria
- Policy data records, per maker, whether registration changes coverage (extends it, unlocks it, or does nothing) and the registration window
- The item shows one of: "registration not needed", "register by [date]", "registration window has passed", "unknown"
- Window passed: the next step offers to ask the maker whether late registration or the standard terms apply, with the contact and a short script (S2.7.9)
- Registration deadlines are urgent reminders when still open (S4.2.2)
- The user can record "I registered" with the date and proof, or "I did not"
- Wording never promises coverage; it says what the policy says and what to try
