# S2.7.13 Match a partial item to the purchase that arrives later

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.2.4 (De-duplicate forwarded receipts); S2.7.3; S2.7.4  

As a **user**, I want the later receipt or email to join the item I started from a manual, not create a second one.

## Acceptance criteria
- New receipts, emails and uploads are matched to existing items and pending items by maker, model, serial, seller, date range and order number
- A confident match is offered as "Looks like your Cooper AC unit. Attach this receipt?" with accept or "not the same"
- No silent merge; a merge keeps both sources as evidence and takes the better facts
- Match tolerates missing fields (a missing serial is not a mismatch)
- Un-merge is possible for a mistaken merge
- Two units of the same model (a pair of ACs) are never merged without the serial or an explicit choice
