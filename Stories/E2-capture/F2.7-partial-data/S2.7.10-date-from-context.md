# S2.7.10 Date clues from context

**Feature:** F2.7 Partial data and uncertain facts  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6  
**Depends on:** S2.7.1; S2.7.2; S2.7.3  

As a **user**, I want helpful bounds on a date from things I already know.

## Acceptance criteria
- A home profile (optional): when the owner moved in or closed, year built. "Came with the house" bounds the date to the move-in date or earlier, and not later
- A photo's date stamp and a file's date are offered as clues when the user attaches a photo; clearly labelled as a clue
- Statements and orders in the account (the card used, forwarded emails near the date) are suggested as clues, never silently applied
- "About N years old" turns into a range using the user's plain-language precision
- Each clue is evidence (S2.7.2) with its own confidence; the user accepts or ignores it
- No clue is applied without the user seeing it

## Notes
Pattern: bounds from context.
