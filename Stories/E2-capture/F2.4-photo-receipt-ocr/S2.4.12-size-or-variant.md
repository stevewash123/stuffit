# S2.4.12 Keep the size or variant in its own field

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v1  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1 (document reader); S2.4.2 (one intake)  
**Related:** S2.7.6 (model number fills in the rest); S3.2.8 (product tree: sizes under a line)  

As a **user** adding something I bought, I want its size (40 gal, 18 in x 3/4 in, 84 in) and its colour kept apart from its name and its model number, so nothing is dropped and the names stay consistent.

## Why
Sizes were sometimes put in the model number, sometimes in the name, sometimes dropped. A size in the model number breaks matching on the maker's code; a size in the name makes names inconsistent and cannot be searched or compared. One field, "Size or variant", gives it a home.

## Acceptance criteria
- Items and New Items have a **Size or variant** field, free text, up to 100 characters.
- The reader fills it from the line: a dimension, capacity, volume, count or fit as printed (40gal, 4.5 cu ft, 225/65R17, #8 box, 12-pack), and the colour or finish when printed. Units are kept as printed.
- The size is taken out of the item's name and out of the model number. The name keeps everything else after the brand: a series or style name, or a tag such as Pro or Dalton, is never dropped. Example: Rheem Perf Platinum 40gal is brand Rheem, name "Perf Platinum Water Heater", size "40gal".
- A name that is nothing but the size is left as it is.
- New Items shows the size beside the name; the item page shows and edits it; the Add form offers it, and a document read on the Add form or the item page proposes it like any other fact.
- A size not read is never a failure: it can be typed later.

## Not in this story
- A separate store part number (a distributor's Part No is still kept as the model number; whether to split it is open).
- Using the size to match a policy or product-tree node.
- Searching or filtering by size.

## Status
Built 2026-10-20 (API and UI, uncommitted): `size` on items and pending items (migration ItemSize), reader prompt document-v15, `ReadingText.WithoutSize`, the Add form, the item page and New Items. Unit and UI tests pass; integration tests not run yet. The answer key and viewer have a `size` column (read-only to the verdict: it never fails a receipt).
