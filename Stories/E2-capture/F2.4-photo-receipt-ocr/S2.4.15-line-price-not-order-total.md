# S2.4.15 Show the line's price, not the order total

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v1  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1 (document reader); S2.4.2 (one intake)  
**Related:** S2.4.7 (protection plan lines), S2.4.4 (cross-check what was read)  

As a **user** adding something from a receipt, I want the price on the item to be what that item cost, so my records and my claims show the right amount.

## Why
UAT of 982952c (FS-41-1 and FS-41-6) found item rows showing the order total: the headphones at $421.98 instead of $349.99, and the dishwasher at $1,539.22 instead of $1,099.00. The total is either tax included or the sum of several lines (item, plan, install), so it is the wrong number for the item. Steve: not now; fix later.

## Acceptance criteria
- The row and the saved item carry the line's own price. Tax, plan and service lines are not added into it.
- The order total is kept as the order total (it is already used to cross-check), never as the item price.
- When the line price cannot be told from the total, the row says so and asks, rather than showing the total as the price.
- A check on the sample receipts: headphones 349.99, dishwasher 1,099.00, and the other plan receipts match the answer key (`answer-key-by-item.csv`).

## Not in this story
- Splitting tax across items.
- Reading prices from receipts that print no line prices.
