# S2.4.11 Tell the person when a receipt has nothing to track

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v1  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1 (document reader); S2.4.2 (one intake)  
**Related:** S9.8.5 (the "worth tracking" rule); S2.4.9 (return receipts)  

As a **user** who photographed a receipt, I want to be told when nothing on it can have a warranty or be returned, so I know it was read and why nothing was added.

## Why
A grocery receipt, or one of only supplies or labour, has nothing to track. Making a blank New Item for it is noise, and saying nothing looks like the upload failed. The person gets one plain sentence instead and nothing is added to New Items.

## Acceptance criteria
- When the reader lists lines on a receipt and none is kept (every line is a consumable with no product beside it, a service, a fee, or labour only), no New Item is made and the stored pages are not kept.
- The person sees exactly: **"No items on this receipt appear to have warranties or be returnable."**
- The message appears wherever a receipt is sent: the scan panel and the Home quick photo (shown as "Nothing to track"), and the upload on New Items.
- A receipt with at least one product, repair, or consumable beside a product behaves as before.
- A document the reader cannot read, or one with no product list (an order email, a warranty document), is not affected.
- Sending the same receipt again reads it again (there is no kept copy to match).
