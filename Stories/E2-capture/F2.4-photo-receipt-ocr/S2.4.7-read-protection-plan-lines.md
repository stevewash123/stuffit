# S2.4.7 Read a protection plan on a receipt, and keep it out of the item

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1 (document reader); S2.4.4 (checks); S3.4.2 (plans)  
**Pairs with:** S3.4.3 (the plan is created when the item is added)  

As a **user** who bought a protection plan with an item (Geek Squad, a store's extended warranty, AppleCare), I want the receipt to show me the plan next to the item, not as another item or part of its name, so I can add the plan to the item with one tick.

## Why
A receipt line for a plan is not a thing the person owns. Today the reader lists every line under `items`, so a plan line can become the item's name, or turn "TV" into "TV and 1 other item". The reader is already told not to use a plan as the main item when it picks the item price, which helps the price but not the name. The plan is its own record (S3.4.2) attached to the item; the receipt should feed that, not the item.

## The model (one rule)
- The **seller** is on the purchase. The **manufacturer** is on the item. The **plan company** is on the plan: whoever you contact to make a claim, as the plan names them. A card issuer is on the payment method.
- A plan is never an item. It is a separate record attached to the item through the existing plan link.
- A plan company can belong to a parent (Geek Squad under Best Buy) using the party's existing parent link. That records that they are related and changes nothing else.
- Only one kind of company record exists. A company can hold several roles (Apple is a maker and a plan company).

## Acceptance criteria
- **The reader finds plans.** New fields for a plan on a receipt: plan name (as printed), company, price, term in months (or the end date if printed), contract number, **when the term starts as the document says it** (on delivery, on purchase, or a date), and **what it covers as printed** (a SKU, model or product name, for example "Covers SKU 6505727"). They are read only when the line is a protection plan, extended warranty, service plan or accidental-damage plan. Plan lines are left out of `items`, so they cannot become the item's name or its count of "other items".
- **Item price excludes the plan** (as today), and the checks of S2.4.4 gain one: item price plus plan price is not more than the total.
- **More than one plan** on a receipt: each is kept. The common case is one.
- **A pending item holds the plans it found** in their own rows, separate from the item and from the policies of S3.2.x, and they go away with it. Each keeps its confidence, so a doubtful one is flagged and is not added on a quick add.
- **The company is matched** to a known warranty provider by name (exact, then normalised). A name that matches nothing is kept as a company private to the account, with the role "warranty provider", flagged as not verified, as for an unlisted brand (S2.4.1). A known company brings in what we know about it (claim phone and website), marked as coming from the company's record.
- **Which item it is for.** A pending item makes one item, so a plan attaches to that item. When the plan says what it covers (a SKU or model), it is checked against the item's SKU, model number or name; a match is stated, a mismatch is shown with "check this is for the item above" and is not added on a quick add. A plan that names nothing is attached to the item above and not added on a quick add if there is more than one product on the receipt.
- **New Items shows it.** The item's row says what was found ("Includes: Geek Squad 3-year plan, $199"), with the company and term, and a way to edit or remove it before adding.
- **Nothing is invented.** A term the receipt does not state is left blank, and the plan then cannot be added on a quick add (S3.4.3).
- The reading prompt is versioned; cached readings from before are not reused for this.
- Deterministic and unit tested with plain readings (fake provider); the sample Geek Squad receipt is a case in the labelled set (S2.4.3).

## Not in this story
- Reading the plan's terms from the plan document itself (S3.4.2 covers a contract upload).
- Multiple items per receipt, each with its own plan. One item per receipt is how New Items works today.
- Adding Geek Squad and other plan companies as shared data. Seeding is held until testing winds down; an unlisted company is kept for the account in the meantime.

## First real example (2026-10-18)
Synthetic Best Buy order confirmation, `21-letter-bestbuy-email-marketing-geek-squad.pdf`: Sony WH-1000XM5 headphones ($349.99, SKU 6505727, Model WH1000XM5/B) and "Geek Squad Protection - 2-Year Plan for headphones" ($49.99, "Covers SKU 6505727", "Plan term starts on delivery"); subtotal $399.98, tax $22.00, total $421.98, Visa 1212; footer "Geek Squad Protection plans are sold by Geek Squad"; a marketing block with offers and four recommended products. Read today by the reader (no plan fields yet): class, store, order number, date, total, item price, tax, card, brand, model name and model number (SKU correctly left out) were right; the delivery range "Oct 6 - Oct 8" gave the first day; the marketing products and the offer were ignored. The plan line was returned inside `items`, which is the gap this story closes. It shows the document can state its own start ("on delivery") and what it covers (the SKU), so both are read, not assumed.

## Second real example (2026-10-18)
Synthetic Best Buy sales receipt, `16-a5-bestbuy-dishwasher-geek-squad-install.pdf`: "Bosch 800 Series Dishwasher SHP78CM5N" ($1,099.00; SKU 6489211, serial FD9402-0147733), "Geek Squad Protection 3-Year" ($229.99; "Covers dishwasher, term from delivery 08/19/2026") and "Geek Squad Standard Install" ($129.99); subtotal $1,458.98, tax $80.24, total $1,539.22. It adds three things: (1) a **service line is not a plan and not an item**: an install is a one-off service, kept out of the item and out of the plans (its price still counts toward the total); (2) the plan names what it covers in plain words ("dishwasher"), not a SKU, so the match is by product word; (3) the item's name is "800 Series Dishwasher": a series name made of ordinary words and numbers is part of what the item is called, a model code (SHP78CM5N) is not. A serial number is read as the item's serial, never its model number.

## Open questions
- A plan that starts after the maker's warranty ends (some do): read from the plan's own words when stated, otherwise the person is asked. The default when nothing is stated is the purchase date.

## Status (2026-10-19): not built; what happens today
A protection plan line is classed `service` by the multi-item reader (document-v9, S2.2.6) and left out, so a Best Buy receipt with "GEEK SQUAD PROTECTION 4YR $299.99" adds the TV and drops the plan silently. The same happens for every store, so no Best Buy rule is needed; Geek Squad is only the plan company (a party under Best Buy). The plan should become its own record on the item (this story, S3.4.3) rather than an item.

Sample receipts with plans (`Testing/SampleData/receipts/pdf`): Geek Squad 15, 16, 21, 26, 27, 31, 32, 56, 57 and `bestbuy-invoice-01`; Lowe's 33; Walmart/Allstate 30; PC Richard 28; Sears Outlet 35; Staples 60; Crutchfield 59; Circuit Barn 58; small stores 01, 29, 34. Thermal receipts print a plan number and term, often without a start date; email versions say "starts on delivery". **No Home Depot sample has a plan**: add one (a Home Depot protection plan on an appliance or tool) to cover that store. Plan companies need to be added as shared warranty providers (S3.2.9).

## Status (2026-11-02): built on branch feat-intake (API and UI), not merged, not measured with the real model
Built without a migration, so the planned `pending_item_plans` table is not made (see below). The reader (prompt document-v22) lists a plan as its own `plan` line in `products` (plan_name, company, price, term_months, end_date, contract_number, starts, covers, covers_line); plan lines are out of `items` and out of the "other items" count; an install, haul-away or delivery stays a service. Each plan is attached to the product it belongs to (covers_line, else the one its model, SKU or product word names, else the first); one that names something that is not the item, or names nothing on a receipt with several products, is kept at 70% with "check this is for the item above" and is not added on a quick add. Reader unsure of the list: 50%. The company is matched by name (exact, then normalised, a plan company first) or kept for the account as a "warranty provider" party (not verified, noted on the line). The plan is a proposed extended-plan coverage with company, term, price, plan number, covers and the company's claim contact when its record has one. The start is what the paper says (a printed date; on delivery uses the delivery date, flagged if estimated, and 75% (not quick-added) when the delivery date is not known; on purchase), and when the paper says nothing the purchase date is used and the line says so. A term the receipt does not state leaves the plan shown with no end date at 50%. The S2.4.4 check "item price plus plan price is not more than the total" is added. New Items shows "Includes: ..." with company and term (UI badge "plan"). Choices: (1) no new table: the plans (and the stated warranty of S2.4.8) are kept as JSON in the existing `pending_items.receipt_plan_line` column and become `plan:receipt` coverage rows in the item's existing policy list; (2) the plan is a proposed coverage only: creating the `WarrantyPlan`/`PlanItem` on accept and listing it under /plans is S3.4.3, not done; (3) a plan with several products and no pointer goes to the first product; (4) editing or removing before adding is the tick on Add Details (no per-field edit of a plan yet); (5) the plan's parent-company link (Geek Squad under Best Buy) and shared seeding are not done (held, per the story). Not done: the sample Geek Squad receipts as cases in the labelled set (S2.4.3) and a real-model accuracy measure (run the ReaderHarness on 15, 16, 21, 28, 30, 33, 56, 58, 81 to 93); a Home Depot sample with a plan exists now (81, 82, 83), so the "no Home Depot sample" gap is closed in the data.
