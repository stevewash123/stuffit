# S2.4.13 A refund of a protection plan cancels that plan

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v1  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S2.4.9 (return and refund documents); S5.4.20 (mark an item returned); S3.1.1 (add or edit a coverage); S3.4.2 (warranty plans)  
**Related:** S2.4.7 (protection plan on a receipt becomes a coverage)  

As a **user** who cancelled a protection plan and got the money back, I want the refund to end that plan on the item, so I am not reminded about, or counting on, coverage I no longer have, and the item itself is not marked returned.

## Why
A refund of a plan is the same event as cancelling the plan. Before this, a refund email for a plan alone (Amazon: "Refund issued for the protection plan on your order. Your TV stays with you.") was offered the TV as a return, and one tap would have marked the TV returned. The plan, which is what actually ended, was left running.

## Acceptance criteria
- A return or refund document whose only returned line is a protection plan or warranty (no product) is a **plan refund**. It is shown in New Items as "Plan refund", named for the plan, with the refund amount.
- It is offered the items of the original order (the same matching as any return), each with when its tracked plan was to end. Nothing changes until the person confirms; the button says "Cancel the plan", never "Mark it returned".
- Confirming ends every extended-plan coverage on that item on the refund date (an end date later than that is moved back; an earlier one is kept), notes "Cancelled <date>, refunded <amount>" in its terms, drops its reminders, and stops a warranty plan (S3.4.2) from covering that item. The refund document is kept with the item as proof and the New Item leaves the list. The item stays owned.
- When no plan was tracked on the item, the refund is still kept with the item and the person is told nothing else changed.
- Marking the item returned from a plan refund is refused. A return of the item and its plan together (a TV and its Geek Squad plan) is still a return of the item, and ends its coverage with it.
- The reader gives the plan line's price (negative on a refund); the plan's name is the plan line as printed.

## Not in this story
- Cancelling a plan without a refund document (a manual "Cancel this plan" on the item page).
- A partial refund of a plan (a prorated cancellation) is recorded the same way, with the amount refunded; no proration maths.
- Finding the plan's own order number when it differs from the item's.
- Marking the cancelled plan as "cancelled" in the coverage list rather than ended on that date.
