# S2.4.4 Check what was read against itself

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** M6 (proposed)  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1; S2.4.3 (to show it helped)  

As a **user**, I want a reading that does not add up to be flagged for me to check, so a wrong total or date does not slip into my items because the model said it was sure.

## Why
Today the only trust signal is the model's own "high, medium or low". The checks after reading only test each value's type. A failed arithmetic check is a better signal than the model's own score.

## Acceptance criteria
- After a reading, fields are checked against each other, and a failure lowers that field's confidence (or sets its issue) so it is shown flagged and never used unchecked:
  - Item price plus tax is not more than the total.
  - The purchase date is not in the future, and is not before the delivery date's order.
  - The quote the model gave contains the value it gave (in the form the document prints it).
  - Card last four is four digits.
- Fields that matter most (date, store, price, brand and model) are checked first and a failure on them always asks the person; a failure on a minor field does not.
- Deterministic and unit tested with plain readings, no model call.

## Not in this story
- Reading twice or voting across passes (F2.4 "Later").

## Status (2026-10-17): first version built
- `ReadingChecks.Apply` runs at the end of `DocumentReaderService.Tidy`. Critical fields (store, date, total, item price, brand, model name/number) get an issue; minor fields (tax, delivery date, card, order number) drop to Low confidence.
- Checks built: item price + tax ≤ total (one cent allowed), item price ≤ total, purchase date not after tomorrow, purchase not after delivery, card last four is four digits, and the quote holds the value (any number in the quote for money; identifiers must match once spacing is ignored; names and items are not held to the quote because the reader tidies them).
- 19 unit tests, no model. On the four photos (Sonnet, Haiku): no false flags, 37/37 and 36/37 as before. Whether it catches anything real is for the next batch of photos.
- Not built: the "quote contains the value" check for dates (printed forms vary too much), and any check on the UI side of what "low" does.
