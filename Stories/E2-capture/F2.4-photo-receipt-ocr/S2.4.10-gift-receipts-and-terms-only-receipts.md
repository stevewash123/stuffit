# S2.4.10 Gift receipts and receipts that only state return terms

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** future (only if it turns out cheap)  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1 (document reader); S2.4.2 (one intake)  
**Related:** S2.4.8 (a warranty the receipt states); S2.4.9 (return receipts)  

As a **user** who was given something, I want a gift receipt ("Gift receipt: exchange or store credit only within 60 days") to keep what it says, so I know the terms for taking it back.

## Why
A gift receipt prints no prices and says how long and in what form the store will take the item back: "EXCHANGE OR STORE CREDIT ONLY WITHIN 60 DAYS". Today it reads well enough (store, date, items, no prices; sample 11), but the terms are dropped because the item has no field that holds free text from the document, and a note field on the item would be a second place to keep what S2.4.8 already keeps for warranty statements.

## Thoughts on the return on effort
- The terms matter for a short time (60 days) and the value is a reminder, not coverage; a person who got a gift is likely to decide within a few weeks.
- Special code for one receipt type is a poor trade. A small, general mechanism is a better one: the **stated terms** of S2.4.8 (text as printed, a term, who stands behind it) extended with a second kind, "return terms", that feeds the item's return window as evidence (S2.7.2) when the store has no policy of ours. That would serve gift receipts and any receipt that prints its own return window ("15 day return window").
- So this story waits for S2.4.8 and is only built if that mechanism makes it nearly free.

## Acceptance criteria (draft)
- A receipt that prints return terms keeps them as stated text with the term and the form (exchange, store credit, refund) when printed.
- The terms are shown on the item as evidence for its return window, labelled as stated on the receipt, and never replace a store policy we know.
- A gift receipt with no prices is a normal receipt: it makes items with no price, and is never read as a return (S2.4.9).
