# S2.4.14 A warranty or plan document belongs to an item you already have

**Feature:** F2.4 Photo and document reading  
**Phase:** v1  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S2.4.1 (document reader); S2.4.2 (unified intake); S3.1.1 (add or edit a coverage); S3.4.2 (warranty plans)  
**Related:** S2.4.7 (a plan on a receipt becomes a coverage); S2.4.8 (a warranty the receipt only states); S2.4.3 (measure reading accuracy); S2.4.13 (a refunded plan cancels it)  

As a **user** who has the protection plan or warranty paperwork for something I own, I want to give it to the item it covers and have its terms offered as that item's coverage, so the plan's real term, end date and number are tracked, instead of the app guessing a standard 12-month warranty.

## Why
A warranty or plan document describes an item that already exists; it is not a purchase. Run through the receipt path (20 sample PDFs in `Testing/SampleData/warranties-from-receipts`, tested 2026-10-20), the reader got the item right (brand, model) but:
- made a purchase out of it: the plan price became the item's total and the plan number became its order reference;
- dropped the plan's own terms (a 4-year plan to 2030-09-27, a 7-year compressor warranty) and attached the default policy lookup instead (15-day return, 12-month manufacturer), which is wrong for every multi-year plan;
- gave no coverage at all to manufacturer warranties it should have read (11, 12, 14 to 17);
- made nothing for a Home Depot plan (04), saying there was nothing to track.

The reader already has a warranty-contract field set (company, plan name, contract number, start, end, term, price, covers, exclusions, claim steps). Nothing connects it to an item's coverage.

## Where the document comes from
The user should be giving it to an item, but will do it in any order.
1. **From New Items** (preferred): the item is waiting and the document is simply added to it: its terms join the item's proposed coverage with no question asked. The person reviews everything, this included, when they send the item to My Items.
2. **From My Items**: the document is added to an item already kept. Today that only stores the file (`POST /api/items/{id}/documents`); it is also read. Once it is read, a popup asks before anything changes (see below).
3. **Loose** (no item chosen, uploaded on its own, or arrived by email): it is read, matched to the item it most likely belongs to (plan or order number, model number, serial, store, date) and the person is asked to confirm. When nothing matches it waits in New Items as a document, not a purchase, until the person picks an item.

## Acceptance criteria
- A warranty or plan document is never turned into a purchase. Its price is the plan's price, never the item's total; its contract number is the plan's number, never the order reference.
- It is read for: who provides it (the maker, the store or a third party), plan name, contract number, start date, end date or term, price, what is covered and not, claim phone and website, cancellation terms, whether it transfers.
- When the reading says which item it is about (model, serial) and that differs from the chosen item, the person is told before anything is added. They can still add it.
- **On a waiting item** its coverage is added to the item's proposed coverage as it is read: kind (retailer plan or manufacturer warranty), start, end, term, price and the plan number, with the document kept as proof. No confirmation; the review at Add to My Items is the check. A date the document does not give is left blank (S2.4.7 rules for estimating apply), not invented.
- **On a kept item** the coverage is not added until the person confirms in a popup shown after the document has been read, naming the item and what will be added, in the wording the app uses elsewhere. For example: "This looks like the protection plan for your LG 65in OLED TV. Add it as an active warranty? 4 years, ends Sep 27, 2030." with Add and Not now. Add makes it active coverage with the document kept as proof; Not now keeps the document with the item and adds no coverage.
- A coverage that already exists for that plan or warranty is updated rather than duplicated (the same plan number, or the same kind on the same dates).
- When the document states a manufacturer term (S2.4.8) it replaces the standard policy for that item; the standard one is not shown as well.
- A document that is not a warranty or plan (a receipt, a manual) uploaded to an item is read for what it is and kept with the item as today.
- The same document read as a PDF or as a photo scan gives the same result; a scan that cannot be read well says so (S2.4.4) instead of offering guessed terms.

## Test runner (S2.4.3)
The test runner gets a second mode for documents:
- The key is `index.csv` in the folder (kind, product, model, serial, plan number, start or purchase date, end, term, price, confidence).
- **Every file needs a target item.** Two ways: the runner sets up the item itself by uploading the receipt the document came from (`source_receipt`, from `Testing/SampleData/receipts/pdf`) and accepting or leaving it in New Items; or the tester picks an item for one file from a picker listing the test user's New Items and then My Items, searchable by name, with the likely match (by model or serial) at the top.
- It uploads the document to that item the same way the app does and shows the coverage that was offered next to the expected row: provider, kind, start, end, term, price, plan number, in the runner's red and amber.
- It can run the same folder as PDFs and as photo scans (a folder of the same files as images, made next to it).
- A loose upload (no target) is one of the cases in the key: the match and the question the app asks are checked.

## Not in this story
- Reading the full terms text into structured claim rules; the plan's text is kept as notes.
- Warranty terms that arrive only by email without an attachment.
- Cancelling a plan (S2.4.13 and a manual cancel).
- Price-protection, rebates and card benefits.

## Open questions
- Loose documents that match two items (two TVs, one plan number): ask which.
- Where a document with no match waits: New Items, or a separate "Documents to place" list.

## Status
Reader and the two "known item" paths built 2026-10-20 (API only, uncommitted):
- Reader kind `item_warranty` (prompt `item-warranty-v2`): retailer plan, third-party plan or maker's warranty; company, plan name, plan number, model, serial, dates, length, price, parts with their own terms, covers, exclusions, claim steps, cancellation.
- Measured on the 20 sample PDFs (each added to the item from its source receipt): all 10 retailer plans match the key on plan number, dates, term and price; the maker's warranties match on provider and term, and a component with a longer term (compressor 5 years, rest 1) is its own part. Known gaps: file 10 (one plan for a washer and a dryer, two prices) reads as one plan with no price; file 16's 12-year compressor term needs registration, so the reader gives the 5 years that apply without it.
- `POST /api/pending-items/{id}/warranty-document`: added to the waiting item's coverage at once, reviewed when the item is added to My Items.
- `POST /api/items/{id}/warranty-document` reads and describes (nothing changes); `POST /api/items/{id}/warranty-document/{documentId}/apply` makes it active coverage. The same plan (by plan number, or same dates) updates instead of doubling. A maker's warranty replaces the one suggested from shared data.
- A document for another model or serial than the item comes back with a `mismatch` message.
- Unit tests and integration tests (reference project) cover the reader, both paths, the mismatch warning and the stored read.
- Added 2026-10-20: the "Add this warranty?" popup on the item page (and on New Items for a loose upload that matched a kept item); a loose upload is matched to one item in New Items or My Items by serial, model number and name (a printed purchase date only adds; a different model or serial rules an item out; a tie is no match), added at reduced confidence so a quick add leaves it a suggestion; with no match nothing is kept and the person is told to use Add Item or upload from the item's page.
- Test runner documents mode: `warranty-viewer.html` beside the receipt key viewer (same server and sign-in; `warranty-docs` and `receipt-pdfs` are folder links to the sample folders). Five ways to give a document: to the waiting item, to the kept item (read, then confirm), loose with its item waiting, loose with its item kept, loose with no item. An item can be picked from New Items or My Items, or made from the file's source receipt. Compares the plan, dates, price and kind with `index.csv`. "Clear New Items" dismisses and deletes the waiting items so each run starts clean.
- Not built: the photo-scan copy of the sample folder; browser checks of the popups.
