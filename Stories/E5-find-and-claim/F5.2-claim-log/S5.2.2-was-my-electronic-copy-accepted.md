# S5.2.2 Was my electronic copy accepted?

**Feature:** F5.2 Claim log
**Phase:** v3 (future; needs real users and a claim flow first)
**Depends on:** S5.2.1 (Claim friction rating); S5.4.8 (Say what did not work); S3.1.9 (What proof a warranty asks for)

As a **user**, I want to know whether other people's photos or emailed receipts were accepted for this brand or store, because most warranties just say "proof of purchase" and what is accepted in practice is not written down.

## Acceptance criteria
- When a claim or return is closed, one optional question: "What proof did they accept?" with: digital copy (photo, PDF or email), original paper receipt, card statement, nothing needed, or they refused it
- Stored with the brand or store and the kind of proof; never with the purchase, the person, or any document
- Shown on the coverage only once enough people have answered (a minimum count per brand or store, set in configuration, not a number in code), as a count and a wording that is never a promise: "12 people reported a digital copy worked, 1 reported it was refused"
- Under the minimum count nothing is shown, and the stated requirement from S3.1.9 is shown instead
- Shared knowledge, not personal data: aggregate counts only, in line with "your purchases are never shared"; the person can see and delete their own answers
- Reports are weighted down over time (policies change) and can be reviewed or removed by an administrator if abused
- Wording is honest about its limits: a few reports are not a rule, and the company decides

## Notes
- Soft data by nature. Build after the claim log has real use, so there is something to collect.
- The stated requirement (S3.1.9) comes first; this only helps where the document is silent.
