# F5.5 Returns that work for people with disabilities

**Epic:** E5 Find & Claim
**Phase:** future, tentative, speculative (idea from Steve; validate before building). The question story S5.5.1 comes first.

Returning something in person can be hard for a person who uses a mobility scooter or wheelchair, who has chronic pain or fatigue, or who cannot stand in a line: tight aisles, long waits, counters that are out of reach. Anything that avoids the trip is worth a lot. This feature asks what StuffIt can add for these users and collects the first answers: people's own return stories, an accessibility tag on them, and a "no trip needed" preference. Everything here is a sketch until real users say what helps.

## Principles
- **Start from lived experience.** The people who know are users, and their experience is data we can gather (S5.5.2) before we guess at features.
- **General information, not legal advice.** Where we mention rights (the ADA's reasonable-modification rule), it is general, sourced and dated, and never promises an outcome.
- **Opt-in, private by default.** What someone records about their own return stays private. Sharing is a separate, explicit act.
- **Do not make disability a required field or a label on the account.** Accessibility needs are chosen per return or per search, not stored as a diagnosis.
