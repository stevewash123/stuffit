# S5.4.3 Do I have what I need? (pre-flight checklist)

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S5.4.1, S2.1.2 (Attach receipts, manuals, photos)  

As a **user**, I want to know before I go to the store or open the claim form whether I have everything they will ask for, so I do not make a wasted trip.

## Acceptance criteria
- Checklist is built from the required items in the steps for the chosen route; if there are none, a generic list for that kind of route
- Each line shows what we already have: receipt attached, serial number saved, order or card statement match, photos of the defect; missing lines say how to get them (for example: find the order in your email, ask the card issuer for a statement)
- A line can be ticked by the user for things we cannot see (original packaging kept, accessories present); ticks are saved on the purchase
- One tap adds a photo or file to the purchase from the checklist (reuses S2.1.2 and the photo clean-up)
- When a line is missing and the deadline is near, say which route is still possible without it
