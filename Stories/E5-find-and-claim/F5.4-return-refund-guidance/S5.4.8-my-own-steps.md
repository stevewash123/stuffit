# S5.4.8 Add my own steps and say what did not work

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.1, S6.1.1 (Submission creates a private policy and a candidate)  

As a **user**, I want to correct or add steps from what actually happened, so the guidance gets better for me and for others.

## Acceptance criteria
- On any how-to: **That did not match what I was told**, with a note and optional photo of the policy sign or email
- A user can write their own step list for a seller or plan; it is private to them and shown above ours with a label
- With the user's consent, a correction or new step list goes to the candidate queue (S6.1.1) for verification before it becomes shared guidance
- Reports are counted per policy version; several disagreeing reports flag the steps for review (S6.2.3)
- Rate-limited and moderated like other contributions (S6.7.5 when credits exist)
