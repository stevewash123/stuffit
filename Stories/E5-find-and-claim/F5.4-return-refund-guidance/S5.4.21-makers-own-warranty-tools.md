# S5.4.21 Point to the maker's own warranty and service tools

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Depends on:** S5.4.2 (claim steps are reference data); S3.2.5 (verified from the company's own page); S5.4.14 (wizard result); S3.4.8 (service requests)  
**Found by:** Steve, 2026-10-28, while collecting ASUS warranty pages: ASUS has a chat assistant that matches your product to the right warranty.

As a **user** about to ask for a repair or return, I want to be told when the maker has its own tool for finding the warranty that applies to my product or starting a service request, so I use the quickest official route and not the generic one.

## Why this matters
Makers differ in what their own pages offer. ASUS has a warranty-status lookup by serial number (and in its MyASUS app), an online RMA, and a chat assistant that matches a product to its warranty. Those are often the fastest and most accurate answer to "what am I covered for", and they know the product's real terms in a way a shared policy record may not. Mentioning them costs nothing and does not require WarrantyCat to be right about the terms.

## Acceptance criteria
- A maker (manufacturer party) can carry **service tools**: a short list, each with a kind (warranty status lookup by serial number, online repair or return request, chat or assistant that finds the warranty, registration, support phone), its address, a one-line "what it does", and the page it was seen on. Versioned and sourced like other reference data (S5.4.2); an operator confirms each from the maker's own page.
- In the repair or return guidance (S5.4.14, S9.2.6) the maker's tools are shown **before** the generic steps, in the person's words: "ASUS has a tool that checks your warranty from the serial number" with a button that opens it. Shown only for a tool that is confirmed.
- WarrantyCat **opens the tool and stops there.** It never fills in a serial number or a form on the person's behalf and never reads the tool's answer back as fact. The person's own warranty record is unchanged unless they edit it.
- A tool's answer is not authoritative for WarrantyCat's reminders: if the person learns a different end date there, the guidance offers "Use this date" for them to confirm (as for any value they enter).
- The AI page pass (tools/page-saver, `analyze_pages.py`) lists such tools it sees ("registration_or_claim" pages and assistant mentions) for an operator to review, with the sentence it relied on.
- A maker with no confirmed tools shows nothing extra.

## Open questions
- How much of a maker's assistant is worth describing. Assistants change; keep only "there is one, here is the page", never what it says.
- Whether the support phone and warranty-status page belong to this story or to the existing `claim phone` and `claim link` on the party (S3.4.4). They probably merge: the party's claim link becomes the first tool.
