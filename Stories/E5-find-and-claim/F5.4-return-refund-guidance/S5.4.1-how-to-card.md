# S5.4.1 A how-to for each way out

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.1.2 (Ranked coverage detail), S5.4.2 (Claim steps on policies)  

As a **user** with something I want to return or claim on, I want plain steps for the option I picked, so I do not have to hunt through the store's website.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each coverage on the Something-broke view gets a **How to** section with ordered steps for that route (return, store warranty claim, manufacturer claim, extended plan, card protection)
- Steps come from the claim-steps data for that seller, manufacturer, plan or card as it was on the purchase date (S5.4.2), not from generic text
- Shows the route (in store, online, by mail, by phone), what to bring or send, the condition the item must be in, fees (restocking, return shipping), and what the refund looks like (original payment, store credit, replacement)
- Shows the deadline from the coverage and what counts as the deciding date (purchase, delivery, or receipt of the item)
- Official link and phone number open straight from the section
- When we have no steps for that party, say so and show the generic checklist (S5.4.3) and the official link if known; never invent steps
- Reachable from the item page, a reminder (S4.5.4) and the notification link
