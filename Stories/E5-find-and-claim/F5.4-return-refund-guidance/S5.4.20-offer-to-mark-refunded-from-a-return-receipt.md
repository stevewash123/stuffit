# S5.4.20 Offer to mark an item refunded when a return receipt arrives

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S2.4.9 (read a return or refund receipt); S5.4.11 (record money recovered); S2.2.5 (pending items)  
**Related:** S2.4.7 and S3.4.2 (a plan refunded with its item); S2.2.4 (de-duplicate)  

As a **user** who returned something and was sent a return receipt or a refund email, I want StuffIt to find the item I already track and offer to mark it refunded, so my list stays true and I do not have to find the item and type the amount myself.

## Why
A return receipt is the best evidence there is that an item went back and what was refunded, and the item is usually one we already track. Today the receipt would sit in New Items as something it is not (S2.4.9 fixes what it is) and the person would still have to mark the item refunded by hand (S5.4.11). The rule of S5.4.11 stays: **nothing is marked refunded on its own.** This story only offers, with the evidence, and the person confirms.

## The model (one rule)
- A return document is matched to **tracked items** (My Items first, then New Items). A match is a **suggestion with its reason**, never an action.
- Confirming creates the same recovery record S5.4.11 describes (kind store refund, amount, date, method, note), and moves the item to Returned.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Matching, strongest first:** the original transaction or order number printed on the return equals the purchase's order number or receipt number; then the same store with the same model number or a close item name and a price that agrees with the returned line, within a window after the purchase date; then a name-only match, shown as weak. Several equally good candidates are listed for the person to choose from; none is picked for them.
- **The offer** appears where the person already is: on the item page and on the return's row in New Items. It reads like "This looks like a return of your Ryobi 40V Leaf Blower: $188.84 refunded to Visa 1212 on 5/20. Mark it refunded?" with the reason for the match (original transaction number matches).
- **Confirm fills the form of S5.4.11** from the receipt: the amount (the returned line plus its tax, or the refund total when it is the whole return), the date, the method (original payment, store credit, gift card, other), and a note naming the return receipt. All can be edited before saving. Decline leaves everything as it was and remembers the decline for that receipt.
- **Partial returns:** one return of two items refunds one of them; the item not returned is untouched. A refund that is less than the price (a restocking fee) is recorded as the amount actually refunded.
- **A plan returned with its item** (S2.4.7, S3.4.2): the offer names it ("and its Geek Squad 4-year plan, $299.99") and, on confirm, ends the plan with the refund recorded against it. A plan refunded alone (cancelled within its window) is offered the same way against the plan.
- **An exchange** ends the returned item the same way; the new item arrives with its own receipt and is not created from the slip.
- **Not tracked:** a return for something we do not track is kept as a record of the return, with no item, and nothing is offered. If the purchase is tracked later (the receipt arrives after the return), the offer is made then.
- **The receipt stays attached** to the item under the retention rules (S2.6.1), as proof of the refund.
- **Audit:** who confirmed, when, and from which return document; an undo follows S5.4.11.
- **Tests:** matching by original number, by store and model and price, and by name only; several candidates; a partial return; a plan refunded with its item; a return with no tracked item; declining and later matching; no recovery ever created without a confirm.

## Not in this story
- Reading the return document (S2.4.9).
- The wizard and guidance on how to return something (S5.4.1 to S5.4.17).
- Chasing a refund that has not arrived.

## Status
Partly built 2026-10-20.

**Built:** a return waiting in New Items is matched to the items in My Items that are still owned: by the original order number (strongest), then the same store with the same model or a close name and an agreeing price, then a close name alone (weak); the offer lists the candidates with the reason and the refund it would record, and nothing is marked until the person confirms. Confirming marks the item returned (status Returned, with the date, the amount refunded and the method taken from the document), keeps the return document on the item as proof, and takes the return out of New Items. Repeating the confirmation is safe. Checked live against the sample Best Buy return (matched the TV by its transaction number) and marked and reverted.

**Not built:** matching to items still waiting in New Items; ending a protection plan returned with its item; recording the money recovered (S5.4.11) beyond the amount, date and method on the return record; remembering a decline for that receipt (dismissing sets it aside); audit and undo beyond the pending item's own event; a second return of the same item.
