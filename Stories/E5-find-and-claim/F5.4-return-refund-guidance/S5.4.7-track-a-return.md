# S5.4.7 Track the return or claim

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.1, S5.4.11 (Record money recovered), F5.2 (Claim log)  

As a **user**, I want to see where my return or claim stands and be nudged to follow up, so a refund is not forgotten.

## Acceptance criteria
- Start a return or claim from the how-to; record route, date started, and the reference or tracking number
- Steps the user can mark: started, shipped or dropped off (tracking number and receipt photo), received by the seller, refund issued, refund received
- When the policy states a refund time (for example 5 to 10 business days) show the expected date and send a reminder if it passes without a refund recorded
- Follow-up nudge offers the prepared message (S5.4.4) and the contact from the policy
- Outcomes feed the claim log (F5.2) and, when the user agrees, the crowd data on how routes actually went (S6.2.1)
- The item shows its state (return in progress, refunded) so reminders for it stop

## The commit point (Steve, 2026-10-03)
"Start a return" is the guided "how do I return this" wizard (S5.4.12). Looking at the steps is not yet a return. At some point in the wizard the user affirms they are acting: **I am doing this** (filing the form online, printing the label, or going to the store). That one action is what puts the item on the returns-in-progress list (S9.2.8), with the route they chose.
- Until then the wizard is research and leaves no record on the item.
- What we keep at the commit: route (online form, mail with label, in store, curbside, phone), date committed, and optionally the reference, RMA or tracking number.
- Stages after that, each one tap: *committed*, *handed over* (shipped or dropped off, with tracking number or receipt photo), *received by the seller*, *refund issued*, *refund received* (this is today's Mark returned form: amount, method, how hard, problems).
- Two derived states matter most for the user: **to do** (committed, not handed over: the shopping list, "going to Walmart today") and **waiting on money** (handed over, refund not received: nudged when the policy's refund time passes).
- Until the wizard exists, the item page offers a plain **Start a return** that records only the commit (no route) so the list works.
