# S5.4.4 A ready-to-send message or phone script

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.1, S5.4.3  

As a **user**, I want the details of my claim written up for me, so contacting the company takes a minute.

## Acceptance criteria
- Builds a short draft from the purchase and coverage: item, model, serial, purchase date, price, order number, what went wrong (user's own words), and what I am asking for (refund, repair, replacement)
- Three forms: email text, web-form field text, and a short phone script with the details in the order they will be asked
- Copy to clipboard, or open the user's own mail app with the draft; **the user always sends it** (StuffIt never contacts a company for the user, S7.2.1 wording)
- The draft cites the policy the claim rests on and its date, in neutral language; no threats and no legal claims
- Nothing is sent to an AI service unless the user opts in, and then only the fields in the draft (S7.6.5 cost and data rules)
