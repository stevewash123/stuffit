# S5.4.15 Wizard hand-off: save the attempt, record the outcome

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.14; S5.4.11 (Record money recovered); S5.4.4 (A ready-to-send message or phone script); S5.4.7 (Track the return or claim)  

As a **user**, I want to carry on from the advice without starting over.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each run of the wizard is saved on the item as an attempt: reason, answers, the result at the time, when
- From the result: "Start this return" saves the attempt as a tracked claim (S5.4.7); "Write the message" opens the prepared text or call script (S5.4.4) filled with order number, model, serial and dates; "I got my money back" opens S5.4.11
- A user can leave and resume; the item page shows "Return in progress" with the next step
- Re-running later (new facts, a new problem) creates a new attempt and keeps the old one
- The attempt is private personal data: included in export and account deletion (S1.4.1, S1.4.2)
- Never sends anything to a company on the user's behalf
