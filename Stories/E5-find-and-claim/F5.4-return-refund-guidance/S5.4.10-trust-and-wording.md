# S5.4.10 Honest labelling of guidance

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.1, S7.2.1 (Terms, privacy, disclaimer)  

As a **user**, I want to know how current and how reliable the guidance is, so I do not rely on something stale.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every how-to shows: source link, date verified, and whether it is verified, unverified or user-supplied (reuses S6.1.5)
- A how-to whose source has not been verified within the stale threshold (set per policy kind) carries a **Check the store's page first** notice
- Standing wording: WarrantyCat reports what the company has published; the company decides; terms can change; this is not legal advice. Wording reviewed with the legal pages (S7.2.1)
- A report that the page changed (S5.4.8, S6.3.1) marks the steps for re-check immediately
- Guidance never states a consumer-law right (for example statutory refunds) unless it is in the sourced data
