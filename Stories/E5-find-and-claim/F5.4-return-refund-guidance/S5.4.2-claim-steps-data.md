# S5.4.2 Claim steps are versioned reference data

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables), S7.1.1 (Edit reference data with versioning)  

As a **developer**, I want claim steps stored beside the policy they belong to, so guidance is versioned, sourced and editable like every other policy fact.

## Acceptance criteria
- Seller policy, manufacturer policy and card benefit versions carry structured claim steps: route, ordered steps, required items (receipt, original packaging, serial number, ID, card statement), conditions, fees, refund method, contact, and a form or portal URL
- Same effective_from/to, source_url and verified_at rules as the rest of the policy (S3.2.1); lookup by purchase date
- A step list is data, not a blob of prose: each step has a short instruction and an optional link, so the UI can number, check off and translate them
- Admins edit through the existing versioned editor (S7.1.1); every change is audited
- Seed the most common sellers and the cards from S3.2.4 first; the long tail fills in through S5.4.7 and the curation queue (F6.1)
- Private and shared policies behave as today: a user's own steps for their own policy stay private (S5.4.8)
