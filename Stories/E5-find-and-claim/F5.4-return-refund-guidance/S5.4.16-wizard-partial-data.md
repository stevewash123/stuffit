# S5.4.16 The wizard with partial information

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** v2  
**Milestone:** unassigned  
**Depends on:** S5.4.14; S2.7.1; S3.1.6  

As a **user**, I want useful advice even when I am not sure of the purchase date or which seller it was.

## Acceptance criteria
- An approximate purchase date gives a range; the result uses the cautious end and says "depends on the exact date" where the answer would change
- When the date decides between routes, the wizard asks for it once ("About when did you buy it?") and offers the recovery steps of S2.7.9
- Unknown seller or store: skip the return route, show maker and card routes, and say the return window could not be checked
- Items with no purchase record (S2.7.3) show only routes that do not need one
- Never invents a date, a seller or a policy to produce a confident answer
