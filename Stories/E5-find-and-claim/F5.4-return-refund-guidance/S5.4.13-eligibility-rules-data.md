# S5.4.13 When a coverage applies (eligibility rules as data)

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S5.4.2; S3.2.1 (Party and versioned policy tables)  

As a **developer**, I want each coverage to say what it does and does not cover, so the wizard can decide without guessing.

## Acceptance criteria
- Each policy version (seller, manufacturer, card benefit, paid plan) carries structured eligibility, stored with the claim steps of S5.4.2:
-   - reasons it covers (change of mind, defect, damage in transit, not as described, price drop, accidental damage, theft, loss)
-   - conditions on the item (new or unopened, complete with accessories, original packaging, not used or modified, serial number readable)
-   - proof needed (receipt, order number, card statement, serial number, photos, repair estimate, the manufacturer's denial)
-   - how the clock runs (from purchase, from delivery, from installation, after another coverage ends) and the window length
-   - dependencies ("needs the maker warranty first", "claim within N days of the failure")
-   - fees and limits (restocking fee, a deductible, per-claim and yearly caps, excluded categories)
- Each rule has a plain-language wording that the result page can show, and the source it came from (S5.4.10)
- Unknown stays unknown: a rule we have no data for evaluates to "can't tell", never "applies"
- Versioned like every policy fact: lookup by purchase date; operators edit in the existing editor (S7.1.1); changes audited
- Seed for the largest sellers, the common makers and the cards from S3.2.4 first
