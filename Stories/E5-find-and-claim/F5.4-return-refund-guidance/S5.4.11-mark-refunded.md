# S5.4.11 Record money recovered (refund, card payout)

**Feature:** F5.4 Return and refund guidance  
**Phase:** MVP candidate  
**Milestone:** QA  
**Depends on:** S9.2.3 (Item detail page); the item status Returned already exists  

As a **user**, I want to record that I got my money back, so WarrantyCat stops reminding me about the item and I can see what I have recovered.

## Acceptance criteria
- **Mark refunded** (and **Add a card payout**) on the item page, the Something-broke view and the items list; works without ever using the how-to or tracking (S5.4.7)
- Asks for: amount refunded (defaults to the item's price, editable), date received (defaults to today), and how it came back (original payment, store credit, gift card, check, other); a note is optional
- Partial refunds are allowed (restocking fee, damaged-box deduction); the item's price is kept and the recovered amount is what the user typed
- Each recovery is its own record on the item (kind, amount, date, method, note, who and when), so one item can have a store refund and a card payout, or several refunds (the item, then the shipping); one purchase can have refunds on some items only
- Recoveries are separate from item status: only a **store refund** moves the item to Returned; a card payout or warranty payout leaves the status alone (the user can say the item is gone, which sets Disposed)
- Recording a store refund moves the item to **Returned** (existing status) and cancels its pending reminders; nothing is deleted, and the receipt and files stay under the retention rules (S2.6.1)
- Kinds: **store refund**, **card reimbursement** (a card benefit paid out, S3.3.2), **warranty payout**; replacement or repair is not money and is only noted
- Store credit and gift cards are saved as recovered value but labelled as credit, not cash (S9.2.5 shows them apart)
- Undo: a recovery can be edited or removed (a refund that bounced, or a mistake) and the change is kept in the audit trail; if the last store refund is removed the item returns to Owned only if the user says so, and its reminders are rebuilt from the coverage dates (S4.1.1)
- Counts for the item's people (S1.3.2)
- The amount and date show on the item page; recoveries appear in the data export (S1.4.1) as their own CSV
- Data: a `recovery` table, one row per recovery (item, kind, amount, received_on, method, note, created_by, created_at). Never inferred or auto-marked from an email or statement; only the user's confirmation counts
